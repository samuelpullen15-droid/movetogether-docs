MoveTogether reads your steps, calories, and workouts from **Apple Health**. If your device doesn't show up when MoveTogether asks "Which one is your tracker?", it usually means your wearable isn't sending its data to Apple Health yet.

Turn that on in the wearable's own app using the steps below, then reopen the tracker picker in MoveTogether and choose it.

## Apple Watch and iPhone

Nothing to set up. Apple Watch and iPhone write to Apple Health automatically — you only need to grant MoveTogether permission to read Apple Health when it asks.

## Turn on Apple Health sharing, by device

### Oura

1. Open the Oura app.
2. Tap the menu (three lines) in the top left, then **Settings**.
3. Scroll to **Data Sharing** and tap **Apple Health**.
4. Make sure **Connect to Health** is enabled.

### WHOOP

1. Open the WHOOP app.
2. Go to **More → App Settings → Integrations → Apple Health**.
3. Turn on all categories — make sure **Steps** is ON (it's off by default).
4. Tap **Allow**.

### Garmin

1. Open the Garmin Connect app.
2. Tap **More → Settings → Connected Apps → Apple Health**.
3. Enable the health categories, then tap **Allow**.

### Strava

1. Open the Strava app.
2. Go to the **You** tab → **Settings → Manage Apps & Devices → Health**.
3. Tap **Connect**, then turn on **Send to Health**.

### Polar

1. Open the Polar Flow app.
2. Go to **More → General settings → Apple Health**.
3. Turn all categories on, then tap **Allow**.

### Ultrahuman

1. Open the Ultrahuman app.
2. Tap your profile, then **Apple Health**.
3. Turn on all categories, then tap **Allow**.

## Fitbit and Google Health

Fitbit connects through **Google Health**, not Apple Health. In MoveTogether, choose **Google Health / Fitbit** and sign in to the account that holds your activity — you don't need the Apple Health steps above.

## Confirm it's reading

After you've turned sharing on:

1. Open the Apple **Health** app.
2. Tap **Browse**, choose the metric (for example **Steps**), and scroll to **Data Sources & Access**.
3. Confirm your wearable is listed there as a source.
4. Back in MoveTogether, reopen the tracker picker — your device should now appear to select.

<Note>
Not every wearable writes every metric to Apple Health. A device can share workouts but not continuous steps, for example — so a source may appear for one metric and not another.
</Note>

## Still not showing up?

If your data is in Apple Health but MoveTogether still doesn't list your device, [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) and let us know which wearable you use — that points to something on our side.
