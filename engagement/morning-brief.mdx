title: "Use Morning Brief"
description: "Schedule the daily Coach Mo brief, open it from Home, and check missing widgets or notifications."
---

Morning Brief is Coach Mo's daily recap and tip. Depending on the data available, it can include weather, sleep or recovery, yesterday's rings, today's competitions, streak context, and a tip.

## Set it up

1. Open Coach Mo (on iPhone, tap the red **+** button, then **Ask Coach Mo**), open Coach Mo settings, and tap **Morning Brief**.
2. Turn on **Daily Morning Brief**.
3. Choose a **Greeting time** between **5:00 AM and 10:00 AM**.
4. Tap **Save**.
5. If you want an alert, check MoveTogether's notification access and [notification preferences](/account/notifications).

The schedule is a preference for the brief; system notification settings separately control whether an alert can reach you.

## Open the brief

Open **Home** and tap the Morning Brief card, or tap a Morning Brief notification. Read the date and available widgets so you know which day the recap describes.

Morning Brief is different from the [Weekly Mo Report](/engagement/weekly-report). The brief helps you start today; the weekly report reviews the previous completed week.

## Why a widget may be missing

Weather needs location access. Sleep and recovery need a device that records those metrics and shares them into Apple Health or Health Connect. Rings and competition context need synced activity and relevant participation.

A missing widget does not mean its value is zero. Check the source and permissions in [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources). You can use the parts of the brief that have data without expecting every device to supply every widget.

## The brief or alert has not arrived

First reopen the Morning Brief settings and confirm the enabled state and saved time. Then check Home: if the brief is there but no alert arrived, troubleshoot [notifications](/troubleshooting/notification-problems). If the brief itself is missing, refresh after activity syncs and try again when connectivity is restored.

If the issue continues, tell [Marco](/troubleshooting/contact-support) your selected greeting time, date, and whether the Home card or only the notification was missing.
