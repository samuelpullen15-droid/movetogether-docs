---
title: "Get Coach Mo's Morning Brief"
description: "Turn on the daily Pro Morning Brief, pick your greeting time on iPhone or Android, read it from Home before noon, and fix a missing brief or alert."
---

Morning Brief is a short daily recap from Coach Mo. It covers your weather, last night's sleep, yesterday's rings, today's competitions, and a tip. It's ready each morning at the time you pick.

<Note>
  Morning Brief is a Pro feature. It isn't made during a Pro free trial, or on Free or Member. You can still switch it on in settings, but no brief arrives until your Pro plan is active.
</Note>

## Turn it on and pick a time

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open **Coach Mo**.
    2. Tap the menu button at the top to open your conversations.
    3. Tap **Settings** at the bottom of the list.
    4. Under **Customization**, tap **Morning Brief**.
    5. Turn on **Daily Morning Brief**.
    6. Under **Greeting time**, pick a time.
    7. Save your choice.
       - **In version 2.2.0:** tap the checkmark (**Save**) at the top right.
       - **In version 2.1.3:** tap **Save**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open **Coach Mo**.
    2. Tap the menu button at the top to open your conversations.
    3. Tap **Settings**.
    4. Under **Customization**, tap **Morning Brief**.
    5. Turn on **Daily Morning Brief**.
    6. Under **Greeting time**, pick a time.
    7. Tap **Save**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The **Morning Brief** row shows your time, such as **7:30 AM**, or **Off**. It's on by default at 7:30 AM.

| Greeting times you can pick |
| --- |
| 5:00, 5:30, 6:00, 6:30, 7:00, 7:30, 8:00, 8:30, 9:00, or 10:00 AM |

Your brief is made within about 15 minutes of that time, in your home time zone.

## Read your brief

On **Home**, the Morning Brief tile sits next to the **Weekly recap** tile. It shows today's date and one of these:

| The tile says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Tap to read** | Today's brief is ready. A dot means you haven't opened it yet. |
| **Waiting on today** | Today's brief isn't ready yet. |
| **Tomorrow at sunrise** | It's after noon. Today's brief has closed. |

Tap the tile, or the **Your morning brief is ready.** notification, to open it. Each brief is for the morning only. At 12:00 noon in your home time zone, the tile dims and stops opening.

## What's in a brief

Each part only shows when there's data for it, in this order:

1. **Weather**, with a short headline such as **Walking weather**.
2. **Last night's sleep**, with stages, if a wearable tracks your sleep.
3. **Recovery**, if your wearable provides it.
4. **Yesterday's rings**, such as **All three closed.**
5. **Today**, with your streak, your active competitions (tap one to open it), and a note from Mo.
6. **A tip** for the day.

Weather uses your iPhone's location, and the app asks for permission the first time. The Android app doesn't share your location, so briefs on Android usually skip weather.

A missing part doesn't mean the value is zero. It means MoveTogether didn't have that data.

## The alert

The alert comes from the **Coach Mo** notifications. To turn it off, go to **Settings** > **Notifications** > **Coach Mo** > **Push notifications**. With it off, your brief still appears on Home.

MoveTogether limits how many alerts it sends in a day. On a busy day, the brief can arrive on Home without an alert.

Morning Brief is different from the [Weekly Mo Report](/engagement/weekly-report). The brief starts your day. The weekly report looks back on last week.

## Something not right?

- **The tile always says Waiting on today.** Check that you're on Pro, not a free trial. Then check that **Daily Morning Brief** is on and saved.
- **The brief is on Home but no alert came.** Check **Coach Mo** notifications and your phone's notification settings. See [Notification problems](/troubleshooting/notification-problems).
- **It's after noon.** Today's brief has closed. Tomorrow's arrives at your greeting time.
- **Sleep or weather is missing.** Sleep needs a wearable that saves sleep to Apple Health or Health Connect. See [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).

If the brief still doesn't arrive, tell the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) your greeting time, the date, and whether the Home tile or only the alert was missing.
