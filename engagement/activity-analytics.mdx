Your daily activity screen shows each day's rings for free on every plan. Pro adds trend charts for your activity, sleep, weight, and BMI (body mass index) over weeks, months, and years. These charts describe your fitness data. They don't diagnose anything.

## Open your activity

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open **Home**.
    2. Tap the **Today's Activity** heading, or tap your rings card.

    The screen is called **Activity**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open **Home**.
    2. Under **Today's Activity**, tap your rings card.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Today's Activity** on **Profile** is different. Tapping a ring there changes that ring's goal. See [Set your goals](/getting-started/setting-goals).

## What every plan sees

- A strip of the last 7 days. Tap a day to see it.
- Your Move, Exercise, and Steps rings for that day.
- **On iPhone:** your workouts for the day, from Apple Health.
- **On Android:** steps, distance, workouts, heart rate, and sleep for the day, when your tracker records them.

## Pro trend charts

If you're not on Pro, the charts area shows an upgrade card instead (**Activity analytics is a Pro feature** on iPhone, **Trends are a Pro feature** on Android). Member doesn't include the charts.

| Chart | iPhone | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Yes | Yes |
| Exercise | Yes | No |
| Steps | Yes | Yes |
| Sleep and sleep stages | Yes | Yes |
| Weight | Yes | Yes |
| BMI | Yes | Yes |

### Pick a time period

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    Each chart has its own period buttons: **D** (day), **W** (week), **M** (month), **6M** (six months), and **Y** (year). Changing one chart doesn't change the others.

    Tap or drag on a chart to see the exact value and date. Tap anywhere else to clear it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    One set of buttons changes every chart at once: **W** (week), **M** (month), **6M** (six months), and **Y** (year).

    Each chart shows its highest value as **Peak**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Longer periods group your days. Six months shows weekly bars, and a year shows monthly bars. A bar in those views is not a single day.

**Example:** On iPhone, the Activity chart on **W** shows a header like **5 of 7 days**, which is how many days you met that goal. Switch to **6M** and each bar is a whole week.

## Sleep

Sleep only counts when a wearable records it. Sleep your phone estimates on its own, and sleep you type in by hand, never count.

- **iPhone:** sleep counts from an Apple Watch, or from the Oura, WHOOP, Garmin Connect, Polar Flow, Fitbit, or Ultrahuman app writing to Apple Health.
- **Android:** sleep counts when a watch, ring, or band saves it to Health Connect.

Some wearables send only your total sleep, with no stages. Then the chart shows **Your wearable sends total sleep only, so there are no stages to show for this range.**

An empty sleep chart doesn't mean you slept zero hours. Check the period you picked, your wearable's own app, and the sleep permission. See [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).

## Log your weight

You log weight on the Weight chart, which is part of the Pro charts.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. On the **Weight** chart, tap **+**.
    2. In **Log today's weight**, choose **LBS** or **KG**.
    3. Turn the dial to your weight.
    4. Tap **Save**.

    Weigh-ins from Apple Health also appear on the chart. The Weight chart shows pounds.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. On the **Weight** card, type your weight in **Weight (lbs)**.
    2. Tap **Log weight**.

    Weigh-ins from the last 30 days in Health Connect also appear. If you log a weight in the app and Health Connect has one for the same day, the app's entry wins.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Logging a weight records today's value. It doesn't change past readings in Apple Health or Health Connect.

## Height and BMI

BMI needs your height and at least one weigh-in.

1. On the BMI card, tap **Set your height to see BMI**.
2. Enter your height in **Your height**.
3. Tap **Save**.

Your height is shared between your iPhone and Android apps. On Android, tap **Edit height** on the BMI card to change it later.

The BMI card shows your current BMI and a label: **Underweight** (below 18.5), **Normal** (18.5 to 24.9), **Overweight** (25 to 29.9), or **Obese** (30 and above). Treat it as general information, not a diagnosis.

## Something not right?

| What you see | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| A whole period is empty | Pick dates when you know you were active, and check your tracker has synced. |
| One metric is missing | Make sure your device records it and shares it to Apple Health or Health Connect. |
| Two devices disagree | Check [which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source). |
| Exercise differs from your workout's length | Read [how workouts count](/troubleshooting/sync-issues#my-workouts-sync-but-my-exercise-ring-looks-different). |

For a spreadsheet you can open outside the app, use [Export an activity report](/engagement/export-activity-report). It's free on every plan.
