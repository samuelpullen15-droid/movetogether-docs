---
title: "Complete weekly challenges"
description: "Open the full challenge board, interpret weekly progress, and understand resets and how completions contribute to the monthly collection."
---

Weekly challenges accumulate progress across the week. They are separate from today's challenges, your workout streak, and the weekly Move League race.

## Open the weekly list

1. On **Home**, find the **Challenges** card.
2. Tap the **Challenges** heading to open the full board.
3. Scroll to **All week**.
4. Read each challenge's target and progress before choosing what to work toward.

Home previews only part of the weekly list. Open the full board when you want to see every challenge rather than assuming the preview is the complete set.

## Read progress

Each row shows the challenge and how far you have progressed toward its target. The target tells you whether you are accumulating an activity total or satisfying another requirement. A completion indicator means that challenge's requirement has been met; it does not mean all challenges for the week are finished.

**Example:** if a challenge asks for an activity total across the week, activity from several days can contribute. You do not need to reach that entire total in one day unless the challenge explicitly says so.

## Weekly reset

The weekly window resets at **Monday 00:00 UTC**. This is a single worldwide instant, so it can be Sunday evening in the Americas. Daily challenges instead reset at your local midnight.

Check the current challenge and its period when a value seems to have reset. A new week's challenge is a new target, not lost progress in the previous one.

## Monthly collection

Completed daily and weekly challenges contribute to the [monthly collection](/engagement/monthly-challenges). A weekly challenge completed after a month boundary counts in the month of completion, not necessarily the month in which the week started.

Read any reward or bonus shown for an individual challenge rather than assuming every completion adds the same points to every competition. The monthly completion count and competition scores measure different things.

## A challenge is not updating

Wait for your tracker to finish syncing, then pull to refresh the board. Check that the challenge measures the activity you recorded and that you are looking at its current period. Use [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources) for missing readings or [contact Marco](/troubleshooting/contact-support) if synced activity still does not count.
