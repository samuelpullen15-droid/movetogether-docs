title: "Complete weekly challenges"
description: "Find this week's challenges under All week, see how progress builds from Monday to Sunday, and know when they reset and what they count toward."
---

Weekly challenges are five goals that build up over a week, like walking 50,000 steps or closing 14 rings. Everyone in MoveTogether gets the same five each week. They're free on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

They're separate from the challenges you pick under **Choose a challenge**, your workout streak, and your Move League.

## Find this week's challenges

1. Open **Home** and tap the **Challenges** heading.
2. Scroll to **All week** (**Builds up Monday to Sunday**), at the bottom of the screen.
3. Each row shows the challenge, your progress, and a bar. A checkmark means that challenge is done.

## What a week can ask for

Each week, five of these are chosen, each at an easy, medium, or hard target:

| Challenge | Targets |
| --- | --- |
| Walk steps this week | 35,000, 50,000, or 70,000 |
| Burn active calories this week | 1,500, 2,500, or 4,000 |
| Complete workouts this week | 3, 5, or 7 |
| Close rings this week | 7, 14, or 21 |
| Log activity before 8 AM | on 2, 4, or 7 days |
| Join competitions | 1, 2, or 3 or more |

**Close rings** counts each ring you close on each day, against your own goals. Closing all three rings on 5 days is 15 rings.

## When progress updates

Steps, calories, workouts, and rings count **finished days only**. Today's activity shows up tomorrow.

**Example:** The challenge is **Walk 50,000 steps this week**. You walk 8,000 steps a day Monday to Thursday. On Friday the row shows 32,000, because Friday isn't finished yet. You need 18,000 more over Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.

## Weekly reset

The week resets at **Monday 00:00 UTC**. That's one moment worldwide, so it can be Sunday afternoon or evening in the Americas. A new week brings five new challenges. Last week's progress doesn't carry over.

The swap allowance for the challenges you pick resets at your own midnight instead.

<Note>
  In time zones behind UTC, such as the Americas, the week resets on Sunday your time. Your Sunday activity then counts toward the next week's challenges.
</Note>

## What weekly challenges count toward

Weekly challenges don't earn points. They don't count toward the [monthly challenge badge](/engagement/monthly-challenges) or your rank. Only the challenges you pick under **Choose a challenge** do. See [Pick and complete challenges](/engagement/daily-challenges).

## Something not right?

- **Today's activity is missing.** Steps, calories, workouts, and rings count from tomorrow.
- **Progress reset.** Check whether it's past Monday 00:00 UTC. A new week means new challenges.
- **Nothing updates.** Pull to refresh the Challenges screen after your tracker syncs. Check [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources) for missing readings.

If synced activity still doesn't count, message us in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
