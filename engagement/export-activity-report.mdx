An activity report is a **CSV spreadsheet** of your daily activity. It opens in Numbers, Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app. It's free on every plan, and works the same way on iPhone and Android.

## Create your report

1. Open **Profile** and tap the gear icon to open **Settings**.
2. Under **Data**, tap **Export a report**.
3. Under **Dates**, tap **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last month**, or **Last 90 days**. Or set your own **From** and **To** dates.
4. Under **What to include**, turn on the metrics you want. Move, Exercise, and Steps start switched on.
5. Tap **Create my report**.
6. When you see **Your report is ready**, tap **Save or share the file** and choose where it goes.

If you change the dates, the list under **What to include** updates. Check your choices again before you create another report.

## Pick your dates

| Choice | Dates it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** | That many days, ending yesterday |
| **Last month** | The whole previous calendar month |
| **From** and **To** | Any range up to 366 days, and it can include today |

The report starts on the last 30 days. Today's numbers can still change as you move and sync, which is why the presets end yesterday.

## What to include

Only metrics with a reading above zero in your dates are listed, so the list changes with your dates. A missing metric means there's no recorded value in those dates, not that your device can't measure it.

The metrics you can choose are:

- Move calories, Exercise minutes, Steps, Workouts
- Distance (metres), Stand hours
- Sleep, Deep sleep, REM sleep, Light sleep, and Awake (all in minutes)
- Average, Max, and Min heart rate (bpm)

## What's in the file

- **At the top:** your name, the period, how many days are in it, how many days have data, and when you made the report.
- **A summary table:** each metric's total, its average per day with data, and how many days have data. Heart rate has an average only, because adding heart rates together doesn't mean anything.
- **One row per day**, with a column for each metric you chose.

Days your tracker missed are left blank, not zero. Averages only count days with data.

**Example:** You export the last 7 days, and your tracker missed Tuesday. **Your report is ready** says 6 of 7 days have data. The Tuesday row is blank, and your Steps average is your 6 days' total divided by 6.

## Choose the right export

| What you need | Use |
| --- | --- |
| A spreadsheet of your daily activity | **Export a report** |
| A copy of all your account data | [Download my data](/account/data-and-privacy#downloading-your-data), sent by email |
| Coach Mo's take on your week | [Weekly Mo Report](/engagement/weekly-report), in the app |

## Something not right?

- **You see No activity was recorded in these dates.** Try a wider range that includes days you know synced.
- **A metric is missing.** It had no reading in those dates. Check it in [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).
- **You see You've made a few reports in a row.** You can make 10 reports an hour. Wait an hour and try again.
- **Creating the report failed.** Check your connection and tap **Create my report** again.

A saved file doesn't update. If more activity syncs later, create a new report.
