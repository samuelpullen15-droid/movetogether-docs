title: "Pick and complete challenges"
description: "Pick challenges from five offers, hold up to three, swap or remove up to three a day, bank points, see your rank in your group, and find your completed challenges."
---

Challenges are personal targets you pick yourself, such as a step count, active calories, exercise minutes, or workouts. Each one is worth points. MoveTogether tracks them from your synced activity, so you never mark one done yourself. Challenges are free on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

## Open your challenges

1. Open **Home** and scroll to **Challenges**.
2. Tap the **Challenges** heading, or **View your challenges**.

The Home card shows this month's badge, your points this month (such as **21 POINTS**), the challenge you're closest to finishing, and your rank. If you aren't holding a challenge, it says **Pick a challenge**.

## Pick a challenge

Under **Choose a challenge** you get five offers. Each shows its points (for example **+21**) and when it counts:

| The offer says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **One day. Starts now.** | Reach the target in a single day. Only activity after you pick it counts, so what you did earlier today doesn't. |
| **On 5 different days. Counts from tomorrow.** | Reach the target on 5 days, in any order, starting tomorrow. |
| **7 days in a row. Counts from tomorrow.** | Reach the target 7 days running, starting tomorrow. |

Tap **Pick** to take one. It moves to **Active**, and a fresh offer fills its spot.

- You can hold up to **3** challenges at once. The **Active** heading shows how many, such as **2 of 3 held**.
- You can pick up to **4** new challenges a day. After that you'll see **That's enough new challenges for today.**
- Offers match what you've been logging. A metric shows up once you've logged it on at least 3 of the last 21 days. If nothing qualifies yet, you get step challenges.
- Offers stay for the whole month until you pick or swap them. They don't change every day.

**Example:** You've already walked 4,000 steps today when you pick **Walk 13,000 steps** (**One day. Starts now.**, **+21**). You need 13,000 more steps today, or 13,000 on a later day this month.

## Swap or remove

Don't like an offer? Tap **Swap** to replace it. To drop a challenge you're holding, tap **Remove** and confirm. A removed challenge earns no points.

- Swaps and removals share one allowance: **3 a day**. Under the offers you'll see, for example, **2 swaps left today**, or **No swaps left today. More tomorrow.**
- The allowance resets at midnight in your profile's time zone.
- If a challenge you remove had actually reached its target, it banks its points instead.

## Points

Harder challenges are worth more. The points are locked in when you pick a challenge, so they never change after that.

When synced activity reaches the target, the challenge banks its points and leaves **Active**. That frees a spot for another pick.

A held challenge has no deadline within the month. Anything still unfinished when your month ends earns nothing, and a new month starts with fresh offers.

<Warning>
  A **7 days in a row** challenge picked in the last week of the month can't finish before the month ends. Check how many days are left first.
</Warning>

## Read your progress

Each held challenge has a progress bar and one of these lines:

- **Best day: 6,200 of 9,000** for a one-day target. This is your best day so far.
- **2 of 5 days. Counts from Oct 5.** for a multi-day target.

For a **7 days in a row** challenge, the day count includes every day you hit the target. It still needs 7 in a row to finish.

## Your points and rank

At the top of the screen, **Your points** shows your **points this month**, your rank (such as **#4 of 533**), and how many days are left in the month. Tap **View the live leaderboard** to see everyone in your group.

- You're ranked against your group, not everyone in the app. The rank line shows how many people are in it.
- Ties go to the person whose latest finished challenge came on an earlier date. If that's tied too, the person who finished fewer challenges ranks higher.
- Other people show by first name and last initial, or by username.

To hide yourself from the leaderboard, go to **Settings** > **Privacy Settings** and turn off **Show on public leaderboards**. Your name is then hidden from everyone, including you. Your points, progress, and rank in **Your points** still count.

## Completed challenges

**Completed this month** lists your three most recent finished challenges, each with its points. Tap **View all completed** to open **Completed Challenges**, a list of every challenge you've finished, newest first, grouped by month.

Every finished challenge also counts toward [your monthly challenge badge](/engagement/monthly-challenges).

## Something not right?

- **Progress looks low.** A **Starts now** challenge ignores what you did before you picked it. A **Counts from tomorrow** challenge ignores the day you picked it.
- **A metric looks wrong.** Compare the target with the same metric and date in your activity.
- **Nothing is updating.** Pull to refresh after your tracker syncs, and check [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources) if a reading is missing.

If a challenge stays unfinished even though your synced activity meets the target, message us in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) with the challenge name, date, and metric.
