Daily challenges give you specific targets for today, such as steps, calories, Exercise minutes, or ring progress. MoveTogether tracks them from synced activity; you do not need to mark a fitness target complete yourself.

## Find today's challenges

1. Open **Home** and find **Challenges**.
2. Review today's targets and progress in the card.
3. Tap the **Challenges** heading to open the full board.
4. Look under **Today** for the complete daily list.

The Home card also previews weekly progress. The full board separates **Today** from **All week**, so a weekly target should not be mistaken for something you need to finish tonight.

## How targets fit your activity

Template targets can be calibrated to your recent activity. With enough history, MoveTogether compares the available targets with your previous 14 local days and chooses a spread of achievable, at-pace, and stretch goals. It selects from available template targets rather than promising an exact percentage increase.

With fewer than three days of history, there is not enough evidence for that calibration, so starter template difficulty is used. Keep syncing as you establish your routine. This template calibration is distinct from Pro's AI-generated challenge features.

Challenges also depend on your metrics. If your tracker doesn't provide steps, step challenges can be left out.

**Example:** two people can receive different step targets because their recent activity differs. A target being lower than a friend's is not an error, and a new account's target may become more relevant as history builds.

## Completion and reset

Daily challenges reset at your local midnight. Read the target on the current challenge; completing yesterday's target does not complete a different challenge today.

When synced activity satisfies the target, progress is recorded as complete. A completion can also contribute to your [monthly challenge collection](/engagement/monthly-challenges). That monthly count is separate from a competition's score or daily bonus rules.

## If progress looks wrong

Compare the target with the same metric and date in your activity. Refresh the board after your tracker syncs. Check [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources) if a required reading is absent.

If the board shows no challenges or an error, use its retry action or pull to refresh. If a target stays incomplete despite matching synced activity, contact [Marco](/troubleshooting/contact-support) with the challenge name, date, and relevant metric.
