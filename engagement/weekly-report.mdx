The **Weekly Mo Report** looks back at your last full week, Monday to Sunday. It's ready on Monday morning. Every plan gets the report with your charts and stats. Pro adds Coach Mo's grade and write-up.

## What's in it on each plan

| Part of the report | Free and Member | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move, Exercise, and Steps charts for each day, with your goals | Yes | Yes |
| Stats: rings closed, active days, workouts, week streak, competitions | Yes | Yes |
| Badges you unlocked that week | Yes | Yes |
| Mo's letter grade, story, encouragement, and roast | No | Yes |

Mo's grade and write-up aren't included during a Pro free trial.

The grade comes from how close you got to your Move, Exercise, and Steps goals over the week. It runs from **A+** down to **F**.

## Open your report

1. Open **Home**.
2. Tap the **Weekly recap** tile. It shows the week's dates and, on Pro, your grade. You can also tap the **Your Weekly Mo Report is ready** notification.
3. Scroll to read your week.

The screen is called **Weekly recap** on iPhone and **Your week** on Android.

**On iPhone,** the tile opens last week's report from Monday to Friday. On Saturday and Sunday it reads **Coming Monday** until the next one is ready. **On Android,** the tile opens last week's report any day until the new one arrives.

## When it arrives

Reports are made on Monday at about 9 to 10 AM in your home time zone. Your coaching style and language shape how Mo writes it.

Monday's report looks backward. Activity you record on that Monday belongs to the new week.

**Example:** To check your report against your tracker, pick the same Monday through Sunday. A "last 7 days" view that includes today will give a different total.

## Share your week

At the bottom of the report, tap **Share with friends** to open **Pick your vibe**. Swipe to choose a card, then tap **Share** or **Save to Photos** (**Save to gallery** on Android). See [Share your wins](/engagement/sharing-your-wins).

## The notification

On Pro, the **Your Weekly Mo Report is ready** alert follows the **Coach Mo** switch in **Settings** > **Notifications**. The separate **Weekly recap** switch on that screen doesn't control it. On Free and Member, the **Coach Mo** switch isn't shown, so the only way to stop this alert is your phone's notification settings for MoveTogether.

## The Sunday recap push and email

The Sunday recap is separate from the report, and every plan gets it. On Sunday at 7 PM in your time zone, MoveTogether sends a short summary of your week so far, Monday through that Sunday. It comes two ways:

- **A push notification** with your rings closed and your highlight of the week, such as a competition win, a new badge, or your week streak. It's in your app language.
- **An email** titled **Your Week in Review** with your rings closed and, if you have one, your current week streak. The email is in English.

To stop both, turn off **Weekly recap** (**Push and email**) in **Settings** > **Notifications**. To stop only the email, tap the unsubscribe link at the bottom of it. That link also stops our other update emails.

Monday's Weekly Mo Report covers the same Monday-to-Sunday week, with your charts and, on Pro, Mo's grade.

## Why a report may be missing

- **It's not Monday morning yet.** The tile reads **Coming Monday** until the new report is ready.
- **Last week didn't have enough data.** A report needs at least 4 days of synced activity in the week, including Sunday. Without that, there's no report for that week.
- **It's your first week.** You'll see **No recap yet** until a full Monday-to-Sunday week has finished.
- **Nothing was recorded.** The report reads **A quiet week**.
- **There's no grade.** The grade and Mo's write-up are for Pro, and aren't included during a free trial.
- **The report is there, but no alert came.** Check the **Coach Mo** notification switch and [notification settings](/troubleshooting/notification-problems).

Refresh Home and check your connection. If a report is still missing, tell the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) which week you expected.

For today's briefing, use [Morning Brief](/engagement/morning-brief). For daily numbers in a spreadsheet, use [Export an activity report](/engagement/export-activity-report).
