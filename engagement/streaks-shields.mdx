Your workout streak rewards showing up. MoveTogether keeps two streak counts. Your **day streak** shows on Home, and your **week streak** earns streak badges. Streaks and streak freezes work on every plan. Earning streak badges needs Pro.

## What makes a day count

A day counts toward your streak when you have either:

- **30 or more Exercise minutes**, or
- **at least one recorded workout**, of any length.

Closing your Move or Steps ring doesn't count by itself. The 30 minutes is a fixed number, not your Exercise ring goal. Days use your local time zone.

## Your two streaks

| | Day streak | Week streak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Where you see it** | The **Workout Streak** card on Home and Profile | Streak badges, and the **Week Streak** home-screen widget |
| **What it counts** | Days in a row that count | Weeks in a row with at least 3 days that count |
| **Week runs** | Not used | Monday to Sunday |
| **Freezes protect it** | Yes | No |

**Example:** You work out Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday. Your week counts toward your week streak, because it has 3 counting days. Your day streak breaks on Tuesday, unless a freeze covers it.

## Read your streak

1. Open **Home** and find the **Workout Streak** card.
2. The big number is your day streak.
3. The strip shows Monday to Sunday. A flame with a check is a day that counted. A gray X is a missed day. A shield is a day a freeze covered. Today shows a small flame until it counts.
4. Tap the number or the strip to open **Streak History**.

A blank today doesn't break your streak. You have until the end of your local day.

## Streak freezes

A streak freeze covers one missed day so your day streak keeps going. The **Workout Streak** card shows how many you have, such as **2 streak freezes**. Tap the info icon next to it for a reminder.

- **You earn one** every time your current streak reaches another 7 counting days in a row.
- **You can hold up to 3.**
- **They don't refill weekly.** You only get more by earning them.
- **They're used for you.** When your activity syncs after a gap, each missed day uses one freeze.
- **It's all or nothing.** If a gap needs more freezes than you have, none are used and your day streak starts over.
- **A covered day doesn't add to your count.** It keeps the streak alive, but your number stays the same.
- **Late syncs give freezes back.** If a workout for a covered day syncs later, that day counts and the freeze returns (up to the limit of 3).
- **Freezes don't protect your week streak.** A week needs 3 real counting days.

**Example:** You have a 15-day streak and 2 freezes. You miss Thursday and Friday, then work out Saturday. Both freezes are used, and your streak continues at 16 on Saturday. If you'd had only 1 freeze, none would be used and your streak would restart at 1.

## Streak History

**Streak History** shows a calendar of your months. Swipe between them.

- The top shows your current **day streak**, your **Best** streak, and **Workouts** (the total number of days that counted).
- Days that counted, missed days, and days a freeze covered each have their own mark.
- Today shows as pending until it counts. It never shows as broken while the day is still going.

<Note>
  The **Shields** number at the top of Streak History isn't your freeze count. Your freeze count is on the **Workout Streak** card on Home.
</Note>

## Streak badges

Streak badges count your **week streak**. Each one asks you to stay active 3 days a week for a number of weeks in a row: **5 Week Streak**, **10 Week Streak**, **25 Week Streak**, then every 25 weeks up to **300 Week Streak**.

- Earning streak badges needs Pro.
- Open a streak badge from **Profile** > **Badges** > **Streaks** to see **Your progress**, such as **7 / 10 weeks**.
- When you reach one, a celebration screen shows the badge. Tap **Share this** to send it, or **Keep going** to close it.

See [How badges work](/engagement/badges).

Streaks don't add bonus points in competitions. Your competition's [scoring method](/competitions/scoring-reference) decides its points.

## Something not right?

- **Your streak reset.** Check that the missed days had 30 Exercise minutes or a workout. Then check whether you had enough freezes for the whole gap.
- **A workout is missing.** Make sure it appears for the right date in your activity, then pull to refresh after your tracker syncs. See [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).
- **Your badge progress doesn't match your day streak.** Badges count weeks, not days.

If your history still looks wrong, send the dates and the workout involved through the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
