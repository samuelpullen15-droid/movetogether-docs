title: "Join seasonal events and earn badges"
description: "Find seasonal events in Compete, distinguish challenge, competition, and community formats, and check badge qualification."
---

Seasonal events are limited-time activities with their own dates, goals, and badge requirements. They appear in **Compete** when available. Seasonal badges can be earned on any plan.

## Join an event

1. Open **Compete** and find the seasonal event.
2. Tap it to read its dates, format, and requirements.
3. Check that your tracker records the metric it needs.
4. Use the event's join action when enrollment is available.
5. Return to the event details as your activity syncs to follow progress.

Do not assume that opening an event or joining it earns its badge. Read the qualification requirement before starting.

## Understand the format

| Format | What you work toward | How to read progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Challenge | Your own event goal | Compare your personal progress with the requirement; there is no ranking. |
| Competition | A leaderboard result | Read both your score and any active-day or other badge-qualification requirement. |
| Community | A shared target | The progress reflects contributions toward the community goal; there is no individual ranking. |

**Example:** being high on a competition leaderboard may not be enough for a badge if the event also requires activity on a minimum number of days. Conversely, a personal challenge can award its badge for reaching a target without beating another person.

## Event badges

The event details explain the reward and qualification rule. Some competition events have placement variants for qualified finishers. Check the event's own rule instead of applying the same ranking requirement to every event.

After your qualifying activity syncs and the reward is processed, check your [badges](/competitions/achievements) on Profile. The [monthly challenge collectible](/engagement/monthly-challenges) is a separate feature; participation in a seasonal event is not automatically a monthly challenge completion.

## Missing progress or reward

Confirm the event dates, enrollment, required metric, and qualification rule. Then check [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources) and refresh the event. If you still appear to qualify without a reward, send [Marco](/troubleshooting/contact-support) the event name and affected dates.

Manage event alerts in **Settings → Notifications**. Turning off an alert does not replace checking the event deadline yourself.
