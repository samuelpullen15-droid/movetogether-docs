Seasonal events are limited-time events with their own dates, goal, and badge, such as a Halloween step race or a Movember exercise goal. Anyone can join, and seasonal event badges are free to earn on every plan (Free, Member, and Pro).

## Find an event

1. Tap the **Compete** tab.
2. Look under **Limited-time events**. A month-long event shows above it as the **Monthly challenge**.
3. To see everything, swipe to the end of the row and tap the **View all events** card (it shows the count, such as **View all 12 events**). The **All Events** screen lists events under **ACTIVE**, **UPCOMING**, **Results pending**, and **COMPLETED**.
4. Tap an event to open its details. You'll see its dates, format, daily bar, and how the badge is earned.

Each card shows a small label: **LIVE** while it runs, a countdown such as **3 DAYS** or **SOON** before it starts, and **WRAPPED** when it's over.

## The Home card for your next event

When an event is running, or starts within 7 days, and you haven't joined it, a card for it can appear on **Home**.

- Before the event, the card's top line counts down, such as **Starts in 3 days · Want in?**
- Once it's running, it reads **Live now · Not too late to join**.
- Tap **Join** to join right there. The card changes to **You're in**.
- Tap the card to open the event's details first.
- Tap **✕** to hide the card for that event.

If several events fit, the card shows the one that starts first. It doesn't appear for an event you can no longer finish.

## Join an event

1. Open the event.
2. Tap **Join Event**.
3. If the event has paces, a sheet suggests a pace for you first. Choose one and tap **Join at Easy**, **Join at Steady**, or **Join at Strong**. See [Choose your pace in a seasonal event](/engagement/event-paces).

You can join an upcoming event before it starts. You can also join one that's already running.

## The three formats

Every event card shows its format.

| Format | What you do | Who earns the badge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Challenge** | Hit the daily bar on a set number of days. No leaderboard. | Everyone who reaches the number of days. |
| **Competition** | Score points on a leaderboard. | The top finishers (for example, the top 3). People tied at the last badge place all earn it. |
| **Community** | Add your activity to one shared total. | If the group reaches the goal, everyone who added any activity. If the goal is missed, nobody. |

Some month-long Competitions also ask you to log a set number of active days to qualify. Only qualifiers can place, and the top qualifiers earn placement badges. The event details spell this out.

**Example:** Movember Movement (November 1 to 30, 2026) is a Community event. Everyone's exercise minutes add up toward 20,000 minutes. If the community gets there, everyone who logged any exercise minutes during the event earns the badge.

**Example:** Gentle Weekend (October 23 to 25, 2026) was a Challenge with no paces. A day counted at 4,000 steps or more, and any 2 of the 3 days earned the Gentle Stepper badge.

## Which days count for you

| Your plan | Days that count |
| --- | --- |
| Free or Member | From the day you join the event. |
| Pro | Every event day, back to the event's start (but never before you created your account). |

Days before you created your MoveTogether account never count, on any plan.

On iPhone, if the days left can't add up to the badge, the event shows **Badges out of reach** and explains why. You can still join, score points, and appear on the leaderboard.

## Event badges

Seasonal event badges are free for everyone. Streak, achievement, and competition badges need Pro to earn. See [How badges work](/engagement/badges).

When you earn an event badge, it appears in **Profile** > **Badges** under the seasonal grid at the top. Placement badges in Competitions arrive after the event ends and final scores are in.

The [monthly challenge badge](/engagement/monthly-challenges) is a separate badge. Joining a seasonal event doesn't count as finishing a challenge.

## Event notifications

| Notification | How to turn it off |
| --- | --- |
| Morning and evening event reminders | The bell on the event screen, or **Settings** > **Notifications** > **Competition Lifecycle** > **Lifecycle alerts** |
| Community milestones (25%, 50%, 75% of the goal) | The bell on the event screen |
| An event has ended, or Mo moved your pace up | **Settings** > **Notifications** > **Competition Updates** > **Push notifications** |
| You earned an event reward | **Settings** > **Notifications** > **Promotions & Events** > **Push notifications** |

To open **Settings**, go to **Profile** and tap the gear icon. See [Notification settings](/account/notifications).

## Something not right?

- **Progress looks low.** Check the event's daily bar and your pace, then compare it with the same day's activity. Pull to refresh after your tracker syncs.
- **Days before you joined don't count.** On Free and Member, only days from your join date count.
- **A badge is missing.** Placement badges arrive after the event ends. Community badges need the shared goal to be reached.
- **A reading is missing.** Check [Data Sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).

If you still think you qualify, send the event name and dates through the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
