---
title: "Share your wins"
description: "Share your weekly recap card, a badge, a streak milestone, or a competition recap as an image from MoveTogether on iPhone or Android."
---

You can share a picture of your week, a badge you earned, a streak milestone, or how a competition went. Sharing works on every plan, on iPhone and Android. Each share opens your phone's share sheet, so you can send it to any app.

If you have an invite link, the message also includes a line inviting friends to join you, such as **Come move with me:** and your link.

## What you can share

| What | Where to find **Share** |
| --- | --- |
| Your week | **Weekly recap** tile on **Home** > **Share with friends** |
| A badge you earned | **Profile** > **Badges** > tap the badge > **Share** |
| A badge you just earned (version 2.2.0) | The **New badge earned** screen > **Share** |
| A streak milestone | The celebration screen when you reach one > **Share this** |
| A finished competition | The competition > **More options** (•••) > **View recap** > last slide > **Share** |

## Share your week

1. On **Home**, tap the **Weekly recap** tile.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Share with friends**. **Pick your vibe** opens.
3. Swipe between the cards to preview each one.
4. Tap **Share** to pick an app, or **Save to Photos** (**Save to gallery** on Android) to keep the image.

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Grade | **MO'S WEEK GRADE** with your letter grade and headline |
| Mo's Take | **MO'S TAKE**, a line from Mo's write-up, sometimes a roast |
| Rings | **THE RINGS**, how many of your 21 rings you closed |

Mo's grade and write-up are Pro features. On other plans, the Grade card shows a dash instead of a letter, and the Mo's Take card doesn't appear.

The Grade card's photo follows your grade. It's a sunrise coast for A, a mountain trail for B, a city at dawn for C, morning fog for D, and stadium lights for F.

## Share a badge

1. Open **Profile**, then tap **See all** next to **Badges**.
2. Tap a badge you've earned. Locked badges can't be shared.
3. Tap **Share** and pick an app.

The image shows the badge art, its name, and the date you earned it. See [How badges work](/engagement/badges).

## Share a streak milestone

When you reach a streak badge, such as **10 Week Streak**, a celebration screen appears. Tap **Share this** to send the badge with a line such as "10 weeks straight on MoveTogether." Streak badges need Pro. See [Workout streaks](/engagement/streaks-shields).

## Share a competition recap

1. Open a finished competition.
2. Tap **More options** (•••), then **View recap**.
3. Swipe to the last slide and tap **Share**.
4. Preview the card (your total points, your place, and your best day), then tap **Share**.

## Before you share

The image includes your results and can include your name or username. Once you send it outside MoveTogether, the people and apps you send it to keep their copy. Changing a setting later doesn't take back an image you've already shared.

Shared images don't update. If more activity syncs later, share a new one.

## Something not right?

- **The share sheet doesn't open.** Close the card and try again once the image has loaded.
- **The other app fails.** Try a different app that accepts images.
- **The Mo's Take card is missing.** It needs Mo's write-up, which is a Pro feature.

For a spreadsheet instead of a picture, use [Export an activity report](/engagement/export-activity-report). For a missing weekly report, see [Weekly Mo Report](/engagement/weekly-report#why-a-report-may-be-missing).
