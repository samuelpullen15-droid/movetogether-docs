MoveTogether Wrapped is a short story about your 2026 on MoveTogether. You tap through it in the app. Every slide but one is built from your own activity, workouts, league weeks, and competitions. The other shows everyone's totals together. It's free on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

## When it arrives

MoveTogether Wrapped 2026 opens in the app in **early January 2027**. We wait until the year is over so every day of 2026 counts, December 31 included.

Once it's out, your numbers can still change until **January 12, 2027** if a late sync comes in. From January 12 your Wrapped stays exactly as it is. See [If your numbers change](#if-your-numbers-change).

## Who gets one

You get a MoveTogether Wrapped if you were active on at least **7 days in 2026**. An active day is any day with steps, Move calories, or Exercise minutes above zero, counted in your own local days.

| Active days in 2026 | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| 30 or more | The full story: up to 10 slides, including your Mover type |
| 7 to 29 | A shorter story about your start: your first slide, your totals, your biggest day, your league (if you had a scoring week), the community, and your summary |
| Fewer than 7 | No Wrapped this year. Nothing shows in the app |

If you're under 18, you get the same Wrapped without the slide that compares you with other members. That slide also needs your age or birthday on file.

## Where to find it

Once MoveTogether Wrapped is in the app:

- **Home.** A banner reads **Your 2026 is ready** (or **Your start is ready** for the shorter story). Tap **Watch it**. The banner shows until the end of January.
- **Your profile.** Tap your photo at the top right of **Home**. Under **Your year**, tap **MoveTogether Wrapped 2026**. It stays there after January, so you can watch it again.
- **A notification.** If your **Promotions & Events** notifications are on, we send one notification in the week it arrives, saying your Wrapped is ready. It has no numbers in it. See [Notifications](/account/notifications).

The story plays on its own. Tap the right side to go forward, tap the left side to go back, and hold to pause.

## What it's made from

Your Wrapped uses data MoveTogether already has:

- Your daily steps, Move calories, and Exercise minutes
- The days you closed each ring
- Your workouts
- Your league weeks and competition places
- Your personal records, week streaks, badges, and completed challenges
- How you used Coach Mo: how many messages you sent, on which days, and at what times of day

It **never** uses sleep, heart rate, or weight. For Coach Mo, it uses counts, days, and times only, never what you said.

We work out every number on our servers after the year ends, so you see the same Wrapped on iPhone and Android.

## How we count each slide

You only see the slides your year has enough data for, in the order below, and at most 10. The summary is always last. If more than 10 slides fit your year, we leave out the community slide first, then milestones, then where you stand, then your rhythm. Your Mover type always stays.

A few rules apply everywhere:

- **Days** are your own local days. Workouts, Coach Mo messages, badges, challenges, and personal records go on the day they happened in the time zone on your account, or US Eastern time if there isn't one.
- **Daily limits.** A day over 70,000 steps, 5,000 Move calories, or 600 Exercise minutes counts at that limit, and it can't be your biggest day. One bad sync can't take over your year.
- **Rings** count on the days MoveTogether recorded you closing them, against that day's goal. Changing a goal later doesn't change past days.

### Your 2026 with MoveTogether

When you joined and how many days you moved in 2026. The calendar has one dot per day from your first active day to December 31. Yellow dots are days you closed all three rings.

### Your totals

Your total steps for the year, with your Move calories and Exercise minutes. A line such as **That's 7,621 a day on the days you moved** is your steps divided by your active days, so rest days don't pull it down. The bars show each week (Monday to Sunday), and your biggest week is the dark one.

### Your biggest day

The day with your most steps. If you have no step counts, it's the day with your most Move calories, then your most Exercise minutes. Below it you'll see anything else that happened that day: all three rings closed, a new personal record, or a competition win.

### Your day of the week

Shown with 30 or more active days and at least 4 active days on every weekday. It's the weekday with your highest average steps on the days you moved (or Move calories, then Exercise minutes, if you have no step counts), and the ring you closed most often.

### Your favorite workout

Shown with 30 or more active days and at least 5 workouts. Before counting:

- A workout recorded by two sources at once counts once.
- Workouts shorter than 5 minutes or longer than 4 hours don't count.

Your favorite is the type you logged most. If walking is more than half of your workouts and another type has 3 or more, you see your favorite after walking. The line about how often you work out is your workouts per week, from your first active day to December 31.

### Your Mover type

Shown with 30 or more active days. See [How your Mover type is decided](#how-your-mover-type-is-decided) below.

### Your highest league

Shown if you had at least one league week with a final score above zero. It shows the highest league you reached, where you started, and how many promotions you had. **Best weekly finish** only appears for a week when you finished in the top half of your league group.

### Competitions you finished

Shown with 30 or more active days and at least one competition round with two or more people that ended in 2026. Rounds you did on your own don't count.

- A **podium finish** is a win, or second or third in a round with 4 or more people.
- We only compare places, never points across rounds, because competition scoring changed during 2026.
- You may also see one story: your **biggest comeback** (a round where you were third or lower partway through and finished at least two places higher, in the top three) or, if you had none, your **closest finish** (a round decided by 5% or less).

### You moved most with

Shown with 30 or more active days. This is someone on your friends list who was in at least one 2026 competition with you. If there's no one like that, the slide reads **Your most-met rival** and shows a rival you went up against at least twice.

A friend's name shows as first name and last initial, such as **Maya R.** A rival who isn't your friend shows by username, unless they turned on **Show real name on leaderboards**. We skip anyone who deleted their account or who blocked you or was blocked by you. If no one is left, you don't get this slide.

The chart shows the days you both moved during a competition you shared. It only appears if their privacy settings already let you see their daily numbers in that competition. You never see their other days.

This slide stays in the app. You can't share it.

### You and Coach Mo

Shown with 30 or more active days and at least 3 messages to Coach Mo. It shows how many messages you sent, on how many days, your first chat of the year, and the hour you usually talked. It never shows or uses what you said.

### Your milestones

Shown with 30 or more active days and at least one of these:

| Milestone | Shown at |
| --- | --- |
| Personal records | 3 or more |
| Longest week streak | 3 weeks or more in a row. A week counts when it has 3 or more days with 30 or more Exercise minutes or a workout |
| Badges earned in 2026 | 1 or more |
| Challenges completed | 5 or more |

### Where you stand

Shown with 30 or more active days, if you're 18 or over and your age or birthday is on file. It compares your average steps on the days you moved with everyone who has a MoveTogether Wrapped.

This slide only appears if you're in the top half. We round down to 50, 60, 70, 75, 80, 85, 90, or 95 percent, and 50 percent shows as **Half**.

### Together, MoveTogether walked

Everyone's totals added up: steps, workouts, and days of moving across every member with a MoveTogether Wrapped. In the grid, each dot is one member, or a group of members when there are many, in the order they joined. Yours is lit. Its place is when you joined, not a rank.

### Your summary

Your steps, days moved, biggest day, highest league, and Mover type on one card. It's the last slide and doesn't play on by itself.

## How your Mover type is decided

Your Mover type comes from three habits in your year. We use your main number: steps, or Move calories, or Exercise minutes, whichever you have on the most days.

| Habit | How we decide |
| --- | --- |
| **Steady weeks** or **Up-and-down weeks** | How much your weekly totals (Monday to Sunday) changed from week to week, compared with the middle of everyone with 30 or more active days. Above the middle is Up-and-down. Weeks after you stopped moving don't count |
| **Weekdays** or **Weekends** | How much of your year's total came on Saturdays and Sundays, compared with the middle of everyone with 30 or more active days. Above the middle is Weekends |
| **Your own pace** or **In competitions** | In competitions when you were in a competition with at least one other person on at least a third of your active days. Leagues don't count. Seasonal events do |

Exactly at the middle counts as Steady weeks or Weekdays. The three habits give one of eight types:

| Mover type | Habits | What it says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Clockwork | Steady weeks, Weekdays, Your own pace | Your weeks look alike and run on weekdays. Nobody has to remind you. |
| The Pacesetter | Steady weeks, Weekdays, In competitions | Your weeks barely changed, weekdays did the work, and there was usually a competition on. |
| The Wanderer | Steady weeks, Weekends, Your own pace | Quiet weekdays, reliable weekends, and a route you pick yourself. |
| The Regular | Steady weeks, Weekends, In competitions | Every weekend, without fail, and usually with company. |
| The Spark | Up-and-down weeks, Weekdays, Your own pace | Big weeks that come out of nowhere, mostly on weekdays, mostly on your own. |
| The Contender | Up-and-down weeks, Weekdays, In competitions | Put a competition on the calendar and your week changes gear. |
| The Adventurer | Up-and-down weeks, Weekends, Your own pace | Long quiet stretches, then a weekend that makes up for all of it. |
| The Main Event | Up-and-down weeks, Weekends, In competitions | Your biggest weeks come with a crowd and a Saturday. |

Mover type names are in English in every language.

## If your numbers change

Until January 12, 2027, we recount your Wrapped when you open it, at most every 15 minutes. If your tracker syncs late days from 2026, they show up. At 12:00 UTC on January 12 (7 a.m. US Eastern time) we save your Wrapped as it is, and after that it doesn't change.

If a number looks different from Apple Health or Health Connect, see [Why your numbers can differ from Apple Health](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-apple-health) and [Why your numbers can differ from your tracker](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker). The daily limits above can also make a total lower.

## Share your Wrapped

1. Open your Wrapped and go to the slide you want to share.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Choose where to send it.

Your phone makes the image. It has no link in it. The text that goes with it includes your invite link.

- On the summary, **Show my name on the card** puts your first name on the shared card. Turn it off to share it as **My 2026**.
- Other members' names never appear on a shared card.
- **You moved most with** stays in the app. It shows **This one stays in the app** instead of **Share**. The first slide can't be shared either.

## Privacy

- **Only you see your Wrapped.** Nobody else can open it, and nothing is shared unless you tap **Share**.
- **Another member's Wrapped can name you.** If you're the person they moved with most, their **You moved most with** slide shows your name. A friend sees your first name and last initial. Someone who isn't your friend sees your username, unless you turned on **Show real name on leaderboards**. Blocks work both ways, so someone you blocked, or who blocked you, never sees you there. Your days only show on their chart if your [privacy settings](/account/privacy-settings) already let them see your daily numbers in that competition: **Show detailed stats to competitors** is on, you haven't hidden all of Steps, Calories, and Exercise, and, for a public competition with someone who isn't your friend, **Show on public leaderboards** is on. We check these each time they open it.
- **Notifications and email** about MoveTogether Wrapped say it's ready and nothing more. They carry no health numbers.
- **Deleting your account** deletes your Wrapped. Your name and days are also removed from other members' Wrapped. See [Delete your account](/account/data-and-privacy#deleting-your-data).

The [Privacy Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/privacy) explains how MoveTogether uses your data.

## Why don't I see it?

- **It hasn't arrived yet.** MoveTogether Wrapped comes to the app in early January 2027. Until then, nobody sees it.
- **You had fewer than 7 active days in 2026.** There's no Wrapped for this year, and no banner or profile row.
- **Your app is older than version 2.3.0.** Update MoveTogether from the App Store or Google Play.
- **The app couldn't reach MoveTogether.** Without a connection, or if Wrapped is briefly unavailable, the banner and profile row don't show. Try again later.
- **It's after the end of January.** The Home banner is gone, but your Wrapped is still on your profile under **Your year**.

If you've checked these and still don't see it, [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
