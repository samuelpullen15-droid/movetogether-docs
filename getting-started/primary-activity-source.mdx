<Note>
  Most of this page is about iPhone. Android has a tracker picker too, and it works a little differently. See [On Android](#on-android).
</Note>

Apple Health can hold readings from several devices at once: your iPhone, a watch, a ring, a workout app. MoveTogether counts **one** of them, the tracker you choose, for your Move, Exercise, and Steps rings. Those numbers are what your competitions, leagues, and challenges use.

If you haven't chosen one, MoveTogether counts your Apple Watch if you have one. Without a Watch, it picks for you based on which device recorded the most. Choose one yourself to be sure.

Move has two exceptions. On days you wear an Apple Watch, Move is your Watch's Move ring. Without a Watch, Move is the higher of your tracker's active calories and Apple Health's Move total. And until your tracker syncs today, MoveTogether counts another device so your rings don't sit at zero. See [why your numbers don't match your tracker's app](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker).

Workouts are the exception. Workouts from any device still show up and still count toward a **Workout Count** competition.

## Why only one

If you wear two trackers on the same walk, both record it. Adding them together would count that walk twice and reward owning more devices. Counting one tracker keeps every score fair.

## Choose or change your tracker

1. Open **Settings → Data Sources**.
2. Under **Your tracker**, find the card that says **We're counting your** followed by your device.
3. Tap **Change** (or **Choose your tracker** if you haven't picked one) and choose another tracker.

If the card says your tracker **hasn't synced recently**, tap **Get it syncing** for help, or open the device's own app and let it sync.

If your device isn't in the list, it isn't sharing into Apple Health yet. Follow the [Apple Health guide](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker).

Your choice is saved to your account. If you sign in on a new iPhone or reinstall the app, MoveTogether picks the same tracker again.

## Your tracker versus the Health app's sources

Changing your tracker in MoveTogether doesn't change which apps are allowed to write to Apple Health. Manage those in the Health app under **Browse**, then the metric, then **Data Sources & Access**.

## If totals look wrong

Compare the same date and metric on your chosen tracker. Active calories are not total calories, and Exercise minutes are not total workout time. Your Steps and Exercise will often be lower than Apple Health's, because Apple Health combines every device. If they don't match your tracker's own app, see [why your numbers don't match your tracker's app](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker). Steps and workouts you type in by hand don't count in MoveTogether. See [why your numbers differ](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-apple-health).

If the wrong tracker seems to be counting, tell [the support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) which tracker you chose, the date, and the metric.

## On Android

Android has a **Your tracker** picker too. Health Connect knows which app wrote each reading, not which device, so you pick an app. A Galaxy Watch and a Galaxy phone both write as Samsung Health, so they're one choice.

1. Open **Settings → Data Sources**.
2. Under **Your tracker**, tap **Choose your tracker**, or tap **Change** if the card says **We're counting your** followed by your tracker.
3. Pick the device you actually wear.

The list shows the apps that wrote activity to Health Connect in the last 7 days. If yours isn't there, it isn't sharing into Health Connect yet. Follow the [Health Connect guide](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker).

New members are asked during setup. If you used MoveTogether on Android before the picker arrived, it asks you once. Tap **Not now** to skip and pick later in Data Sources.

### What counts on Android

- **Steps and calories:** your tracker's number for the day. If your tracker didn't record that metric that day, MoveTogether uses Health Connect's combined total for it, so a day you left your watch at home doesn't read zero.
- **Exercise:** on a day your tracker recorded anything, Exercise minutes come from its workouts. On other days, workouts from every app count.
- **Workout Count competitions:** workouts from any app still count.
- **No tracker picked:** Health Connect's combined total counts every day. Health Connect's app priority decides which app counts when two record the same activity.

Steps and workouts you type in by hand never count.

### A new pick counts from today

When you pick or change your tracker, today and later days follow it. Days that already ended keep the numbers they had, so changing your tracker doesn't rewrite last week.

### On a new phone

If your tracker is Garmin, Oura, WHOOP, or Polar, your pick is saved to your account and comes back when you sign in on a new phone. It counts from the day it comes back. For any other app, pick it again in **Settings → Data Sources**.
