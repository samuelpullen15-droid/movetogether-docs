---
title: "Choose which tracker counts"
description: "Pick the one tracker that counts toward your rings, change it later in Data Sources, and understand why MoveTogether never adds devices together."
---

Apple Health and Health Connect can hold readings from several devices at once: your phone, a watch, a ring, a workout app. MoveTogether counts **one** of them, the tracker you choose. Only that tracker counts toward your rings, competitions, leagues, and challenges.

## Why only one

If you wear two trackers on the same walk, both record it. Adding them together would count that walk twice and reward owning more devices. Counting one tracker keeps every score fair.

## Choose or change your tracker

You pick your tracker during setup, when MoveTogether asks which one you use. To change it later on iPhone:

1. Open **Settings → Data Sources**.
2. Find the card that says **We're counting your** followed by your device.
3. Tap **Change** and choose another tracker.

If the card says your tracker **hasn't synced recently**, tap **Get it syncing** for help, or open the device's own app and let it sync.

If your device isn't in the list, it isn't sharing into Apple Health or Health Connect yet. Follow the guide for [Apple Health](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker) or [Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker).

## Your tracker versus the health app's sources

Changing your tracker in MoveTogether doesn't change which apps are allowed to write to Apple Health or Health Connect. On iPhone, manage those in the Health app under **Browse**, then the metric, then **Data Sources & Access**. On Android, manage them in Health Connect.

## If totals look wrong

Compare the same date and metric on your chosen tracker. Active calories are not total calories, and Exercise minutes are not total workout time. Your MoveTogether total will often be lower than the Apple Health or Health Connect total, because those combine every device. See [why your numbers differ](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-apple-health).

If the wrong tracker seems to be counting, tell [Marco support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) which tracker you chose, the date, and the metric.
