title: "Choose which tracker counts"
description: "Pick the one device that counts toward your rings on iPhone or Android, change it in Data Sources, and see what counts on days it hasn't synced."

Apple Health and Health Connect can hold readings from several devices at once: your phone, a watch, a ring, a workout app. MoveTogether counts **one** of them, your **tracker**, for your Move, Exercise, and Steps rings. Those are the numbers your competitions, leagues, and challenges use. This works on both iPhone and Android, on every plan.

## Why only one

If you wear two trackers on the same walk, both record it. Adding them together would count that walk twice and reward owning more devices. Counting one tracker keeps every score fair.

## Choose or change your tracker

1. Open **Settings** > **Data Sources**.
2. Under **Your tracker**, find the card that says **We're counting your** followed by your device.
3. Tap **Change**, or **Choose your tracker** if you haven't picked one.
4. Pick the device you actually wear.

If the card says your tracker **hasn't synced recently**, open that device's own app and let it sync. On iPhone you can also tap **Get it syncing** for help.

If your device isn't in the list, it isn't sharing into Apple Health or Health Connect yet. Follow the [Apple Health guide](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker) or the [Health Connect guide](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker).

On iPhone, your choice is saved to your account, so a new phone or a reinstall picks the same tracker again. On Android, that works for Garmin, Oura, WHOOP, and Polar. If you chose a phone app such as Samsung Health, pick it again on the new phone.

## How it works on each phone

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    **No tracker chosen yet:** MoveTogether counts your Apple Watch if you have one. Without a Watch, it counts whichever device recorded the most that day. Choose one yourself to be sure.

    Move has two exceptions. On days you wear an Apple Watch, Move is your Watch's Move ring. Without a Watch, Move is the higher of your tracker's active calories and Apple Health's own Move total.

    **Before your tracker syncs today**, MoveTogether counts another device so your rings don't sit at zero.

    **A new choice** also applies to the last 30 days.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    **The one-time ask:** after onboarding, once Health Connect has activity, the app asks **Is this your tracker?** or **Which one's your tracker?** Tap **Yes, that's my tracker**, pick one, or tap **Not now**. It only asks once. After that, use **Settings** > **Data Sources**.

    **No tracker chosen yet:** MoveTogether uses Health Connect's own combined total. When two apps record the same activity, Health Connect's app priority decides which one counts.

    **What you choose:** an app. Health Connect knows which app wrote a reading, not which device. A Galaxy Watch and a Galaxy phone both write as Samsung Health, so they're one choice.

    **On a day your tracker wrote nothing**, MoveTogether uses Health Connect's combined total instead, so a forgotten watch doesn't blank your day.

    **A new choice:** applies to today and later days. Days that already ended keep their numbers.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Workouts, Exercise minutes, and typed-in entries

- **Workouts:** workouts from any device still show up and still count toward a **Workout Count** competition.
- **Exercise minutes:** these come only from your tracker. A workout recorded by another device shows up but doesn't add minutes.
- **Typed-in entries:** steps and workouts you type in by hand never count, whichever tracker you pick.

## Your tracker versus the system's sources

Changing your tracker in MoveTogether doesn't change which apps may write to Apple Health or Health Connect.

- **iPhone:** in the Health app, tap **Browse**, then a metric, then **Data Sources & Access**.
- **Android:** open Health Connect from your phone's settings and manage app permissions there.

## Something not right?

- Compare the same date and metric on your chosen tracker. Active calories are not total calories, and Exercise minutes are not total workout time.
- Your Steps and Exercise are often lower than Apple Health's or Health Connect's totals, because those combine every device. See [why your numbers don't match your tracker's app](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker) and [why your numbers differ from Apple Health](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-apple-health).
- If the wrong tracker seems to be counting, tell [the support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) which tracker you chose, the date, and the metric.
