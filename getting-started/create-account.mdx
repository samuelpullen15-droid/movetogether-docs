## How to sign up

1. Get MoveTogether:
   - **iPhone:** download it from the App Store.
   - **Android (open beta):** install it from Google Play. See [Join the Android public beta](/getting-started/android-public-beta).
2. Open the app and tap **Get Started**.
3. Choose how you want to create your account:
   - **Sign in with Apple** uses your Apple ID. Apple can hide your email for privacy.
   - **Sign in with Google** uses your Google account.
4. Review and accept the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Community Guidelines.
5. Choose a display name and username. Your display name is what friends see on leaderboards. Your username is how people find and add you.
6. Add a profile photo if you like. You can change it later from Profile.

## Choosing a good username

Your username is how friends find you, so keep it short and recognizable. Usernames can include letters, numbers, and underscores. Change it later from **Settings → Personal Details → Username**; changes are limited to once every 30 days, and your old handle is held for 30 days.

## What happens next

Onboarding helps you pick your tracker, introduces leagues and seasonal events, and sets up invitations, notifications, and Coach Mo. New accounts also start a 7-day free trial of Membership; see [plans](/subscriptions/plans). [First-run onboarding](/getting-started/first-run-onboarding) has the details.

If you skip a step, you can finish it later in Settings.
