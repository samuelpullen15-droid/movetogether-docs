---

You create an account with your Apple or Google sign-in. There's no separate password. You must be 13 or older. New accounts choose a plan (Member or Pro) during onboarding.

## Sign up

1. Install MoveTogether.
   - **iPhone:** from the App Store.
   - **Android:** from Google Play. See [MoveTogether on Android](/getting-started/android).
2. Open the app and pick a sign-in button.
   - **iPhone:** **Sign in with Apple** or **Sign in with Google**.
   - **Android:** **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google**.
3. Read the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Community Guidelines, then tap **Accept and continue**. **Decline and sign out** takes you back out.
4. On **Set Up Your Profile**, add your first and last name, a username, and your birthday, and pick what Coach Mo should focus on.
5. Add a photo if you like. You can change it later.

Onboarding carries on from there. See [first-run onboarding](/getting-started/first-run-onboarding).

<Note>
  Already have an account? Sign in with the same method you used before (Apple or Google), so your profile, friends, competitions, and plan stay with you.
</Note>

## Your profile details

| Field | What it's for | Can you change it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | What friends see on leaderboards | Yes, in **Settings** > **Personal Details** |
| Username | How people find and add you. Letters, numbers, and underscores only | Yes, once every 30 days |
| Birthday | Confirms you're 13 or older | No. It's saved once, so check it before you continue |
| Photo | Your picture everywhere in the app | Yes, any time |

If you skip the photo, your profile shows a drawn character on a colored circle until you add one. If you sign in with Google and your Google account only has a letter picture, MoveTogether swaps it for a drawn character when you finish onboarding.

### Choose a character

<Note>New in version 2.2.0. Update the app if you don't see this yet.</Note>

You can pick which drawn character you get, and its color.

- **iPhone:** tap your photo on your profile and choose **Choose a character**, or go to **Settings** > **Personal Details** and tap **Choose a character**.
- **Android:** go to **Settings** > **Personal Details** and tap **Choose a character**.

Pick a character and a color, then tap **Save**. Saving a character replaces your photo. To use a photo instead, see [change your profile photo](/account/profile#change-your-profile-photo).

## Choosing a plan

Accounts made on version 2.1.1 or later choose Member or Pro during onboarding. Prices are US prices in the app.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member | \$1.99 | \$14.99, with a 3-day free trial |
| Pro | \$12.99 | \$99.99 |

Monthly Member has no free trial. The app shows your local price before you confirm. See [plans](/subscriptions/plans) for what each plan includes, and [first-run onboarding](/getting-started/first-run-onboarding#choosing-a-plan) for who doesn't have to choose one.

## If you don't finish signing up

An account that never finishes onboarding and is never used (no activity, friends, competitions, or plan) is deleted. About 23 days after sign-up, we email you first. Open the app by the date in that email, and sign in if it asks, to keep the account. Accounts aren't deleted before 30 days, and never without that email.

## Something not right?

- **Username taken:** try another. Availability is checked as you type.
- **Wrong birthday saved:** it can't be changed in the app. [Contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
- **Signed in and your old profile is missing:** sign out and sign in with the method you used the first time. See [account security](/account/security).
