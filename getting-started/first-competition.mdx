A competition is a race between friends over set dates, scored from your rings. You can join one with a code or link, or host your own. It works the same way on iPhone and Android.

## Join a competition

1. Open the invite. Any of these work:
   - A friend's invite link.
   - **+** > **Join with code**, then type the code.
   - On **Compete**, tap **Add competition** > **Enter invite code**.
   - An invitation in the app.
2. Check the dates, scoring mode, and who's in.
3. Tap **Join Competition**.

Your activity from the competition's start date counts, even if you join late.

If you can't join, the competition may be full, finished, or invite-only, or the code may be out of date. If you're on the Free plan and your account was made on version 2.1.1 or later, a private competition needs its host to have a free spot for you, or you need Member. See [plans](/subscriptions/plans).

## Host a competition

These steps follow the iPhone screens. Android asks the same questions in the same order.

1. Tap **+** > **New competition**. (Or on **Compete**, tap **Add competition** > **Create competition**.)
2. On **What kind of competition?**, pick **Compete** for a race with a leaderboard. **Collective** is one shared group goal instead. See [Collective competitions](/competitions/personal-and-shared-goals#collective-competitions).
3. Name it, and add a cover photo if you like.
4. Pick how points are scored. **Head to Head** on Steps is an easy first choice. See the [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference) for all eight modes.
5. Pick the dates, and whether it repeats.
6. Set the goal, if your scoring mode needs one.
7. Choose public or private, and teams if you want them.
8. Invite friends now, or after you create it.
9. Review the summary and tap **Create competition**.

The competition shows as upcoming until its start date.

## Build with AI

On the create screen, tap **Build with AI**. Answer a few questions about your group, dates, scoring, and who's in, then review and tap **Create competition** yourself. See [set up a competition with Build with AI](/getting-started/build-with-mo).

## Hosting limits

| | Free | Member | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Competitions you can host at once | 2 (active, upcoming, or draft) | No limit | No limit |
| Host Personal Goal, Stick To It, Collective, Sleep, or Best days | No | Yes | Yes |
| Host a group challenge (up to 100 people) | No | No | Yes |

Joining never counts toward the hosting limit. See [group challenges](/competitions/group-challenges).

**Friends on the Free plan:** in your private competitions, friends on the Free plan can join at no cost: up to 3 friends in all if you're a Member, and any number if you're Pro.

## Fair Play agreement

The first time you create a competition, a **Fair play** sheet appears before it's created. It lists a few rules: log activity honestly, respect other competitors, report suspected cheating instead of accusing publicly, and have fun.

1. Tick **I agree to play fair and follow the community guidelines.**
2. Tap **Accept and continue**.

You only agree once. After that, creating a competition goes straight through. If you close the sheet instead, nothing is created, and you can try again whenever you're ready. Joining a competition doesn't show this sheet.
