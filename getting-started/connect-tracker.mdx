---
title: "Connect your tracker"
description: "MoveTogether reads Apple Health on iPhone and Health Connect on Android. Share your wearable into one of them, then choose the tracker that counts."
---

MoveTogether reads your activity from one place: **Apple Health** on iPhone, or **Health Connect** on Android. Any watch, ring, or app that shares into those works. You don't need a wearable at all, because your phone's own step count works too.

MoveTogether doesn't connect to wearables directly, so there's no wearable account to sign in to here.

| Phone | MoveTogether reads | Your wearable's job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iPhone | Apple Health | Share its data into Apple Health from its own app |
| Android | Health Connect | Share its data into Health Connect from its own app |

## Set it up

1. In your wearable's own app, turn on sharing to Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android).
2. In MoveTogether, allow access to Apple Health or Health Connect when it asks.
3. Choose your tracker in **Settings** > **Data Sources**, under **Your tracker**. On Android, the app also asks you once after onboarding.

Step-by-step guides:

- [Connect your tracker to Apple Health](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker) (iPhone)
- [Connect your tracker to Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker) (Android)

## Supported devices

| Device | iPhone | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your phone alone | iPhone steps and activity | Phone steps and activity |
| Apple Watch | Writes to Apple Health on its own | Not available |
| Garmin, WHOOP, Oura, Polar, Ultrahuman | From its app to Apple Health | From its app to Health Connect |
| Google Health / Fitbit / Pixel Watch | From the Google Health app to Apple Health | From the Google Health app to Health Connect |
| Samsung Galaxy Watch | Not available | From Samsung Health to Health Connect |
| Strava | Workouts only, through Apple Health | Workouts only, through Health Connect |

Not every device shares every metric. Strava brings workouts but not all-day steps, and some trackers share workouts but not sleep.

MoveTogether also has an [Apple Watch app](/account/apple-watch-app). Installing it is separate from giving MoveTogether Apple Health access.

## Which data counts

You choose one **tracker**: the device whose numbers count toward your rings, competitions, and leagues. Two devices are never added together, so the same walk never counts twice. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source) for the full rules on each phone.

Also on both phones:

- Steps and workouts you type in by hand don't count.
- Workouts from any device still show up, and still count toward a **Workout Count** competition.
- **Sleep** counts only when a wearable recorded it: Apple Watch (iPhone), Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Polar, Fitbit, or Ultrahuman. On Android, any watch or ring that Health Connect marks as a wearable also counts, such as a Galaxy Watch. Sleep from your phone alone, or typed in by hand, never counts. See [sleep competitions](/competitions/sleep).

## Had an older direct connection?

Before October 9, 2026, some wearables connected to MoveTogether directly. Those connections stopped syncing on that date and may still be listed.

- **iPhone:** under **Connected services** in **Settings** > **Data Sources**. They can only be removed.
- **Android:** under **Moving to Health Connect** in **Settings** > **Data Sources**, marked **Stopped**.

To keep that device counting, turn on Apple Health or Health Connect sharing in its own app. Use the [Apple Health guide](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker) or the [Health Connect guide](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker).
