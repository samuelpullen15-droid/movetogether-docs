---
title: "Connect your tracker"
description: "MoveTogether reads Apple Health on iPhone and Health Connect on Android. Share your wearable into one of them, then choose the tracker that counts."
---

If your watch, ring, or app syncs to Apple Health or Health Connect, it works with MoveTogether. You don't need a wearable at all: your phone's own step count works too.

MoveTogether doesn't connect to wearables directly, so there is no wearable account to sign in to here.

| Phone | MoveTogether reads | Your wearable's job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iPhone | Apple Health | Share its data into Apple Health from its own app |
| Android | Health Connect | Share its data into Health Connect from its own app |

## Set it up

1. In your wearable's own app, turn on sharing to Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android).
2. In MoveTogether, allow access to Apple Health or Health Connect when asked.
3. Pick your tracker when MoveTogether asks which one you use.

Step-by-step guides for each device:

- [Connect your tracker to Apple Health](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker) (iPhone)
- [Connect your tracker to Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker) (Android)

## Supported devices

| Device | iPhone | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Watch | Writes to Apple Health on its own | Not available |
| Your phone alone | iPhone steps and activity | Phone steps and activity |
| Garmin, WHOOP, Oura, Polar, Ultrahuman | From its app to Apple Health | From its app to Health Connect |
| Google Health / Fitbit / Pixel Watch | From its app to Apple Health | From its app to Health Connect |
| Samsung Galaxy Watch | Not available | Samsung Health to Health Connect |
| Strava | Workouts only, through Apple Health | Workouts only, through Health Connect |

Strava brings workouts, not all-day steps. Not every device shares every metric, so a tracker can supply workouts but no sleep, for example.

MoveTogether also has an [Apple Watch app](/account/apple-watch-app). Installing it is separate from giving MoveTogether Apple Health access.

## One tracker counts

You choose one tracker, and only that tracker counts toward your rings, competitions, and leagues. MoveTogether doesn't add devices together, so the same walk never counts twice. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source).

## Had an older direct connection?

If you connected a wearable to MoveTogether directly before version 2.1.1, that connection keeps working for now. Switch to Apple Health or Health Connect in **Settings → Data Sources** so nothing is lost.

For missing readings, see [manage and troubleshoot data sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).
