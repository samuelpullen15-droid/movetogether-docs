---
title: "Setting and editing your goals"
description: "How to change your Move, Exercise, and Steps goals and understand when those goals affect competition scoring."
---

Your daily goals are **Move** (active calories), **Exercise** (minutes), and **Steps**. MoveTogether starts with 500 active calories, 30 exercise minutes, and 8,000 steps if you have not set your own values.

## Change a goal

1. Tap your photo at the top right of **Home** to open **Profile**.
2. In **Today's Activity**, tap the Move, Exercise, or Steps ring.
3. Enter a whole-number target or use the plus and minus controls.
4. Tap **Save**.

You can also change goals in **Settings → Customization → Daily Goals**. Pick targets that are realistic for a typical day; you can change them any time.

## Allowed ranges

| Goal | Range | Increment for the controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | 100 to 2,000 active calories | 50 calories |
| Exercise | 10 to 300 minutes | 5 minutes |
| Steps | 1,000 to 50,000 steps | 500 steps |

You can type any whole number in the displayed range; the listed increments apply to the plus and minus controls.

## Goals in competitions

Your daily goals affect **Ring Close Count** and **Percentage of Goals** competitions. They do not alter raw activity methods such as Step Count, Calorie Burn, Exercise Minutes, Total Activity, or Workout Count.

Personal Goal and Stick To It competitions use competition-specific targets instead. A Personal Goal participant locks their own target within the host’s range; Stick To It uses the host’s daily target for everybody. Read [personal and shared competition goals](/competitions/personal-and-shared-goals) before changing a goal to influence a competition.
