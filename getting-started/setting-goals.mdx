---
title: "Set your daily goals"
description: "Change your Move, Exercise, and Steps goals on iPhone or Android, see the allowed ranges, and learn when goals affect competition scores."
---

Your three daily rings each have a goal: **Move** (active calories), **Exercise** (minutes), and **Steps**. A ring closes when you reach its goal. Every plan can change its goals, on iPhone and Android.

A new account starts at **400** active calories, **30** exercise minutes, and **8,000** steps.

## Change a goal

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Tap your photo at the top right of **Home** to open Profile.
    2. In **Today's Activity**, tap the Move, Exercise, or Steps ring.
    3. Type a whole number, or use the plus and minus buttons.
    4. Tap **Save**.

    You can also go to **Settings** > **Customization** > **Daily Goals**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open **Settings** > **Customization** > **Daily Goals**.
    2. Tap Move, Exercise, or Steps.
    3. Type a whole number.
    4. Tap **Save**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Your rings on **Home** use the new goal right away.

## Allowed ranges

| Goal | Lowest | Highest | Plus and minus step (iPhone) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | 100 active calories | 2,000 active calories | 50 |
| Exercise | 10 minutes | 300 minutes | 5 |
| Steps | 1,000 steps | 50,000 steps | 500 |

Any whole number inside the range works. The step only applies to the plus and minus buttons.

**Picking a goal:** choose something you hit on a normal day, not your best day. A ring you close most days keeps your streak and your competition scores moving.

## Goals in competitions

Your daily goals change your score only in two scoring modes:

| Scoring mode | Uses your daily goals? |
| --- | --- |
| Ring Close Count | Yes. A ring counts when you reach its goal |
| Percentage of Goals | Yes. Your score is your progress against each goal |
| Head to Head, Total Activity, Workout Count, Distance | No. They use your raw numbers |
| Personal Goal, Stick To It | No. They use a target set for that competition |

In a Personal Goal competition, you lock your own target inside the host's range. In Stick To It, everyone has the host's daily target. See [personal and shared competition goals](/competitions/personal-and-shared-goals) and the [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference).

## Something not right?

- **The app won't save your number:** check it's a whole number inside the range above.
- **Ring closes but a competition says otherwise:** that competition may not use your daily goals. Check its scoring mode.
- **Rings aren't moving:** see [fitness data not syncing](/troubleshooting/sync-issues).
