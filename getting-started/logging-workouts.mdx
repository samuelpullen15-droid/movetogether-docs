---
title: "Logging workouts"
description: "How workouts reach MoveTogether, why hand-typed workouts don't count, and what to check when a workout is missing."
---

Workouts reach MoveTogether from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). There is no way to type a workout into MoveTogether, and workouts or steps typed into Apple Health or Health Connect by hand don't count. When a workout is missing, get the source syncing rather than entering it again.

## How a workout becomes ring progress

1. You record the workout on your watch, phone, or wearable app.
2. That app writes it to Apple Health or Health Connect.
3. MoveTogether reads it from there. The workout shows up, and if it's long enough it counts toward a **Workout Count** competition, whichever device recorded it.
4. Your Move, Exercise, and Steps rings update from the same data. On iPhone, they come from the [tracker you chose](/getting-started/primary-activity-source), so a workout from another device shows up but doesn't add to Steps or Exercise. Without an Apple Watch, it can add to Move: see [why your numbers don't match your tracker's app](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker).

If the workout isn't in Apple Health or Health Connect, it can't appear here.

## A workout is missing

Check these in order.

**Is it in the wearable's app?** If not, it was never recorded, and MoveTogether can't recover it.

**Is it in Apple Health or Health Connect?** If not, turn on sharing in the wearable's app. See [Apple Health](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker) or [Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker).

**Was it typed in by hand?** Workouts entered by hand in Apple Health or Health Connect don't count and won't show up.

**Are permissions still granted?** A permission can be revoked in your phone's settings after you first allow it. See [manage and troubleshoot data sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).

Then refresh. The **\+** button has **Sync activity** on both apps.

## Why there is no manual entry

Rings and competition scores are built from measured activity. Typed-in numbers can't be checked, and a competition where some scores are measured and others are typed in isn't fair. That's also why steps and workouts typed into Apple Health or Health Connect by hand don't count.

## Exercise minutes look wrong

MoveTogether credits Exercise minutes by its own rules, which can differ from the recorded duration. On iPhone, Exercise minutes come only from the tracker you chose, so a workout from another device doesn't add minutes. Walking and **Other** sessions need a minimum length and share a daily limit, and overlapping sessions are merged. [Fitness data not syncing](/troubleshooting/sync-issues#my-workouts-sync-but-my-exercise-ring-looks-different) has the exact numbers.

## Still stuck

If the workout is in Apple Health or Health Connect, wasn't typed in by hand, and still hasn't appeared after a day, [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support). Include the date and the app that recorded the workout.
