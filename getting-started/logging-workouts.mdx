---
title: "Logging workouts"
description: "How workouts reach your rings, why MoveTogether has no manual entry, and what to check when a workout is missing."
---

Workouts reach MoveTogether from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). There is no way to type a workout in by hand. When a workout is missing, get the source syncing rather than entering it again.

## How a workout becomes ring progress

1. You record the workout on your watch, phone, or wearable app.
2. That app writes it to Apple Health or Health Connect.
3. MoveTogether reads it from there and updates Move, Exercise, and Steps.

If the workout isn't in Apple Health or Health Connect, it can't appear here.

## A workout is missing

Check these in order.

**Is it in the wearable's app?** If not, it was never recorded, and MoveTogether can't recover it.

**Is it in Apple Health or Health Connect?** If not, turn on sharing in the wearable's app. See [Apple Health](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker) or [Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker).

**Is it from the tracker that counts?** Only your chosen tracker counts. A workout recorded on a different device won't show up. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source).

**Are permissions still granted?** A permission can be revoked in your phone's settings after you first allow it. See [manage and troubleshoot data sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).

Then refresh. On iPhone, the red **+** button has **Sync activity**.

## Why there is no manual entry

Rings and competition scores are built from measured activity. Typed-in numbers can't be checked, and a competition where some scores are measured and others are typed in isn't fair.

## Exercise minutes look wrong

MoveTogether credits Exercise minutes by its own rules, which can differ from the recorded duration. Walking and **Other** sessions need a minimum length and share a daily limit, and overlapping sessions are merged. [Fitness data not syncing](/troubleshooting/sync-issues#my-workouts-sync-but-my-exercise-ring-looks-different) has the exact numbers.

## Still stuck

If the workout is in Apple Health or Health Connect, came from your chosen tracker, and still hasn't appeared after a day, [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support). Include the date and the app that recorded the workout.
