title: "Building a competition with Mo"
description: "Use Build with AI to assemble a competition draft, revise it, switch to manual setup, and create it after review."
---

**Build with AI** is a guided competition builder. It asks questions and fills in a draft. Nothing is created until you review it and tap **Create competition**.

## Build a competition

1. Tap the red **+** button, then **New competition**.
2. Tap **Build with AI**.
3. Answer the questions about your group, dates, scoring, and invitations. Type an answer or pick a suggestion.
4. Watch the draft fill in as you go.
5. When the builder has enough, choose the review option.
6. Check the final screen, make any edits, then tap **Create competition**.

The usual checks still apply, including host limits and Fair Play requirements.

## Change your mind

Tap **Set it up myself** to switch to the manual wizard with your answers so far. Leaving the builder before creating sends no invitations and creates nothing.

The builder is separate from Coach Mo chat. Asking Coach Mo in chat can't create a competition for you.

For the manual path and joining, see [your first competition](/getting-started/first-competition).
