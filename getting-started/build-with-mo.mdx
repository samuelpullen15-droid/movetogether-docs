title: "Set up a competition with Build with AI"
description: "Answer a few questions and let Build with AI fill in a competition draft, then review it, switch to manual setup, or create it."
---

**Build with AI** sets up a competition by asking you questions and filling in a draft as you answer. It's on iPhone and Android. Nothing is created, and no invites go out, until you review the draft and tap **Create competition**.

## Build a competition

1. Tap **+** > **New competition**.
2. Tap **Build with AI** on the create screen.
3. Answer each question: what you want to run, what's counted, who's in, how long it runs, teams, and a name. Type your answer the way you'd say it to a friend, or tap one of the suggestions.
4. When the builder has what it needs, tap **Look it over**.
5. Check the summary, make any edits, then tap **Create competition**.

The usual rules still apply, including [hosting limits](/getting-started/first-competition#hosting-limits) and the one-time [Fair Play agreement](/getting-started/first-competition#fair-play-agreement).

## Change your mind

- Tap **Set it up myself** to switch to the manual steps. Your answers so far come with you.
- Leave the builder before creating, and nothing is created and no invites are sent.

## Something not right?

- **The builder doesn't understand an answer:** tap one of the suggestions instead.
- **It says it can't read messages right now:** tap the options instead of typing.
- Still stuck? Switch to **Set it up myself**. See [join or host your first competition](/getting-started/first-competition).
