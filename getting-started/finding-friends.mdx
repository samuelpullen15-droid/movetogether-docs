title: "Finding and adding friends"
description: "Find MoveTogether friends by username, invite link, or phone contacts, manage friend requests, and control who can send you requests."
---

Friends live in the **Social** tab.

## How to add friends

**Search by username.** Open **Social** and use the search field (**Search by username**). Tap **Add** to send a request.

**Share your invite link.** On iPhone, tap the red **+** button and choose **Invite friends**, then send the link in any messaging app. See [Invite & Earn](/account/referrals).

**Find friends from contacts.** In **Social**, tap **Find friends from contacts**. MoveTogether checks your contacts privately to show who is already on the app, and you can invite the rest.

**Suggestions.** MoveTogether may suggest people you might know based on mutual friends and contacts.

## Friend requests

You get a notification when someone sends you a request. Open **Social** and tap **Friend requests** to see what's pending, then tap **Accept** or decline. **Requests sent** shows requests you're waiting on.

## Who can send you friend requests?

Go to **Settings → Privacy Settings**, find **Who can send friend requests** under **Social**, and pick **Everyone**, **Friends of Friends**, or **No One**.

## Removing a friend

Open the person's profile from **Social**, open its options menu, tap **Remove friend**, and confirm. They stay in any active competitions you share, but they won't appear in your feed or be suggested for future competitions.
