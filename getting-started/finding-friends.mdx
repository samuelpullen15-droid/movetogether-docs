---
title: "Find and add friends"
description: "Find MoveTogether friends by username, phone contacts, or invite link, manage friend requests, and control who can send you requests."
---

Friends live in the **Social** tab, on the **Feed** side. You can find people by username, from your phone's contacts, or by sending your invite link. This works on iPhone and Android.

## Search by username

1. Open **Social** and stay on **Feed**.
2. Tap the search field (**Search by username…**) and type a username.
3. Tap **Add** next to the person to send a request.

## Find friends from your contacts

1. Open **Social** > **Feed**.
   - **iPhone:** tap **Find friends from contacts**.
   - **Android:** find the **Find from contacts** section.
2. Allow access to your contacts when your phone asks.
3. Tap **Add** next to anyone already on MoveTogether.

Only phone numbers are sent to check, they're never stored, and contact names stay on your phone.

<Note>
  Contacts only find people who added **and verified** their phone number in MoveTogether. To be findable yourself, add and verify your number in **Settings** > **Personal Details**.
</Note>

You'll also see this option on the **Bring someone** step of [onboarding](/getting-started/first-run-onboarding).

## Send your invite link

1. Tap **+** > **Invite friends**.
2. Share the link in any messaging app.

A friend who joins through your link gets **30 days of Member free** if their account is new (up to 7 days old) and has never had a plan. You also move toward Invite & Earn rewards. See [Invite & Earn](/account/referrals).

## Suggestions

Under **People you may know**, MoveTogether may suggest people based on mutual friends and contacts.

## Friend requests

You get a notification when someone sends you a request.

1. Open **Social**.
2. Tap **Friend requests** to see who's waiting.
3. Accept or decline each one.

**Requests sent** shows requests you're still waiting on.

## Who can send you friend requests

Go to **Settings** > **Privacy Settings** and, under **Who can send friend requests**, pick **Everyone**, **Friends of friends**, or **No one**. See [privacy settings](/account/privacy-settings).

## Removing a friend

Open the person's profile from **Social**, open its options menu, tap **Remove friend**, and confirm. Removing a friend doesn't take either of you out of competitions you share.

## Something not right?

- **A friend doesn't show up from contacts:** they need to have verified their phone number in MoveTogether. Search their username or send your invite link instead.
- **Can't send a request:** their privacy setting may block requests from people outside their friends.
- **Invited friend didn't get the free month:** see [Invite & Earn](/account/referrals), then [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) if it still looks wrong.
