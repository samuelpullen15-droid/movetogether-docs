MoveTogether is a social fitness app for friendly activity competitions. It's on iPhone through the App Store and in open beta on Android through Google Play. It works with any watch, ring, or app that syncs to Apple Health or Health Connect, or with just your phone.

## What you can do

Track your Move, Exercise, and Steps rings, then join or host competitions with friends. Formats include Ring Close Count, Percentage of Goals, Total Activity, Step Count, Calorie Burn, Exercise Minutes, Workout Count, Personal Goal, and Stick To It. Every format is fair across devices, because every device's data arrives the same way. See the [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference).

You can also follow friends, climb weekly Move Leagues, build streaks, earn badges, and ask Coach Mo for coaching.

## Find your way around

On iPhone, the tab bar has **Home**, **Social**, **Compete**, and **Chat**, plus a red **+** button.

- **+ button:** Ask Coach Mo, New competition, Join with code, Invite friends, Sync activity, and Help.
- **Profile:** tap your photo at the top right of Home.
- **Settings:** open Profile, then tap the gear at the top right. Paths in this Help Center such as **Settings → Data Sources** start there.
