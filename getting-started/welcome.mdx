MoveTogether is a social fitness app. You close three daily rings (Move, Exercise, and Steps) and race your friends in competitions. It's on iPhone through the App Store and on Android through Google Play, and iPhone and Android friends play in the same competitions.

You don't need a wearable. Your phone's own step count works. If you have a watch, ring, or fitness app, it works when it shares into Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android).

## What you can do

- **Rings:** Move is active calories, Exercise is minutes, and Steps is steps.
- **Competitions:** eight scoring modes on both iPhone and Android: Ring Close Count, Head to Head, Workout Count, Percentage of Goals, Total Activity, Personal Goal, Stick To It, and Distance. See the [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference).
- **Leagues:** you're placed in a [Move League](/competitions/leagues) with people at your level.
- **Streaks and badges:** see [streaks](/engagement/streaks-shields) and [badges](/engagement/badges).
- **Coach Mo:** Mo is the app's AI coach. See [Coach Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo).

New accounts choose a plan during onboarding: Member or Pro. See [plans](/subscriptions/plans).

## Find your way around

The tab bar is the same on both apps: **Home**, **Social**, and **Compete**, with a red **+** button at the end. If Movecoins has reached your account (US only), a **Rewards** tab appears too.

- **Social** has two halves, **Feed** (friends and their activity) and **Chats** (messages and group chats). Swipe or tap to switch.
- The **+** button opens **Help**, **Invite friends**, **New message**, **Join with code**, **New competition**, **Data Sources**, **Sync activity**, and **Ask Coach Mo**.
- **Profile:** tap your photo at the top right of **Home**.
- **Settings:** open Profile, then tap the gear at the top right. Paths in this Help Center such as **Settings** > **Data Sources** start there.
- **Help:** **+** > **Help** opens the support chat on both apps.
