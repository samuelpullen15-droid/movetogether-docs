---
title: "Change the app language"
description: "Use MoveTogether in English, Spanish, French, or German, change the language on iPhone or Android, and see what follows your language."
---

MoveTogether is available in English, Spanish, French, and German on iPhone and Android. The app follows the language you set for it in your phone's settings. There's no separate language picker inside the app. If your phone uses a language MoveTogether doesn't support, the app shows English.

## Change the language

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. In MoveTogether, open **Settings** > **Language**. This opens the iPhone Settings page for MoveTogether.
    2. Choose the app's language there.
    3. Return to MoveTogether.

    If iOS doesn't show a language option for the app, add the language to your iPhone's preferred languages first, in the iPhone Settings app under **General** > **Language & Region**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. In MoveTogether, open **Settings** > **Language**.
    2. Android opens its app language screen for MoveTogether. Pick a language.
    3. Return to MoveTogether.

    On older Android versions, the button opens the app's system settings instead, and the app follows your phone's language.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What follows your language

| Part of MoveTogether | In your language? |
| --- | --- |
| App screens and buttons | Yes |
| Coach Mo's replies and cards | Yes |
| Push notifications | Yes |
| What's New release notes | Yes |
| This Help Center | English only |
| Names people type (competitions, chats) | Stays as written |

When you open the app after changing the language, it tells MoveTogether. Notifications and Coach Mo switch from then on.

## Language versus units

Language doesn't change units. Kilometers or miles, and kilograms or pounds, are a separate setting. See [units and appearance settings](/account/appearance-and-display).

The website at movetogetherfitness.com has its own language switcher. Changing it doesn't change the app.

## Something not right?

- **Some text is still in English:** close and reopen the app. Names and messages people typed stay as written.
- **Notifications are in the old language:** open the app once after changing the language.
- Still wrong? [Contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) and tell us which screen.
