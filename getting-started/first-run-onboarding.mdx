Onboarding is the set of screens you see right after you [create your account](/getting-started/create-account). It sets up your profile, connects your activity, shows you your league, and asks you to choose a plan. iPhone and Android show the same steps in the same order.

If you leave partway through, the app picks up close to where you stopped the next time you open it.

## The steps, in order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Profile">
    Your first and last name, a username, your birthday, and **What should Mo focus on?** A photo is optional. See [create your account](/getting-started/create-account#your-profile-details).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Three quick questions">
    **What usually stops you?** and two short screens on how MoveTogether helps. Tap through them. Nothing here changes your settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your device">
    - **iPhone:** on **What do you use?**, pick your wearable or **Just my iPhone**.
    - **Android:** on **How do you track your activity?**, pick **My phone or Wear OS watch**, or your wearable.

    On Android, Suunto shows **(not yet)** and can't be picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your activity">
    Allow MoveTogether to read Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). If you picked a wearable, the screen first shows how to turn on sharing in that wearable's own app. See [connect your tracker](/getting-started/connect-tracker).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet your league">
    You're placed in a weekly league with people at your level. Tap **Let's Go**. See [Move Leagues](/competitions/leagues).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    New accounts choose Member or Pro here. See [choosing a plan](#choosing-a-plan) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring someone">
    Share your invite link, or tap **Find friends from contacts**. Tap **I'll do this later** to skip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On Notifications">
    Allow notifications, or tap **Not now**. You can change this later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**If you opened a friend's invite link before signing up:**

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Version 2.2.0">
    After you connect your activity, the app shows their competition and who invited you, and asks before it joins you. Tap **Join Competition** (iPhone) or **Join** (Android) to join, or **Not now** to skip it. If the code doesn't work, a **Time to Move** screen offers **See my league** or **Join with invite code**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Version 2.1.3">
    After you connect your activity, onboarding joins you to their competition instead of showing your league. If the code doesn't work, a **Time to Move** screen offers **See my league** or **Join with invite code**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Choosing a plan

Accounts made on version 2.1.1 or later must choose Member or Pro to use the app. Both plans are on one screen. The screen has no close button. **Sign Out** is the only other way out.

- **Member:** \$1.99 a month, or \$14.99 a year with a 3-day free trial (US prices).
- **Pro:** \$12.99 a month, or \$99.99 a year (US prices).

The app shows your local price before you confirm. If you cancel a Member free trial, your access ends right away and this screen comes back. See [plans](/subscriptions/plans).

You won't see this screen, or you can close it and use the Free plan, if:

| You are | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Someone whose account was made before version 2.1.1 | You keep the Free plan. See [plans](/subscriptions/plans) for what changes if you start a paid plan |
| Under 18 | You can close the screen and use the Free plan |
| Invited with a friend's invite link | You get 30 days of Member free when you join, so the screen goes away. See [Invite & Earn](/account/referrals) |
| Invited to a private competition by a Pro host, or by a Member host with a free spot | You can close the screen and play in that host's private competitions free |

A Member host has 3 free spots for friends, shared across all their private competitions. A Pro host has no limit. Seasonal events are free for everyone.

## After onboarding

- **Android:** the first time Health Connect has activity, the app asks once which device is your tracker. On iPhone, pick it any time in **Settings** > **Data Sources**. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source).
- **After an app update:** a What's New screen shows what changed. See [what's new after an update](/getting-started/whats-new).

## What you can finish later

- Change your photo: tap your photo at the top right of **Home**.
- Change your goals: see [set your daily goals](/getting-started/setting-goals).
- Change notification types in **Settings** > **Notifications**, and the system permission in your phone's settings.
- Change privacy in **Settings** > **Privacy Settings**.

## Something not right?

- **Stuck on the plan screen and you think you shouldn't be:** check the table above, then [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) from **+** > **Help**.
- **Rings still at zero after connecting:** see [fitness data not syncing](/troubleshooting/sync-issues).
- **Never finished onboarding?** Unused accounts are deleted after a warning email. See [if you don't finish signing up](/getting-started/create-account#if-you-dont-finish-signing-up).
