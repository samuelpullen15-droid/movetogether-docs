title: "First-run onboarding"
description: "What MoveTogether asks for when you create an account, including data-source setup, coaching choices, and optional permissions."
---

Onboarding sets up your identity, activity source, and first MoveTogether experience. The exact path varies with your choices and whether you opened an invite link. If you leave partway through, the app can resume the unfinished flow when you return.

## Identity

Add the name and unique username people will use to recognize you. A profile photo is optional and can be changed later. Usernames are checked for availability as you enter them.

## Data-source setup

Next, allow MoveTogether to read Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), then choose the tracker that counts. On iPhone, MoveTogether asks **Which one's your tracker?** Your wearable shares into the health app from its own app; see [Connect your tracker](/getting-started/connect-tracker).

**Get Started** then introduces the app, followed by **League intro** and **Seasonal events**. You can create or join a first competition now or later from **Compete**.

## Invitations and notifications

You may see **Invite** to share your referral link, then **Notifications**. An invite-link variant can join the competition from the link instead of following the standard first-competition path.

## Coach Mo setup

The later flow introduces **Meet Mo**, lets you choose a **Persona**, and shows a **First Insight**.

## Membership

Accounts created on version 2.1.1 or later then start a 7-day free trial of Membership, which continues as a paid plan unless you cancel. The app shows your price before you confirm. Accounts created before 2.1.1 don't see this step. See [plans](/subscriptions/plans).

After that, you can choose a **Morning Brief** time and respond to the **Location** request if it's shown.

Permissions are optional unless a feature needs them.

## What you can finish later

- Change your photo from **Profile** (tap your photo at the top right of Home).
- Change your tracker from **Settings → Data Sources**.
- Create or join a competition from **Compete**, or with the red **+** button on iPhone.
- Change notification categories in **Settings → Notifications**, and system permission in your phone's settings.
- Change privacy in **Settings → Privacy Settings**.
- Change your Coach Mo persona or Morning Brief schedule in Coach Mo settings.
