MoveTogether is on Android through Google Play. iPhone and Android friends compete in the same competitions and on the same leaderboards.

## Install

1. Open the [MoveTogether Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=studio.designspark.movetogether).
2. Tap **Install**.

<Note>
  Google controls regional availability, device compatibility, and the install button. MoveTogether's website cannot confirm availability on Google's behalf.
</Note>

## Your data comes from Health Connect

On Android, MoveTogether reads your activity from Health Connect. Your wearable shares into Health Connect from its own app. See [Connect your tracker to Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker).

MoveTogether reads six Health Connect types and no more: **steps, active calories, exercise sessions, sleep, weight, and height**. Sleep, weight, and height are read for Pro members only; without Pro, MoveTogether reads the three activity types. Heart rate is not read.

It never writes to Health Connect. It reads up to the last 30 days of activity, and it doesn't ask for Health Connect's history permission. Reading in the background is a separate permission. You can revoke any of it in Health Connect at any time.

Pick your tracker in **Settings → Data Sources**, under **Your tracker**, and its steps and calories count toward your rings. On a day it didn't record one of them, or if you haven't picked a tracker, MoveTogether uses Health Connect's combined total, where Health Connect's app priority decides which app wins when two record the same activity. Steps and workouts you type in by hand don't count. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source#on-android).

## Sign in and compete

Sign in with Apple or Google. If you already have a MoveTogether account, use the same sign-in method so your profile, friends, competitions, and plan stay with that account.

## Get updates

Google Play delivers MoveTogether updates. Open the Play listing to check for an available update.

## Share feedback

Use [Share feedback](https://movetogetherfitness.com/roadmap) for bugs and feature requests.

## Need help with your account?

For account access, billing, privacy, deletion, security, or another sensitive issue, use the in-app support chat instead of the public feedback board. Tap **\+ → Help**, or go to **Settings → Contact Support**. See [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
