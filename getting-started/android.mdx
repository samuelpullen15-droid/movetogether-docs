MoveTogether for Android is on Google Play. It has the same plans, competitions, leagues, and Coach Mo as the iPhone app, and iPhone and Android friends play in the same competitions and on the same leaderboards.

## Install

1. Open the [MoveTogether Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=studio.designspark.movetogether).
2. Tap **Install**.
3. Open the app and tap **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**. See [create your account](/getting-started/create-account).

Already use MoveTogether on an iPhone? Sign in with the same method (Apple or Google) so your profile, friends, competitions, and plan come with you.

<Note>
  Google controls which countries and devices can install the app.
</Note>

## Your data comes from Health Connect

On Android, MoveTogether reads your activity from Health Connect. Your wearable shares into Health Connect from its own app. See [Connect your tracker to Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker).

### What MoveTogether reads

| Data | Who it's asked of |
| --- | --- |
| Steps, active calories, exercise sessions | Everyone (required) |
| Distance | Everyone (optional) |
| Sleep | Everyone (optional) |
| Weight | Pro members (optional) |

- Heart rate isn't read.
- It reads up to the last 30 days. It doesn't ask for Health Connect's history permission.
- Reading in the background is a separate permission, so your rings update while the app is closed.
- It never writes to Health Connect.
- Steps and workouts you type in by hand don't count, and sleep counts only from a wearable.

You can revoke any of this in Health Connect at any time.

### Which device counts

You pick one tracker, and only its numbers fill your rings. The app asks you once after onboarding, and you can change it in **Settings** > **Data Sources**. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source).

## What's different from iPhone

| Feature | Android |
| --- | --- |
| Apple Watch app | iPhone only |
| Siri and Shortcuts | iPhone only |
| Coach Mo on Apple Intelligence | iPhone only |
| A new tracker choice | Counts from the moment you choose. On iPhone it also applies to the last 30 days |
| Changing the app language | Opens Android's app language screen. See [language and region](/getting-started/language-and-region) |

## Get updates

Google Play delivers updates. Open the Play listing to check for one. After an update, the app shows a What's New screen once. See [what's new after an update](/getting-started/whats-new).

If the app says **Update MoveTogether**, your version is too old to keep competing. Tap **Open Google Play** and update.

## Share an idea

Tap **Got an idea? Tell us** in **Settings** to send a feature idea.

## Need help?

Open the in-app support chat with **+** > **Help**, or go to **Settings** > **Contact Support**. Use it for account access, billing, privacy, deletion, or anything else. See [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
