---
title: "What are Movecoins?"
description: "Movecoins are reward points you earn on active days and trade for digital gift cards. Who can use them, where to find them, and the basic rules."
---

Movecoins are reward points. You collect them on the days you move, then trade them for digital gift cards. They're for people in the US, on iPhone and Android.

Movecoins is off until you turn it on. It's optional, and you can turn it off any time.

## Who can use Movecoins

| | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Turn on Movecoins and earn coins | Anyone whose App Store or Google Play account country is the United States, on any plan, at any age |
| Cash out for gift cards | Member or Pro, 18 or older, living in the US (the 50 states and DC) |

- **Pro earns double** coins from activity.
- **Under 18?** You can earn coins now and cash them out once you turn 18. They still expire on the normal schedule while you wait.
- **No paid plan?** You can still earn and keep coins. You need Member or Pro to cash them out. See [cash out for gift cards](/movecoins/cash-out).

If you don't see Movecoins anywhere in the app, your App Store or Google Play account country is probably not the United States.

## Where to find it

- The **Rewards** tab in the tab bar.
- Your coin balance at the top of **Home**. Tap it to open Movecoins.
- **Settings** > **Invite & earn rewards**, on the Movecoins card.

## How you earn

| Way to earn | Movecoins |
| --- | --- |
| Each active day (7,500 steps or 30 exercise minutes) | 200 |
| 10 active days in a calendar month | 500 bonus |
| 20 active days in the same month | 1,000 more bonus |
| Welcome gift, the first time you turn it on | 50, once |
| A friend you invite logs 3 active days | 1,000 |

Pro doubles the coins from active days and monthly bonuses. See [how you earn Movecoins](/movecoins/earning) for the full rules and an example month.

Nothing is left to chance. There are no draws, spins, or prizes for ranking. The same rules apply to everyone.

## What gift cards cost

Cards come in \$5, \$10, \$15, and \$20. A \$5 card costs 5,000 coins on Pro or 5,500 on Member. See [cash out for gift cards](/movecoins/cash-out) for every price and the Pro first-card offer.

## What Movecoins are not

Movecoins are not money. They have no cash value. You can't buy them, sell them, or move them to another account. A subscription pays for app features. It doesn't buy coins.

## When coins expire

You can use a coin until the end of the 12th month after the month you earned it. Months are counted in UTC.

**Example:** coins earned any time in March 2027 can be used until the end of March 2028.

When you cash out, your oldest coins are used first. The app doesn't show expiry dates, so cash out once you have enough for a card.

If you delete your account, your coins are cancelled and aren't paid out.
