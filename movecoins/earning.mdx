You earn Movecoins in four ways: active days, monthly bonuses, a one-time welcome gift, and inviting friends. Everyone earns by the same fixed rules, on any plan. Movecoins must be on. See [turn Movecoins on or off](/movecoins/turn-on).

## The amounts

| How | Movecoins | On Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each active day | 200 | 400 |
| 10 active days in a calendar month | 500 bonus | 1,000 bonus |
| 20 active days in the same month | 1,000 more bonus | 2,000 more bonus |
| Welcome gift, once ever | 50 | 50 |
| A friend you invite logs 3 active days | 1,000 | 1,000 |

## Active days

An **active day** is a day with at least **7,500 steps or 30 exercise minutes**. Either one counts. The day is your local calendar date.

A day with more than 100,000 steps or more than 600 exercise minutes is treated as a data error and earns nothing.

## Monthly bonuses

Reach **10 active days** in a calendar month for a 500-coin bonus. Reach **20 active days** in the same month for 1,000 more, on top. The count starts again on the 1st of each month.

The **Rewards** tab shows **This month**: your active days, coins earned, and how many more active days you need for the next bonus.

## Pro earns double

On any day you're on Pro, including a Pro free trial, your active-day coins and monthly bonuses are doubled. The **Rewards** tab shows **2x earning on**.

- The plan that counts is the one you had that day. Changing plans later doesn't change coins you already earned.
- A monthly bonus is doubled if you were on Pro on the day you reached it.
- The welcome gift and referral coins are never doubled.

## Example month

Jordan turns on Movecoins on March 1 and gets the 50-coin welcome gift. In March, Jordan has 22 active days.

| | Member or Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 22 active days | 22 x 200 = 4,400 | 22 x 400 = 8,800 |
| 10-day bonus | 500 | 1,000 |
| 20-day bonus | 1,000 | 2,000 |
| Welcome gift | 50 | 50 |
| **March total** | **5,950** | **11,850** |

On Pro, 11,850 coins covers a \$10 card (10,000 coins) with coins left over. Your cash-out allowance also has to cover the card. See [cash out for gift cards](/movecoins/cash-out).

## Welcome gift

You get **50 Movecoins** the first time you turn Movecoins on. You get it only once, even if you turn Movecoins off and on again.

## Invite friends

You get **1,000 Movecoins** when a friend you invite creates a new MoveTogether account with your invite link or code, and then logs 3 active days.

- Movecoins must be on before the referral completes. A referral that completed earlier, or while Movecoins was off, doesn't earn coins.
- You can earn this for up to 5 friends a calendar month (UTC). The **Rewards** tab shows how many you have **left this month**.
- Your friend must be a real, separate person who never had a MoveTogether account. Invites to yourself or your own other accounts don't count.
- Your friend doesn't earn Movecoins for being invited. They get a free month of Member instead.
- If you share your invite publicly, say that you get rewards when people join.

Find your invite link in [Invite & Earn](/account/referrals), or tap **Invite** on the **Rewards** tab.

## What counts

- Activity your phone shares with MoveTogether: on iPhone through **Apple Health**, on Android through **Health Connect**. That includes data from wearables that sync to either one.
- Only activity from the day you turn Movecoins on. Earlier days never earn.

## What doesn't count

- Steps and workouts you type in by hand.
- Data from an app or method meant to create activity that didn't happen.
- Days with unrealistic numbers, which are treated as data errors.
- Watching ads, ratings, reviews, downloading other apps, turning on notifications, or how you rank against other people.

## When coins show up

Activity can arrive late from your phone or tracker, so your balance may update after the day ends. If your activity data is later changed or removed, your balance can be corrected.
