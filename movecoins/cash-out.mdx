Trade your Movecoins for digital gift cards. Cards come in **\$5, \$10, \$15, and \$20**, sent by email. You choose the store after you cash out. Cashing out takes Member or Pro, and you must be 18 or older and live in the US.

## Who can cash out

Each time you cash out, you need all of these:

- **Movecoins turned on.**
- **An active Member or Pro plan that you pay for, or that was given to you**, like the free month of Member from a friend's invite or free Pro time from [Invite & Earn](/account/referrals). A paid plan you cancelled still counts until it ends.
- **18 or older.** You can earn coins at any age.
- **Living in the US** (the 50 states and DC), with a US App Store or Google Play account.
- **A verified phone number** on your account. One phone number can be verified on only one account. If you haven't verified one, the app asks you to when you cash out.
- **An account at least 14 days old.**

Free plans and free trials can't cash out. On a trial, the **Rewards** tab says "Members and Pro can cash out. Yours opens once your trial ends and your first payment goes through."

## How to cash out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a card">
    Tap the **Rewards** tab. Under **Cash out**, tap a card that shows **Get for** and a price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    The card is sent to the email in **Send it to**. It's filled in from your account. Change it if you want the card somewhere else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Check **I'm 18 or older** and **I live in the US**, then tap **Get my \$5 card** (or whichever amount you picked).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The app confirms that your card is processing and the day to expect it, for example "We'll email it to you@example.com around October 27." Your coins are set aside right away.

## How long it takes

- Every cash-out is held for **2 days** before it's sent. This gives refunds, cancelled plans, and fraud time to show up.
- A person on our team reviews **your first cash-out** before it's sent. Later ones may be reviewed too.
- Once it's sent, the card usually arrives within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

In **History**, the cash-out shows as **Processing, expected** and a date until it's sent.

### Pro first-card offer

Your first cash-out ever, made on a Pro plan you pay for, costs **30% of the usual coins**. The card shows "70% off your first card".

| Card | First card on paid Pro |
| --- | --- |
| \$5 | 1,500 coins |
| \$10 | 3,000 coins |
| \$15 | 4,500 coins |
| \$20 | 6,000 coins |

- It applies once per person: once per account and once per email address.
- It doesn't apply on Member, or on Pro time that was given to you.
- It doesn't change the card's dollar value or your cash-out allowance.

## "Not available yet"

A card that says **Not available yet** is over your **cash-out allowance**, even if you have enough coins. The allowance is a separate limit on how much you can cash out.

- Each month on Member or Pro adds to it, whether you pay for the plan or it was given to you. Pro adds more than Member. Days on a free trial or the Free plan add nothing.
- Your allowance builds only from the month you first turned Movecoins on.
- Unused allowance carries over, up to a limit.
- A paid Member month counts when you open the app that month. A month you never open the app adds nothing.
- If your plan ends, you keep the allowance you built, but you can use it only once you're on Member or Pro again.

The app doesn't show your allowance amount. Check back later and the card unlocks as your allowance grows.

## What the card buttons mean

| The card shows | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Get for** and a price | You can cash out this card now. |
| A number of **more coins** | You need that many more coins. |
| **Not available yet** | The card is over your cash-out allowance. |
| **Upgrade to cash out** | You need Member or Pro. Use the buttons above the cards: **Become a Member** or **Go Pro**. |
| **Cash out unlocks when your trial ends** | You're on a free trial. |
| **Fix your payment to cash out** | Your Pro payment failed. Tap **Update payment**. |

## Gift card delivery

- Our rewards partner, Tremendous, sends gift cards by email.
- You pick the brand from the options shown when you claim the card. The brands on offer can change, and the brands don't sponsor Movecoins.
- Each card follows its brand's terms, including any expiry date. Claim yours promptly.
- Once a card is delivered, it's yours. Lost or stolen cards can't be replaced.
- Typed the wrong email? Message the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) right away. We can cancel a cash-out before its card is ordered and return your coins.

## History

The **History** list on the **Rewards** tab shows every cash-out and gift.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Processing** | In the 2-day hold or in review. Not sent yet. |
| **On its way** | Sent. Check your email. |
| **Delivered** | The card reached your email. |
| **Delayed, we'll keep trying** | Sending hit a problem. We retry on our own. |
| **Couldn't send. Our team is on it.** | Sending failed. Our team follows up. |
| **Returned. Coins back in your balance** | The card wasn't sent, and your coins and allowance came back. |

If a cash-out fails before a card is ordered, your coins and allowance come back on their own.

## Taxes

Rewards you receive may be taxable income. MoveTogether doesn't give tax advice. We may ask for tax information, like a Form W-9, when the law requires it. See the [Movecoins program terms](https://movetogetherfitness.com/movecoins-terms).

## Something not right?

- Card didn't arrive? Check your spam folder first. If it's still missing, message the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) within 30 days of it being sent.
- Seeing an error when you cash out? See [Movecoins problems](/troubleshooting/movecoins).
