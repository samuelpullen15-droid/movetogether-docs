---
title: "Turn Movecoins on or off"
description: "How to turn on Movecoins on iPhone or Android, what you agree to, the welcome gift, and how to turn it off again."
---

Movecoins is off until you turn it on. Turning it on lets MoveTogether use your daily steps and exercise minutes to award coins. On iPhone that data comes from Apple Health. On Android it comes from Health Connect. Movecoins is for people in the US. See [What are Movecoins?](/movecoins/overview).

## Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Movecoins">
    Tap the **Rewards** tab. Or open **Settings** > **Invite & earn rewards** and tap **Turn on Movecoins** on the Movecoins card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the screen">
    It explains what Movecoins uses and links to the **Movecoins program terms** and the **Privacy policy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Turn on Movecoins">
    Tapping the button is how you agree to the program terms and to MoveTogether using your activity data as the privacy policy describes. Tap **Not now** to skip.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You'll see **Here's 50 Movecoins!** with your welcome gift. Tap **Start earning** to open your wallet.

### Messages you might see

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "We'll update how your activity syncs first. It only takes a moment." | Your activity sync needs a quick update before Movecoins can start. Follow the screen, then turn Movecoins on. |
| "Connect Health Connect first, then turn on Movecoins." (Android) | Connect Health Connect. See [connect Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker). |
| "Our terms were just updated. Take a look, then turn on Movecoins again." | Read the updated terms, then tap **Turn on Movecoins** again. |
| "Update the app to turn on Movecoins." | Update MoveTogether from the App Store or Google Play. |
| "Movecoins are only available in the US." | Your store account country isn't the United States. |

## What happens next

- **Coins count from today.** Activity from before the day you turn it on never earns coins.
- **Welcome gift.** The first time you turn Movecoins on, you get 50 Movecoins. You get it only once, even if you turn Movecoins off and on again.
- **Your balance** shows on the **Rewards** tab and at the top of **Home**.

## Turn it off

1. Open the **Rewards** tab.
2. Tap **...** (**Movecoins options**) at the top right.
3. Tap **Turn off Movecoins**, then **Turn off**.

What happens:

- You keep the coins you have. They still expire on the normal schedule.
- You stop earning. The day you turn it off doesn't earn.
- MoveTogether stops using your activity for Movecoins.
- You can't cash out while Movecoins is off.
- Referrals that complete while it's off don't earn coins.

## Turn it back on

Turn it on again the same way. Earning starts from that day. Days while Movecoins was off never earn coins, and there's no second welcome gift.

## Something not right?

- Don't see the Rewards tab? Check that your App Store or Google Play account country is the United States. See [Movecoins problems](/troubleshooting/movecoins).
- Turned it on but no coins yet? Coins arrive once your activity syncs. See [how you earn Movecoins](/movecoins/earning#when-coins-show-up).
