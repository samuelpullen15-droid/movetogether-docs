MoveTogether keeps the information it needs to run your rings, competitions, leaderboards, and coaching. We don't sell your personal or health data, and we never use health or fitness data for advertising. You can download a copy of your data or delete your account at any time, on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

## What MoveTogether keeps

- **Account:** your name, email, username, photo, and the settings you choose.
- **Fitness:** activity totals, workouts, goals, and other health numbers you allow MoveTogether to read from Apple Health or Health Connect. MoveTogether never writes changes back to them.
- **Competitions and social:** scores, rankings, history, friends, posts, and messages.
- **Diagnostics:** app events, masked session replays, and crash details, only while **Help improve MoveTogether** is on.

Other members see only what your [Privacy Settings](/account/privacy-settings) allow.

## Downloading your data

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **Privacy**, tap **Privacy Settings**.
3. Tap **Download my data**.
4. Tap **Send to my email**.

You get an email with a download link. It can take a few minutes. The link expires after 7 days.

Your export is a JSON file (a structured text file). It includes:

- Your profile, username, and avatar
- Your competition history from the last 12 months
- Your activity rings and streak data
- Your friends list and the direct messages you sent
- The achievements and badges you've earned

You can request up to three exports an hour. Friends in your export appear the way the app shows them to you, by first name and last initial.

The export is different from an [activity report](/engagement/export-activity-report), which is a spreadsheet of the dates and numbers you pick.

## Deleting your data

Deleting your account is permanent. It can't be undone, and signing up again starts from nothing.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription you pay for through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it there too. See [Manage your subscription](/subscriptions/managing).
</Warning>

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **Privacy**, tap **Privacy Settings**.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**.
4. Read what will be deleted.
5. Type **DELETE** in capital letters.
6. Tap **Delete account** (iPhone) or **Delete my account** (Android).

You can also delete your account on the web. Sign in at [MoveTogether account controls](https://movetogetherfitness.com/portal/account) and choose **Delete account**.

### What happens next

- You see **Deletion requested** and you're signed out on every device.
- A confirmation email is sent to you.
- Within 30 days, we delete or de-identify your personal data. That includes your profile, activity and health data, competitions, friends, blocked users, messages, and Coach Mo history.
- Your photos are deleted: profile photo, competition covers, and photos you sent in direct messages.
- We ask our providers to erase you too. That covers your chat data at Stream, your support chats in Chatwoot, your email contact records (Resend and Customer.io), and your subscription record at RevenueCat.
- If support filed a ticket for you, the ticket stays so we can still fix the problem. We replace its title and description with a deletion notice.

We may keep some records where the law requires it, or to settle disputes or prevent fraud. For example, Movecoins gift card records are kept for 3 years, without your email address or a link to your account. See section 5 of the [Privacy Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/privacy).

### Removing health data without deleting your account

You can't delete single days, workouts, or numbers while your account is active. Turning off a permission in Apple Health or Health Connect stops new reads, but it doesn't erase what MoveTogether already stored. To remove stored health data, delete your account.

### Unfinished accounts

If you create an account but never finish setting it up, and then don't open the app for 30 days, we delete the account. We email you at least 7 days before. Opening the app (and signing in, if asked) keeps it. We never delete an account this way if it has a paid plan or trial, any activity, friends, or competitions.

## Other privacy requests

For a privacy question or request, email [**privacy@movetogetherfitness.com**](mailto:privacy@movetogetherfitness.com) or use the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support). We may need to confirm it's you before we act.

## Something not right?

- **The export email didn't arrive:** check spam, wait a few minutes, then tap **Send another export**. If it still doesn't come, ask in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
- **Delete won't go through:** check that you typed **DELETE** in capitals. Deletion is limited to three tries an hour.
- **You can't sign in to delete:** contact us through [MoveTogether support](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact) or email privacy@movetogetherfitness.com.
