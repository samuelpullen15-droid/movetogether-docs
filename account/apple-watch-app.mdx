The MoveTogether Apple Watch app gives you useful pieces of the iPhone app on your wrist. Install it from the Watch app on your paired iPhone if it is not already available.

## Signing in

The Watch app signs in with your iPhone session, so there's no separate login. The first time you open it after installing, give it a few seconds to sync. If it shows a "needs pairing" screen, open the MoveTogether iPhone app once and the Watch should pick up the session.

## Home screen

The main view shows today at a glance:

- **Date header** at the top
- **Live event banner** (only when there's an active seasonal event)
- **Hero ring:** your Move ring, large
- **Three small ring tiles:** Move, Exercise, and Steps progress
- **Streak card:** your weekly streak, with a link to its detail
- **Today's challenges:** your daily challenges with progress
- **Friends preview:** where you stand against friends today
- **Competitions summary:** your active competitions and standings

Two toolbar buttons:

- **Microphone icon:** talk to Coach Mo (see below)
- **Sparkles icon:** opens seasonal events

## Talking to Coach Mo on Watch

Tap the microphone in the top-left of the Home screen.

You'll see the conversation go through a few states:

1. **Idle:** a greeting and a **Chat with Coach Mo** button
2. **Connecting:** a brief spinner
3. **Live:** the orb animates while Mo talks, with a red end-call button at the bottom
4. **Ended:** **Talk again** and **Close** buttons

Mo can answer questions, give you coaching, and react to what you tell it. The conversation transcript appears above the end-call button so you can scroll through what was said.

Coach Mo voice on Apple Watch requires Pro. If Watch voice is temporarily unavailable, the app explains the status and lets you try again when service is available.

For more on how Mo works, see [Coach Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo) and [What makes Coach Mo different](/social-coach/what-makes-mo-different).

## Streak detail

Tap the streak card on Home for the full breakdown:

- **Hero number:** your streak in qualifying weeks and the weeks to its next milestone
- **Last 30 days:** a grid of qualifying days; today is outlined

The day grid and the weekly streak are different measures. A qualifying day can contribute to the current week, while the streak count shown here is in weeks. Badge progress has its own rules and is not the same as the day grid or weekly streak count.

## Events

Tap the sparkles icon on Home to see all active, upcoming, and completed events. Tap an event for its full details: tagline, rewards, deadline countdown ("3d 4h left"), and community progress if there is one.

## Friends leaderboard

The top 3 are shown as a podium with gold, silver, and bronze accents. Ranks 4 and below scroll below the podium with your row highlighted so you can spot yourself quickly.

## Complications

MoveTogether ships an Apple Watch complication that puts your Move ring progress on your watch face.

**What it shows:** a gauge with your current Move-ring progress. The complication requests a refresh about every 15 minutes; watchOS schedules the actual updates.

**Adding it:**

1. Long-press your Apple Watch face.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Swipe to the complications screen.
4. Tap a complication slot, scroll to **MoveTogether**, tap to select.
5. Press the Digital Crown to save.

Designed for the **accessoryCircular** slot on modern Watch faces (Modular, Infograph, and similar).

## Requirements

- Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later
- MoveTogether iPhone app installed and signed in
- Apple Health permissions granted

## Background behavior

The Watch app can refresh when the watch has a connection, but watchOS controls background scheduling. Open the Watch app when you need the latest data. Coach Mo voice needs a live connection.

<Note>
  If the complication shows a dash or data looks stale, open the MoveTogether Watch app once to trigger a refresh, or check Apple Health permissions in **iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MoveTogether**.
</Note>
