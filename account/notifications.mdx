Go to **Settings → Notifications** in MoveTogether to turn each category on or off. System notification access is managed separately by your phone.

On iOS, MoveTogether can use provisional notification delivery before you choose full notification access. Android public beta follows Android's notification permission behavior; it does not use iOS provisional delivery.

## All notification categories

Notifications are grouped into sections. Some sections are only shown when their feature is available to your account. In-app switches, phone permission, Focus modes, and a muted Chat conversation are separate controls.

### Daily reminders

- **Good morning:** a morning nudge with your day ahead.
- **Good night:** an evening recap of the day.
- **Morning Brief:** opens the daily Coach Mo brief when one is ready. Set its time in Coach Mo settings under **Morning Brief** (5:00 AM to 10:00 AM). See [Morning Brief](/engagement/morning-brief).

### Competition

Updates about competitions you're in:

- Competition starting soon
- Competition ended with results
- Leaderboard position changed
- A new participant joined

### Lifecycle

Account-wide moments: invitations, important alerts, and reminders that don't fit elsewhere.

### Friends (Pro)

Activity from your friends list:

- A friend closed their rings
- A friend completed a workout
- A friend earned an achievement
- A friend won a competition

### Direct messages (Membership or Pro)

A friend sent you a direct message. Direct messages live in **Chat**.

### Group chat

A new message in a competition group chat you can access. Competition chat is open to participants on every plan. @mentions can also appear in Chat's notifications view. See [Chat access by channel](/social-coach/direct-messaging#chat-access-by-channel).

### MoveTogether Updates and mentions

Official **MoveTogether Updates** announcements open in **Chat**. Chat's notifications view collects @mentions across your conversations; there is not a separate in-app preference for mentions.

### Achievements (Pro)

You earned a new achievement or hit a streak milestone.

### Coach

- Proactive coaching nudges from Coach Mo
- Your Weekly Mo Report is ready
- Roast card delivery

### Account

- A friend request was received
- A competition invitation was received
- Subscription reminders

### Promotions

Occasional updates about new features, events, or offers.

## System notification settings

### iOS

Open **iPhone Settings → MoveTogether → Notifications**. Turn off **Allow Notifications** to silence MoveTogether at the system level, even when in-app categories are on.

If you use Do Not Disturb, Work, or Sleep Focus modes, they can silence MoveTogether notifications. Review your Focus settings to allow MoveTogether through.

On iOS, a confirmed rest-day or temporary Coach plan can change Coach reminder behavior without changing this system permission or the in-app category switches.

### Android public beta

Open MoveTogether's notification settings from the Android app-info/settings surface. Android can also expose MoveTogether notification channels by category.
