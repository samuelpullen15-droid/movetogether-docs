title: "Choose which notifications you get"
description: "Every switch on the MoveTogether Notifications screen, which plans see each one, and how your phone's own notification setting fits in."

The **Notifications** screen lets you turn each kind of MoveTogether notification on or off. Most switches are on every plan. A few appear only on Member or Pro, as noted below. The screen is the same on iPhone and Android.

## Open the Notifications screen

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **General**, tap **Notifications**.
3. Flip any switch. It saves right away. There's no Save button.

If a change can't be saved, the switch flips back and the app shows an error at the top. Try again.

## Your phone's notification setting comes first

The card at the top shows whether your phone lets MoveTogether notify you. If your phone has notifications turned off for MoveTogether, nothing arrives, whatever the switches below say.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    | The card says | What it means | What to do |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Notifications: On** | Notifications arrive normally | Nothing |
    | **Notifications: Quiet delivery** | Notifications go to Notification Center only, with no banner or sound | Tap **Enable notifications** |
    | **Notifications: Off** | iPhone is blocking every MoveTogether notification | Tap **Open iOS Settings** and turn on **Allow Notifications** |
    | **Enable notifications** | You haven't been asked yet | Tap **Enable notifications** and answer the prompt |

    Focus modes, such as Do Not Disturb, Work, or Sleep, can also silence MoveTogether. Check your Focus settings if notifications don't come through.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    | The card says | What it means | What to do |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Notifications: On** | Notifications arrive normally | Nothing |
    | **Notifications: Off** | Android is blocking MoveTogether notifications | Tap **Open notification settings** and turn them on |

    Android also lists MoveTogether's notification categories in your phone's own settings, such as **Competition updates** and **Friends and chat**. If one is off there, those notifications don't arrive.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Every switch, in order

| Section | Switch | What it covers | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily Reminders** | **Good morning**, **Good night**, **Stay-active nudges** | A daily nudge to open the app, and an afternoon nudge if you've been quiet during a competition | Everyone |
| **Mo Automations** | **Mo Automations**, then **Plan updates**, **Competition edge**, **Streak rescue**, **Recovery adjustments**, and **Quiet hours** | Mo watches for meaningful changes and sends one useful next move | Pro |
| **How much Mo swears** | **Clean**, **Classic**, or **Unhinged** | How Mo talks in notifications, chat, and voice | Everyone |
| **Competition Updates** | **Push notifications** | Standings, results, and invites for your active competitions | Everyone |
| **Competition Lifecycle** | **Lifecycle alerts**, **Position changes** | Start alerts, countdowns, last-call syncs, final results, and when your place changes | Everyone |
| **Leagues** | **Push notifications** | Promotion, demotion, and when someone passes you in your weekly league | Everyone |
| **Friend Activity** | **Push notifications** | Friends joining competitions, hitting milestones, or sending you requests | Everyone |
| **Cheers** | **Push notifications** | When someone cheers you on | Everyone |
| **Direct Messages** | **Push notifications** | When a friend sends you a direct message | Member and Pro |
| **Group Chat** | **Push notifications** | Messages in your competition group chats | Everyone |
| **Achievements & Milestones** | **Push notifications** | New badges and personal records | Everyone |
| **Weekly recap** | **Push and email** | A summary of your week. One switch controls both the push and the email | Everyone |
| **Coach Mo** | **Push notifications** | Tips and insights from Coach Mo, including the Morning Brief push | Pro |
| **Account & Security** | **Push notifications** | Updates about your account, subscription, security, and moderation appeals | Everyone |
| **Promotions & Events** | **Push notifications** | Seasonal events, limited drops, and announcements. Turning this off doesn't stop competition updates | Everyone |

A section you don't see is one your plan doesn't include. There are no separate email switches.

### Daily Reminders

MoveTogether sends one daily nudge. It arrives in the morning or evening slot, never both on the same day. Turning off either **Good morning** or **Good night** stops it.

**Stay-active nudges** is separate. It's an afternoon nudge for people in a competition who haven't been active.

### Mo Automations (Pro)

While it's off, you see a **Turn on Mo Automations** button. Turning it on asks for notification permission first if your phone hasn't given it.

Once it's on, you can turn each kind of update on or off, and set **Quiet hours** with a start and end hour. Mo doesn't send automations during quiet hours.

### How much Mo swears

This is the same setting as **Roast intensity** in Coach Mo settings. If you haven't picked one, Mo uses **Classic**, which swears. If you're under 18, only **Clean** is available and it shows **Available at 18+**. See [Roast mode](/social-coach/roast-mode).

### Morning Brief

Morning Brief isn't on this screen. Turn it on and set its time in **Settings** > **Customization** > **Coach Mo** > **Morning Brief**. The **Coach Mo** switch above also silences the Morning Brief push. See [Morning Brief](/engagement/morning-brief).

## Mentions and MoveTogether Updates

There's no switch for @mentions or for **MoveTogether Updates** announcements. Find your mentions in the **Mentions** filter in your chats. See [Use Chat, Updates, and mentions](/social-coach/chat-announcements-mentions).

## Something not right?

- **Nothing arrives at all:** check the card at the top of the Notifications screen first. Then check Focus (iPhone) or the app's notification settings (Android).
- **A section is missing:** your plan doesn't include it. Direct Messages needs Member or Pro. Mo Automations and Coach Mo need Pro.
- **A rest day or a temporary Coach plan** can change when Coach reminders arrive, without changing these switches.
- More fixes are in [Notification problems](/troubleshooting/notification-problems).
