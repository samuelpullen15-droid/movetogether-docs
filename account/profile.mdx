---
title: "Manage your profile"
description: "Open Profile from Home, change your photo, and edit your display name, username, and bio in Personal Details."
---

To open **Profile**, tap your photo at the top right of **Home**. Profile shows your stats, workout streak, today's activity, and badges. The gear at the top right opens **Settings**, and the share button shares your profile.

## Change your photo

Tap your photo on Profile and pick a new one from your library. Photos must follow the [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation) and are checked by automated moderation. If a photo is rejected, choose one without explicit, hateful, dangerous, or otherwise prohibited content.

## Name, username, and bio

Edit these in **Settings → Personal Details**. See [Personal Details](/account/account-details).

Your username is unique and is used for search and profile links. It must be 3 to 30 characters and can use letters, numbers, and underscores. You can change it once every 30 days, and your old handle is held for you for 30 days after a change.

Control who can see your profile in **Settings → Privacy Settings**.
