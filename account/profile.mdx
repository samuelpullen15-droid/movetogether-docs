Your profile is your page in MoveTogether. It shows your progress to you, and a public view of it to other members. Everyone can edit their profile on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

## Open your profile

Tap your photo at the top right of **Home**. On some iPhones, **Profile** is also a tab at the bottom of the screen.

At the top right of your profile:

- The share button shares a link to your public profile.
- The gear opens **Settings**.

## What your profile shows

From top to bottom:

| Section | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Header | Your photo, name, and @username, a **PRO** or **MEMBER** pill if you have a paid plan, and **View Public Profile** |
| Quick stats | Your day streak and wins, when you have them, and a Pro chip if you have Pro |
| **Your Stats** | **Total Points** and **Competitions** |
| **Workout Streak** | Your weekly streak card, the same one Home shows |
| **Today's Activity** | Your Move, Exercise, and Steps rings. Tap a ring row to change that goal |
| **Badges** | Your latest badges. Tap **See all** for every badge |
| **Invite & earn rewards** | Your invite link and progress. See [Invite friends and earn rewards](/account/referrals) |

The **PRO** or **MEMBER** pill also shows on your profile when other members view it.

## Change your profile photo

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open your profile.
    2. Tap your photo.
    3. Pick a photo from your library.
    4. Move and zoom it to fit the circle, then tap **Save**.

    You can also go to **Settings** > **Personal Details** and tap your photo at the top.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open your profile.
    2. Tap the camera button on your photo.
    3. Pick a photo, fit it to the circle, and save.

    You can also change it in **Settings** > **Personal Details**. To go back to no photo, tap **Remove photo** there.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Every photo is checked before it's saved. It has to follow the [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation).

- If the photo breaks the guidelines, the app says why and doesn't save it. Pick a different photo.
- If the check can't run right now, you see **We couldn't check this photo right now. Please try again in a minute.** Nothing is wrong with your photo. Try again shortly.
- A photo that was turned down before is turned down again if you try it a second time.

New accounts that don't get a photo from Apple or Google sign-in start with a drawn character picture. If you remove your photo, you get a drawn picture back.

## Name, username, and bio

Edit these in **Settings** > **Personal Details**. See [Edit your personal details](/account/account-details).

Your username is your unique handle. Friends use it to find you, and it's part of your profile link.

- 3 to 30 characters.
- Lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only. Capital letters you type become lowercase.
- Words on our blocked-word list aren't allowed.
- You can change it once every 30 days.
- After a change, your old username is held for you for 30 days, so nobody else can take it.

## How your name appears to others

Other members never see your full last name. They see your first name and the first letter of your last name, such as **Sam P.**

On leaderboards, you can show your username instead of your name. Turn off **Show real name on leaderboards** in [Privacy Settings](/account/privacy-settings).

## Who can see your profile

Choose **Public**, **Friends only**, or **Private** under **Profile visibility** in [Privacy Settings](/account/privacy-settings). That page explains what each choice shows.

## Something not right?

- **Photo won't save:** read the message the app shows. If it says the photo couldn't be checked, wait a minute and try again.
- **Can't change your username:** you may have changed it in the last 30 days. The username screen tells you the date you can change it again.
- **Still stuck:** ask in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
