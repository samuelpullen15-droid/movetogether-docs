Your profile is your page in MoveTogether. It shows your progress to you, and a public view of it to other members. Everyone can edit their profile on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

## Open your profile

Tap your photo at the top right of **Home**. On some iPhones, **Profile** is also a tab at the bottom of the screen.

At the top right of your profile:

- The share button shares a link to your public profile.
- The gear opens **Settings**.

Until you pick a background photo, your profile shows a default one. See [Add a background photo](#add-a-background-photo).

Your plan pill or chip also shows on your profile when other members view it.

## Change your profile photo

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open your profile and tap your photo.
    2. Choose one:
       - **Choose a character:** pick a drawn character and a color (**Peach**, **Mint**, **Sky**, **Lavender**, **Butter**, or **Pink**), then tap **Save**. Saving a character replaces your photo.
       - **Choose a photo:** pick a photo from your library, move and zoom it to fit the circle, then tap **Save**.

    You can also go to **Settings** > **Personal Details**. Tap your photo there for the same two choices, or tap **Choose a character** under it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open your profile and tap your photo.
    2. Tap **Choose a photo**.
    3. Pick a photo, fit it to the circle, and tap **Use photo**.

    To use a drawn character instead, go to **Settings** > **Personal Details** and tap **Choose a character**. Pick a character and a color (**Peach**, **Mint**, **Sky**, **Lavender**, **Butter**, or **Pink**), then tap **Save**. Saving a character replaces your photo. To go back to no photo, tap **Remove photo** there.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Every photo is checked before it's saved. It has to follow the [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation).

- If the photo breaks the guidelines, the app says why and doesn't save it. Pick a different photo.
- If the check can't run right now, you see **We couldn't check this photo right now. Please try again in a minute.** Nothing is wrong with your photo. Try again shortly.
- A photo that was turned down before is turned down again if you try it a second time.

New accounts that don't get a photo from Apple or Google sign-in start with a drawn character picture. If you remove your photo, you get a drawn picture back.

## Add a background photo

A background photo runs across the top of your profile, behind your photo. Friends see it when they open your profile. Until you pick one, your profile shows a default background. Anyone can pick one, on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

1. Open your profile and tap your photo.
2. Tap **Change background photo**.
3. Pick one of the ready-made backgrounds, or a photo from your library.
4. For your own photo, pinch to zoom and drag to position it, then tap **Save**.

To go back to the default, tap your photo, then **Remove background photo**. It shows only after you've picked a background.

Background photos are checked the same way as profile photos, and have to follow the [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation). If one is turned down, the app says why and doesn't save it.

## Set up your profile after updating

Profiles got a new look in version 2.2.0. The first time you open the app after updating from an older version, a **Set up your profile** screen opens once, after **What's New** if that opens too. It shows a preview of your profile as friends see it, and three rows:

- **Background photo:** add, change, or remove your background photo.
- **Profile picture:** tap it and choose **Choose a character** or **Choose a photo**.
- **Bio:** type a short bio, up to 160 characters.

Tap **Done** to save your bio and close the screen. A new photo, character, or background photo saves on its own when you tap **Save** on it. Close the screen with the X to leave without saving your bio.

The screen doesn't open on a new install, on an account less than a week old, or if you already have a background photo and a bio. Everything on it can be changed any time from your profile.

## Name, username, and bio

Edit these in **Settings** > **Personal Details**. See [Edit your personal details](/account/account-details).

Your username is your unique handle. Friends use it to find you, and it's part of your profile link.

- 3 to 30 characters.
- Lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only. Capital letters you type become lowercase.
- Words on our blocked-word list aren't allowed.
- You can change it once every 30 days.
- After a change, your old username is held for you for 30 days, so nobody else can take it.

## How your name appears to others

Other members never see your full last name. They see your first name and the first letter of your last name, such as **Sam P.**

On leaderboards, you can show your username instead of your name. Turn off **Show real name on leaderboards** in [Privacy Settings](/account/privacy-settings).

## Who can see your profile

Choose **Public**, **Friends only**, or **Private** under **Profile visibility** in [Privacy Settings](/account/privacy-settings). That page explains what each choice shows.

## Something not right?

- **Photo won't save:** read the message the app shows. If it says the photo couldn't be checked, wait a minute and try again.
- **Can't change your username:** you may have changed it in the last 30 days. The username screen tells you the date you can change it again.
- **Still stuck:** ask in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
