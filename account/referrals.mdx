---
title: "Invite & Earn"
description: "Share an invite link, track qualified referrals, earn the Ringleader badge and Pro time, and claim or bank referral rewards."
---

Find your Invite & Earn link and progress in **Rewards**: open **Settings** and tap the referral card near the top. On iPhone you can also tap the red **+** button and choose **Invite friends**. A friend receives 7 days of MoveTogether Pro when they join through your link. Their referral counts toward your rewards after they finish onboarding and log 3 days of real synced activity.

## Rewards

| Qualified referrals | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Ringleader badge |
| 3 | 1 week of Pro |
| 5 | 1 month of Pro |

Referral rewards qualify after a friend joins through your link, completes onboarding, and records 3 active synced days. Joining a competition is not required. The app shows progress and reward status; qualification is verified to protect the program from activity inconsistent with its intent.

## Claiming and banking Pro time

When a tier is ready, tap **Redeem** in **Rewards**. The first completed referral grants the Ringleader badge. A Pro-time reward is granted when no paid or free Pro entitlement is active. If one is active, the reward is banked and starts automatically after the current entitlement ends.

Rewards stack. At 5 completed referrals, you have unlocked the badge, 1 week of Pro, and 1 month of Pro.

## Tracking your progress

The Referrals screen shows:

- How many completed referrals you have
- Your progress toward the next tier
- Which rewards are ready to claim
- Which rewards you've already claimed

## When rewards arrive

Claim Pro-time rewards from Invite & Earn. A banked reward does not replace your current paid plan or free Pro period; the app shows it as saved for later.

## Tips

- Your link works in any messaging app.
- Friends who already have an account can't be credited as referrals; the link only counts new sign-ups.
- Your referral progress is private. Friends don't see who invited them in your view of the app.
- If the link is unavailable, wait for the screen to finish loading, then reopen Invite & Earn. Do not send a bare referral code: the full link carries the attribution.

<Note>
  An existing account cannot be credited as a new referral. If a new friend's progress does not update after onboarding and 3 real synced activity days, use the in-app support flow or [contact MoveTogether support](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact). Do not post account details in public feedback.
</Note>
