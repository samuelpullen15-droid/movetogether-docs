Invite friends with your own link. Each friend who joins through it gets a free month of Member. When your friends stay active, you earn a badge and free Pro time. Anyone can invite friends, on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

## Find your invite link

Open **Invite & earn rewards** in any of these places:

- **Settings:** the card near the top.
- **Your profile:** the card below **Badges**.
- **The + button:** if your tab bar has one, tap it, then **Invite friends**.

On iPhone the screen is called **Rewards**.

## Share your link

1. Open **Invite & earn rewards**.
2. Tap **Share your invite link**, or tap the link to copy it.
3. Send it in any messaging app.

Always send the full link. There's nowhere in the app to type a referral code, so a code on its own won't work.

## What your friend gets

A free month of **Member**, as soon as they sign up through your link.

The link has to open in the MoveTogether app to count. If your friend taps it before the app is installed, the link doesn't carry over. Your friend should:

1. Install MoveTogether.
2. Tap your link again on their phone. It opens the app.
3. Create their account. The app remembers your link through sign-up.

A friend who already signed up can still tap your link within 7 days of creating their account. The link counts only for new accounts. Someone who already had an account for more than 7 days, or was already invited by someone else, can't use it. Your friend and you also become friends in the app automatically.

Some gifts aren't given right away:

- **Held up to 14 days:** if our fraud checks flag either account, the gift waits for a review. If the flag is cleared in time, the gift goes through.
- **Not given:** if your friend has had a MoveTogether subscription or free trial before, or if you've already had 10 friends join in the last 30 days.

## What you earn

A friend counts toward your rewards once they finish setting up the app and then sync activity on 3 different days.

| Friends who count | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Ringleader** badge, added automatically |
| 3 | **1 Week of Pro** |
| 5 | **1 Month of Pro** |

Rewards stack. With 5 friends, you have the badge, a week of Pro, and a month of Pro. Each reward can be earned once.

### Claim free Pro time

1. Open **Invite & earn rewards**.
2. Under **Your Rewards**, tap **Redeem** next to the reward.

What happens next depends on your plan:

- **No Pro right now** (Free or Member): Pro starts right away.
- **Pro already active** (paid or free): the reward is **Saved** and starts by itself when your current Pro ends.

## Track your progress

The screen shows each friend you invited, how far along they are, and how close you are to the next reward.

Referrals are checked by our team. We may hold back rewards for activity that doesn't fit the program.

## Movecoins for invites

If you're in the United States and have Movecoins turned on, you can also earn Movecoins for each friend who's active for 3 days. The card on the invite screen shows how many coins you get per friend and how many referral rewards you have left this month.

Movecoins counts an active day more strictly than the referral rewards above, so a friend can count toward your badge and Pro time before their Movecoins arrive. A friend only earns you coins if their referral completes after you turned Movecoins on.

If Movecoins is off, the card shows **Turn on Movecoins**. See [How you earn Movecoins](/movecoins/earning).

## Something not right?

- **Your friend didn't get the free month:** check they opened your link in the app after installing it, within 7 days of signing up. If they've had a subscription or trial before, the gift doesn't apply.
- **A friend joined but doesn't count yet:** they need to finish setting up the app and sync activity on 3 different days after joining.
- **Redeem didn't work:** try again. If it still fails, ask in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
