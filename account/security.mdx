## Signing in

MoveTogether supports two sign-in methods:

**Sign in with Apple.** Uses your Apple ID for secure, passwordless authentication. Apple can also hide your email for extra privacy.

**Sign in with Google.** Uses your Google account for quick, secure sign-in.

## Keeping your account secure

- Keep your Apple or Google account secure; MoveTogether uses it to sign you in.
- Don't share your sign-in with anyone.
- Don't share sensitive information in the Coach Mo chat or competition group chats.

## Signing out

Open **Settings**, scroll to the end, and tap **Log out**.

## Switching devices

Install MoveTogether on your new phone, sign in with the same account, allow Apple Health or Health Connect access, and check your tracker in **Settings → Data Sources**. Your competition history, achievements, friends, and other account data sync automatically.

## If you think your account has been compromised

1. Sign out immediately.
2. Review your Apple ID or Google account security settings.
3. Use the in-app support flow or [MoveTogether support](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact) for private help with the account.
