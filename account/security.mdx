MoveTogether has no password of its own. You sign in with Apple or Google, on iPhone and Android, on every plan.

## How you sign in

- **Sign in with Apple:** uses your Apple Account. Apple can hide your real email from us with a relay address.
- **Sign in with Google:** uses your Google account.

Always sign in with the method you used when you created your account. **Settings** > **Personal Details** shows it under **Sign-in method**.

## Keep your account safe

- Protect your Apple or Google account. Whoever can get into it can get into MoveTogether.
- Don't share your sign-in with anyone.
- Don't post passwords, payment card details, or other secrets in Coach Mo, group chats, or the support chat.

## Log out

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Account** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Log out**, then confirm.

## Move to a new phone

1. Install MoveTogether on the new phone.
2. Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before.
3. Allow Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) when asked.
4. Check **Settings** > **Data Sources** to make sure your tracker is right.

Your competitions, badges, friends, and settings come back with your account. See [Connect your tracker](/getting-started/connect-tracker) if your activity doesn't show.

## If you think someone else is in your account

1. Log out of MoveTogether.
2. Check the security settings of your Apple or Google account, and change that password.
3. Tell us in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support). If you can't sign in, use the chat on [MoveTogether support](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact).
