MoveTogether uses AI for Coach Mo and for the first replies in the support chat. Each feature sends a provider only what it needs. This page explains what goes where. It applies on every plan, on iPhone and Android, unless noted.

## At a glance

| Feature | Provider | What it gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coach Mo text replies, insights, and reports | Anthropic (Claude) | Your messages and coaching context, with names swapped for placeholders and no MoveTogether user ID |
| Coach Mo on Apple Intelligence (Pro, iPhone, optional) | Apple Private Cloud Compute | Your message as typed, plus coaching context with names swapped for placeholders |
| Coach Mo voice | ElevenLabs | Your spoken audio, messages, coaching context, your first name, and your MoveTogether user ID |
| Support chat AI agent (Marco) | Skydive, with Anthropic's Claude writing replies | Your support messages and files, and the account details the support chat holds |

Anthropic and ElevenLabs process this data on our behalf and don't use it to train their own models. They may keep conversation data for a limited time for safety and debugging, then delete it. Health and fitness data is never used for advertising.

## Coach Mo text

Coach Mo uses your goals, recent activity, workouts, streaks, competitions, and chat messages to coach you. When **Personalize Coach with my fitness data** is on and the data is available, it can also use sleep and recovery, badges, weekly history, and rivalries.

Before anything goes to Anthropic:

| Type of info | What Anthropic sees |
| --- | --- |
| Names, yours and other people's | Placeholders such as `[USER_NAME]` or `[COMPETITOR_1]` |
| Your MoveTogether user ID | Not included |
| Your activity numbers (calories, steps, rings, minutes) | The real numbers |
| Your competition stats and standings | The real numbers |

The app swaps the placeholders back to names when it shows you Mo's reply. Mo uses the same names the leaderboard shows you. If a rival hides their real name, Mo uses their username, and someone you've blocked (or who blocked you) is just "another member."

## Coach Mo on Apple Intelligence (Pro, iPhone)

With Pro, on iOS 27 and an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, you can choose to have Coach Mo's normal replies written by Apple's model on Apple's Private Cloud Compute servers.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Customization** > **Coach Mo**.
3. Turn on **Use Apple Intelligence**.

It's off until you turn it on. The switch only appears when your plan and iPhone can use it.

When it's on:

- Your message goes to Apple exactly as you typed it, so any names you type are sent as written. Coaching context goes with names swapped for placeholders, and without your MoveTogether user ID.
- Apple says Private Cloud Compute uses the data only to answer and doesn't store it.
- Our servers still receive every message. We check it for safety, count it toward your plan, and save it in your Coach Mo history.
- Roast mode, rich cards, and questions about how to use MoveTogether still use Anthropic. So does any reply Apple can't give.

## Coach voice

Coach voice sends your spoken audio, relevant coaching context, your first name, and your MoveTogether user ID to ElevenLabs. Audio is used only to make a reply, then discarded. Your voice isn't used to build a voice profile or to identify you.

MoveTogether keeps transcripts of your voice conversations until you delete your Coach Mo history or your account. ElevenLabs keeps transcripts for up to 30 days for troubleshooting.

## Support chat

The in-app support chat runs on Chatwoot. Our AI support agent, Marco, may answer first. Marco runs on Skydive, and Anthropic's Claude writes its replies. A member of our team can step in at any time. Marco's replies are AI-generated and can be wrong, so ask for a person if something doesn't look right.

- When you chat while signed in, Chatwoot gets your user ID, email, first name, plan, time zone, sign-up date, and a code that proves the chat is yours.
- Screenshots and files you send go to Chatwoot and are read by the AI agent.
- Support conversations are shared in our team's Slack so we can follow up.
- If your problem needs our engineers, Marco can file a ticket in Linear. The ticket has a summary of your request and technical details about your app, device, syncing, and plan. We remove email addresses and phone numbers we detect, and we don't add your name, email, or user ID.

### Show Marco your screen (iPhone)

Marco may ask **Show Marco your screen?** in the support chat.

1. Tap **Show me**, or **Not now** to decline.
2. Go to the screen with the problem.
3. Tap **Show Marco**.

The app sends one picture of that screen, with your chats hidden. Marco reads it and answers in text. We don't keep the picture.

### Live screen sharing

If support asks to share your screen and you allow it, a live video of MoveTogether goes to them through Stream. Nothing is recorded. See [Share your screen with support](/troubleshooting/screen-share).

Don't send passwords, payment card details, or other secrets in Coach Mo, voice, or support chats. Only share health details with support when they're needed to fix the problem.

## Your controls

- **Turn off personalization:** turn off **Personalize Coach with my fitness data** in **Settings** > **Privacy Settings**. Coach Mo then replies without your activity, competitions, or goals.
- **Delete Coach Mo history:** delete single chats in Coach Mo, or tap **Delete all chats** in Coach Mo settings. Delete saved Files from the **Files** row in Coach Mo. See [Manage Coach Mo conversations](/social-coach/managing-conversations).
- **Turn off Apple Intelligence:** turn off **Use Apple Intelligence** in Coach Mo settings at any time.
- **Delete everything:** deleting your account removes your Coach Mo history and other data. See [Download or delete your data](/account/data-and-privacy).

Deleting a Coach Mo chat doesn't delete a separate support conversation. To ask for support data to be deleted, email privacy@movetogetherfitness.com.

For the full provider list and retention details, see [Download or delete your data](/account/data-and-privacy#who-helps-us-run-movetogether) and the [Privacy Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/privacy).
