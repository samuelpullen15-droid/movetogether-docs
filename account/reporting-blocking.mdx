title: "Block or report someone"
description: "How to block a person, report a person, chat message, or competition on iPhone and Android, and what happens after you do."
---

Blocking stops someone from reaching you. Reporting tells us about behavior that breaks the [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation). You can do both, on every plan.

## Block someone

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open the person's profile.
    2. Tap the **More** menu (the circle with three dots) at the top.
    3. Tap **Block**.
    4. Tap **Block** again to confirm.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open the person's profile.
    2. Tap the menu (three dots) at the top.
    3. Tap **Block**.
    4. Tap **Block** again to confirm.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### What blocking does

- It takes effect right away. The other person isn't told.
- It ends your friendship and cancels any pending friend request between you.
- It deletes your direct message conversation with them, for both of you.
- They can't see your profile or message you, and they can't add you back as a friend.

### Unblock someone

1. Open **Settings** > **Privacy Settings**.
2. Tap **Blocked users**.
3. Tap **Unblock** next to the person.

Unblocking doesn't make you friends again.

## Report a person

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open the person's profile.
    2. Tap the **More** menu (the circle with three dots).
    3. Tap **Report**.
    4. Under **What's the issue?**, pick a reason.
    5. Under **What happened?**, describe what happened. A short description is required.
    6. Tap **Submit report**.

    The reasons are **Inappropriate content**, **Harassment**, **Spam**, **Fake profile**, **Bullying**, **Hate speech**, **Violence**, **Impersonation**, **Explicit content**, **Misinformation**, and **Other**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open the person's profile.
    2. Tap the menu (three dots).
    3. Tap **Report**.
    4. Under **What's happening?**, pick a reason.
    5. Add details in **Anything else? (optional)** if you want.
    6. Tap **Send report**.

    The reasons are **Harassing me**, **Bullying**, **Hate speech**, **Explicit content**, **Inappropriate content**, **Violence or threats**, **Spam**, **Fake profile**, **Pretending to be someone**, **Misinformation**, and **Something else**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

To report a profile photo, pick **Inappropriate content** or **Explicit content** and say it's the photo.

## Report a chat message (iPhone)

1. Press and hold the message.
2. Tap **Report**.

You can't report your own messages.

## Report a competition (iPhone)

1. Open the competition.
2. Open its menu and tap **Report competition**.
3. Pick a reason, describe what happened, and tap **Submit report**.

The person who created the competition doesn't see this option.

You can also tell us about a problem in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).

## What happens after you report

- Your report is checked right away.
- A report on its own can lead to a warning at most. Anything more serious, like a suspension or ban, goes to a member of our team to decide.
- Reporting a chat message counts as a report, so the same rule applies.
- For privacy, we may not be able to tell you what action we took.

If you send many reports in a short time, the app asks you to try again later.

## Blocking or reporting?

| | Block | Report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who it protects | You | Everyone |
| What happens | The person can't reach you | We review what happened |

When someone is bothering you, do both. Block to stop it right away, and report so we can act for everyone.
