---
title: "Privacy Settings"
description: "Control profile, activity, health-metric, social, analytics, data-export, and advertising-tracking choices."
---

Open **Settings → Privacy Settings** to change the privacy choices available for your account.

## Controls

Available controls are grouped by what they affect:

- **Profile:** profile visibility, real name on leaderboards, discoverability by phone or email, and public leaderboard presence.
- **Activity and health metrics:** activity-feed sharing, detailed competition stats, and which metrics others can see.
- **Social:** who can send friend requests or competition invitations, plus blocked users.
- **Data:** the **Help improve MoveTogether** product-analytics choice, data export, and account deletion.

You can request advertising tracking from this screen. If you allow Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, Tenjin can process an IDFA, a Tenjin device identifier, and limited app, device, and network information for install and session attribution plus configured conversion events. Up to 20 eligible events can wait locally while the choice is undecided; nothing is transmitted before authorization. The queue discards events older than 7 days and clears on denial, restriction, revocation, or sign-out. Ads shown without Pro are contextual and non-personalized; Pro is ad-free. Health and fitness data is not used for advertising.

Choose who can see your profile:
- **Public:** anyone on MoveTogether
- **Friends Only:** only people on your friends list
- **Private:** only you

You can also turn off **Show real name on leaderboards** to display your username instead, and control whether others can find you by email.

## Activity sharing

Turn off **Show my activity in friends' feeds** to change whether your activity is shared in friends' feeds. You can also control whether your rank appears on public leaderboards and whether competitors can see your detailed stats or just your rank. These choices affect the related sharing surfaces; they do not remove information that has already been shared or records needed to operate a competition.

## Health data visibility

Control which fitness metrics competition participants can see: steps, calories burned, active minutes, distance, workouts. Each can be turned on or off independently.

## Social controls

- **Who can send friend requests:** Everyone, Friends of Friends, or No One.
- **Who can invite me to competitions:** Everyone, Friends Only, or No One.

You can also view and manage your blocked users list here.

## Analytics opt-out

Turn off **Help improve MoveTogether** to stop future opted-in product analytics from this setting. When enabled, analytics can include usage events, masked session replay, and diagnostics associated with your persistent account UUID. This data is not sold.
