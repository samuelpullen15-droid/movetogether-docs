**Privacy Settings** controls what other members can see about you and how MoveTogether uses your data. Every setting is on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

## Open Privacy Settings

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **Privacy**, tap **Privacy Settings**.
3. Change any setting. It saves right away. There's no Save button.

If a change can't be saved, the control goes back to how it was and the app shows an error. Try again.

## Profile

**Profile visibility** sets who can see your profile.

| Choice | Who sees what |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone on MoveTogether can see your profile and today's activity |
| **Friends only** | Anyone can see your name, photo, and username. Only friends see your activity |
| **Private** | Other members can't open your profile |

New accounts start on **Friends only**. Whatever you choose, people can still find you in search so they can send a friend request.

The other switches in this section:

- **Show real name on leaderboards:** on by default. Turn it off to show your username on leaderboards instead of your name.
- **Allow others to find me by phone:** lets people who have your number find you.
- **Allow others to find me by email:** lets people who know your email find you.
- **Show on public leaderboards:** turn it off to keep yourself off public leaderboards.

Other members never see your full last name. They see your first name and last initial, such as **Sam P.**

## Activity

- **Show my activity in friends' feeds:** turn it off to stop sharing your activity in friends' feeds.
- **Show detailed stats to competitors:** turn it off so competitors see your rank but not your detailed numbers.

These change what's shared from now on. They don't remove anything already shared, or the records needed to run a competition.

## Health metrics

Choose which numbers other people can see: **Calories**, **Exercise**, **Distance**, **Steps**, and **Workouts**. Each one has its own switch.

Hiding a number hides it on your profile and in feeds. Hidden numbers still count in your competitions.

## Social

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Who can send friend requests** | **Everyone**, **Friends of friends**, or **No one** |
| **Who can invite me to competitions** | **Everyone**, **Friends only**, or **No one** |

## AI Coach

**Personalize Coach with my fitness data:** when it's off, Coach Mo replies without using your activity, competitions, or goals. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data).

## Data & analytics

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Help improve MoveTogether** | Shares product analytics with us. When it's on, this can include usage events, masked session replay, and crash details tied to your account ID. Turn it off to stop from now on. We don't sell this data |
| **Blocked users** | See and unblock people you've blocked. See [Block or report someone](/account/reporting-blocking) |
| **Download my data** | Email yourself a link to a copy of your account data. See [Download your data](/account/data-and-privacy#downloading-your-data) |
| **Delete account** | The last row. Deletes your account for good. See [Delete your account](/account/data-and-privacy#deleting-your-data) |

Health and fitness data is never used for advertising. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, an **Ad Privacy Choices** row under **Privacy** in **Settings** lets you change your ad consent. On Android, the row is new in version 2.2.0. See [Ads and your privacy](/account/ads-and-measurement).

## Something not right?

- **Something you hid still shows in an old post:** these settings change what's shared from now on, not what was already shared.
- **People can't find you:** check that **Allow others to find me by phone** or **by email** is on, and that **Who can send friend requests** isn't **No one**.
- **Still stuck:** ask in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
