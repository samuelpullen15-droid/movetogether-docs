Ads appear only on the Free plan. Member and Pro have no ads. MoveTogether never uses your health or fitness data for advertising, and the app doesn't track you across other apps.

## Who sees ads

| Plan | Ads |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Small ads can appear on **Home**, **Compete**, and **Social** on iPhone |
| Member | None |
| Pro | None |

The ads are contextual. That means they're picked from basic app, device, and network information, not from your activity, your health data, or your browsing in other apps. Google's AdMob serves them.

## Ad consent in Europe and the UK

Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA and the UK, Google's consent form appears before any ad is shown. Your choice is saved, and the app doesn't request ads until you've answered.

To change your choice later:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **Privacy**, tap **Ad Privacy Choices**.
3. Update your choice in Google's form.

The row only appears where Google's consent rules apply, such as the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland. On Android, the row is new in version 2.2.0. Update the app if you don't see it yet.

## Watch an ad for a reward (Free plan, iPhone)

On the Free plan, you can choose to watch a short video ad for one of these:

| Where you see it | Button | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coach Mo, after your free messages are used | **Watch an ad for 1 message** | One more Coach Mo message. Up to 2 a day |
| The plan limit screen when you've reached your competition limit | **Watch an ad for 1 competition** | One extra competition spot, which frees up when that competition ends |

The reward is added after Google confirms you watched the whole ad. You'll see a short "Verifying" message while that happens. Member and Pro don't see these buttons.

## Tracking permission on older iPhone versions

MoveTogether 2.1.2 and later never ask for Apple's tracking permission and don't track you.

Older versions could ask for it. If you allowed it then, you can turn it off in iPhone **Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Tracking** > **MoveTogether**. Updating the app also stops it.
