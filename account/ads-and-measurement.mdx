Without Pro, you may see contextual, non-personalized ads. Pro is ad-free. MoveTogether does not use health or fitness data to personalize ads.

## Advertising tracking

During onboarding or from **Settings → Privacy Settings**, you may choose to request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. If you authorize it, Tenjin can process the IDFA, a Tenjin device identifier, and limited app, device, and network information for install and session attribution. It can also measure these configured conversion events: registration, onboarding completion, competition join, trial, and purchase.

Before authorization, eligible conversion events can remain only in a local queue of up to 20 events. Nothing is transmitted to Tenjin before ATT authorization. Events older than 7 days are discarded before transmission. The queue is cleared if tracking is denied, restricted, or revoked, and when you sign out. If you allow tracking, eligible queued events can be sent after Tenjin starts. You can change the permission later in iPhone Settings. Our [Privacy Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/privacy) has the complete disclosure.
