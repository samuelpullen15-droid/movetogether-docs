---

Open **Settings → Personal Details** to edit your account details. (To reach Settings, tap your photo at the top right of Home, then the gear.)

## What you can edit

**Avatar:** change your profile photo from your camera roll or the camera.

**Name:** your display name. Required.

**Username:** the unique handle friends use to find you. You can change it once every 30 days, and your previous username remains reserved for you for 30 days after a change.

**Bio:** up to 160 characters. The counter turns orange near the limit and red past it, and Save is disabled while you're over.

**Featured badge:** pick one of your earned badges to show on your profile.

**Sex:** Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.

**Birthday:** your age is calculated from it.

**Age:** enter it directly if your birthday isn't set.

**Phone number:** add, change, or remove it. The number is masked on screen. Long-press the row for a quick remove option.

## What you can see but not change

**Email:** the email on your account.

**Sign-in method:** Apple or Google.

## Saving changes

**Save** at the top right turns on once something has changed and required fields are valid.

## Account actions

**Log out** is in the Account section at the end of the main **Settings** screen. You confirm before you're signed out.

The first-run setup cannot currently be replayed as a single flow. Revisit data sources, notifications, privacy, and Coach Mo preferences from their individual settings instead.

**Delete account** is in the same Account section. Deleting an account is permanent and cannot be undone; see [Your data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy#deleting-your-data) first.

## Where this info shows up

- Your **display name** and **avatar** appear on leaderboards and in the activity feed (controllable via [Privacy settings](/account/privacy-settings))
- Your **username** lets friends find you
- Your **featured badge** shows on your profile under your name
- **Sex, birthday, and age** help personalize defaults (like your starting Move goal) and aren't shown to other users
- **Phone** is only used to help friends find you via contacts, if you opt in
