**Personal Details** holds the information on your account. Everyone can edit it, on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

## Open Personal Details

1. Tap your photo at the top right of **Home** to open your profile.
2. Tap the gear to open **Settings**.
3. Under **General**, tap **Personal Details**.

## What you can change

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Photo | Tap your photo at the top to pick a new one. On Android you can also tap **Remove photo**. See [Change your profile photo](/account/profile#change-your-profile-photo) |
| **Name** | Your display name. It can't be empty |
| **Username** | Opens its own screen. You can change it once every 30 days. See the [username rules](/account/profile#name-username-and-bio) |
| **Bio** | A short public bio, up to 160 characters. A longer bio won't save until you shorten it |
| **Featured badge** | The badge shown near the top of your public profile. Pick one you've earned, or **Automatic** |
| **Sex** | **Male**, **Female**, **Non-binary**, or **Prefer not to say**. Saves as soon as you pick |
| **Birthday** | On iPhone, tap to set it. On Android it's shown here but can't be changed |
| **Phone** | Tap **Add** to add and verify a number by text message, or tap a saved number to change it. To remove it, press and hold the row and tap **Remove phone** |

**Save** at the top right saves your name, bio, and featured badge. It turns on once you change one of them.

## What you can see but not change

- **Age:** worked out from your birthday.
- **Email:** the email on your account.
- **Sign-in method:** how you sign in, such as Apple or Google.

## Where this information shows up

- **Name and photo:** on leaderboards, in feeds, and in chat. Other members see your first name and last initial, never your full last name.
- **Username:** lets friends find you, and is part of your profile link.
- **Featured badge:** near the top of your public profile.
- **Sex, birthday, and age:** used to confirm you meet the minimum age and to tailor features such as Coach Mo. Other members don't see them.
- **Phone:** helps friends who have your number find you, if you allow it in [Privacy Settings](/account/privacy-settings).

## Log out

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Account** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Log out**, then confirm.

## Delete your account

**Delete account** is the last row of **Settings** > **Privacy Settings**. Deleting is permanent. Read [Delete your account](/account/data-and-privacy#deleting-your-data) first so you know what's removed.

## Something not right?

- **Save is grayed out:** it only turns on after you change your name, bio, or featured badge.
- **Bio won't save:** it's over 160 characters. Shorten it and tap **Save** again.
- **Can't change your username yet:** you changed it in the last 30 days. The username screen shows the date you can change it again.
- **Still stuck:** ask in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
