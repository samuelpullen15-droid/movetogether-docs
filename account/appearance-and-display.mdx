---
title: "Change light or dark mode and units"
description: "Switch between System, Light, and Dark appearance, choose metric or imperial units, and find your phone's text size settings."
---

You can choose how MoveTogether looks and which units it uses. These settings are on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

## Light or dark mode

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **General**, tap **Appearance**.
3. Pick one:
   - **System:** matches your phone's light or dark setting.
   - **Light:** always light mode.
   - **Dark:** always dark mode.

Your choice stays after you close the app.

## Metric or imperial units

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **General**, tap **Customization**.
3. Tap **Unit of Measurement**.
4. Pick one:
   - **Metric:** kilometers, kilograms, and centimeters.
   - **Imperial:** miles, pounds, and feet and inches.

This changes how distance, weight, and height show across the app. Steps and minutes have no units, so they stay the same.

Until you pick, MoveTogether follows your phone's region settings.

## Text size and accessibility

MoveTogether follows your phone's text size and accessibility settings.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    - **Text size:** iPhone **Settings** > **Display & Brightness** > **Text Size**.
    - **Reduce Motion:** iPhone **Settings** > **Accessibility** > **Motion**.
    - **Reduce Transparency** and **Increase Contrast:** iPhone **Settings** > **Accessibility** > **Display & Text Size**.

    See [Apple's accessibility guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-accessibility-features-iph3e2e4367/ios) for the full list.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Change font size, display size, and other options in your phone's **Settings** > **Accessibility**. The exact names depend on your phone's maker.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
