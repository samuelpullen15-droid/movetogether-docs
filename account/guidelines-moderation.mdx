## Our community standards

MoveTogether is a supportive fitness community. We ask all users to follow our Community Guidelines:

- **Be respectful.** Treat everyone with kindness. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or discrimination.
- **Keep it appropriate.** All content has to be suitable for all ages.
- **Play fair.** No cheating or manipulating fitness data.
- **Prioritize health.** Don't promote dangerous fitness practices.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't share others' personal information without consent.
- **No spam.** No advertising, phishing, or solicitation.

## How AI moderation works

AI monitors all user-generated content in the app in real time, including competition group chat messages, direct messages (image moderation), profile photos, and usernames. The AI flags content that includes harassment, hate speech, bullying, explicit or sexual content, threats, spam, or any other violations of our Community Guidelines. Photos that violate guidelines are removed automatically.

## Enforcement actions

We use a combination of automated (AI) systems and human review. Consequences escalate with the severity and history of the violation:

- **Content removal.** The specific content is removed, usually before anyone else sees it.
- **Warning.** A first violation generally results in a warning logged to your account.
- **Temporary suspension.** Continued violations restrict access for a period, and competition scores are frozen.
- **Permanent ban.** Repeated or severe violations result in a permanent, non-reversible ban.

The most serious violations (content that sexualizes or endangers minors, credible threats of violence, terrorism, or non-consensual intimate images) result in an **immediate permanent ban** and may be reported to law enforcement.

## Seeing your account standing

When we take action on your account, we tell you what happened and why. Open **Settings → Account Status** in the app to see your current standing (good standing, warning, suspension, or ban), the specific reason for any action, and to start a review request at any time.

## Appealing a decision

If you think a moderation action was taken in error, you can ask us to review it:

- **In the app:** go to **Settings → Account Status** and tap **Request a review**.
- **With Marco:** chat with Marco at [movetogetherfitness.com](https://movetogetherfitness.com), tell Marco it's a moderation appeal, and explain why you think the decision was incorrect.

Appeals are reviewed independently of the original decision. Clear cases are resolved quickly and can be reversed right away; decisions to uphold a suspension or ban, and anything our systems can't confidently resolve, are reviewed by a member of our team. We aim to respond within **72 hours**, and you can request a review for up to **6 months** after an action. A suspension remains in effect during an appeal unless we decide otherwise.

Read the full Community Guidelines at [movetogetherfitness.com/community-guidelines](https://movetogetherfitness.com/community-guidelines).
