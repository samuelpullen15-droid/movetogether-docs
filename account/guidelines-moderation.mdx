MoveTogether is a supportive fitness community. These rules apply to everyone, on every plan. This page sums them up and explains what happens when they're broken. The full text is in the [Community Guidelines](https://movetogetherfitness.com/community-guidelines).

## The rules in short

- **Be respectful.** No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or discrimination.
- **Keep it appropriate.** Photos, names, posts, and messages must suit a general audience.
- **Play fair.** No faking, inflating, or manipulating fitness data.
- **Put health first.** Don't promote dangerous fitness or eating practices.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't share other people's personal information without their consent.
- **No spam.** No ads, scams, phishing, or harmful links.

If you think someone is cheating, report it in the app instead of accusing them in public. See [Block or report someone](/account/reporting-blocking).

## How content is checked

| What | How it's checked |
| --- | --- |
| Profile photos and competition covers | Checked before they're saved. A photo that breaks the rules isn't saved, and the app tells you why |
| Chat messages | Checked as they're sent. A message that breaks the rules is blocked or removed |
| Photos in direct messages | Checked automatically |
| Usernames | Checked against a list of blocked words |
| Reports from members | Checked right away. See [What happens after you report](/account/reporting-blocking#what-happens-after-you-report) |

## What can happen to an account

Consequences grow with how serious a violation is and how many there have been recently.

| Action | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Content removal** | The photo or message is removed, often before anyone sees it |
| **Warning** | A note on your account. More violations may lead to a suspension |
| **Suspension** | Some features are paused until the date shown in **Account Status** |
| **Permanent ban** | The account is closed for good |

A report from another member can lead to a warning at most. A suspension or ban needs automated detection of the content itself or a decision by our team.

The most serious violations lead to an immediate permanent ban with no appeal. These include content that sexualizes or endangers minors, credible threats of violence, terrorism, and sharing intimate images without consent. We may report them to law enforcement.

### While you're suspended

You can still open the app, sync your activity, and read what's there. You can't:

- Comment or react in feeds and competitions
- Send nudges, taunts, or cheers
- Start a head-to-head challenge with someone
- Send chat messages, direct messages, or competition invites
- Send or accept friend requests

Your competition scores are frozen. Activity during the suspension doesn't count toward your competitions.

## Check your account standing

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **Privacy**, tap **Account Status**.

You see your standing (**Good standing**, **Warning on file**, **Account suspended**, or **Account banned**), when access returns if you're suspended, and a **History** of every action with its reason. An action that was reversed shows **Overturned**.

## Appeal a decision

You can appeal a warning, suspension, or ban for up to 6 months after it happened. Actions you can appeal show **Appeal this**. The zero-tolerance bans above can't be appealed.

1. Open **Settings** > **Account Status**.
2. Find the action under **History** and tap **Appeal this**.
3. Explain in your own words why you think it was a mistake.
4. Tap **Submit for review**.

On iPhone you can also tap **Request a review**, which opens the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support). On any device, you can say in the support chat that it's a moderation appeal, what action was taken, and why you think it's wrong.

### What happens next

- Every decision to lift a warning, suspension, or ban is made by a person on our team.
- We aim to reply within 72 hours, and no later than 7 business days.
- You get a notification and an email with the decision.
- A suspension stays in place while we review your appeal, unless we decide otherwise.
- An appeal only covers the action you appealed. If another suspension or ban is still active, it stays until it ends or is appealed too.

## Something not right?

- **You can't find Account Status:** it's in **Settings** under **Privacy**.
- **A photo keeps getting turned down:** read the reason the app gives, and try a different photo. If the app says the photo couldn't be checked, wait a minute and try again.
- **You didn't hear back about an appeal:** ask in the [in-app support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
