title: "Turn on roast mode and control Mo's swearing"
description: "Turn Coach Mo's roast mode on or off, choose how much Mo swears, and what happens when you tell Mo it went too far."
---

Roast mode switches Coach Mo from supportive to sarcastic. Mo uses your real numbers as the setup, and the joke is about your effort, not your body or who you are. Roast mode is on every plan, on iPhone and Android.

Three separate controls shape how Mo sounds:

| Control | What it sets | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Personality** | Mo's voice: Classic Mo, Drill Sergeant, Hype Man, Big Sibling, or Sports Analyst | Coach Mo settings |
| **Roast mode** | Whether Mo roasts you in chat | The flame button, or Coach Mo settings |
| **Roast intensity** | How much Mo swears: Clean, Classic, or Unhinged | Coach Mo settings, or **Settings** > **Notifications** |

## Turn roast mode on or off

Any of these works:

- **The flame button.** In Coach Mo, tap the flame button in the message bar (on Android it reads **Roast** and fills in while roast mode is on). Tap it again to turn roast mode off.
- **The settings switch.** Open [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings) and turn **Roast mode** on or off. It stays that way across conversations.
- **Your first visit.** The first time you open Coach Mo, it asks **How should Mo talk to you?** Pick **Roast me** to start in roast mode, or **Supportive** for normal coaching.

A fresh conversation also offers a **Roast me** suggestion. Tapping it types a roast request into the message field, such as "Roast me on this week's effort. Don't pull punches." You can edit it before sending. It's a one-time request and doesn't turn roast mode on.

## Choose how much Mo swears

| Intensity | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Clean** | Roasts stay PG-13. No swearing, still savage. |
| **Classic** | Swears like a friend who actually swears. |
| **Unhinged** | No filter. Mo goes for the jugular. |

1. Open [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings).
2. Under **Roast intensity**, tap a level.

You can also change it in **Settings** > **Notifications** under **How much Mo swears**, on every plan. The two settings always match.

Rules:

- If you never pick a level, Mo uses **Classic**.
- The first time you turn on the **Roast mode** switch without having picked a level, Mo asks **How hard should Mo roast?**
- Intensity applies to roasts in chat, Mo's notifications, and Apple Watch voice. Changing it doesn't turn roast mode on.
- Accounts known to be under 18 can only use **Clean**. The other levels show **Available at 18+**.

## If Mo goes too far

Tell Mo. If you say something like "that's harsh" or "stop swearing," Mo switches your intensity to **Clean**, says so in its reply, and stops swearing in chat and in its notifications. To switch back, open Coach Mo settings and pick another level under **Roast intensity**.

## Roasts by voice

On Apple Watch voice (Pro), Mo follows your intensity setting but roasts you only when you ask, for example "roast me." See [Talk to Coach Mo by voice](/social-coach/voice).

## What changes in roast mode

- The tone turns cutting instead of supportive.
- Mo uses your actual numbers, like steps, calories, and missed days, as setups.
- Your style stays. A Hype Man roast is still over the top.

These are the sample lines the style picker shows for 847 steps. They aren't promised replies.

**Classic Mo (roast):**

> not you sitting at 847 steps like that's a flex. my grandma's fitbit is judging you rn

**Sports Analyst (roast):**

> Let me put this in perspective. 847 steps puts you in the bottom 3% of all MoveTogether users this week. Historically bad.

## Mix and match

- **Drill Sergeant, roast mode off**: tough but encouraging.
- **Classic Mo, roast mode on**: friendly voice, sarcastic edge.
- **Drill Sergeant, roast mode on**: the most intense mix. Pick **Clean** if you want the directness without the swearing.

Styles other than Classic Mo need Pro. On the Free plan and Membership, roasts come out of your 5 lifetime Coach Mo messages like any other coaching message. See [Coach Mo access](/social-coach/coach-mo#coach-mo-access).

## Something not right?

- **Mo still swears.** Check **Roast intensity** in Coach Mo settings. If it already says **Clean**, contact the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
- **The flame button is gone or roast mode won't stay on.** Check the **Roast mode** switch in Coach Mo settings.
- **Something Mo said crossed a line.** Tell Mo, and report it through the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
