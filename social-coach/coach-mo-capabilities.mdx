Coach Mo is an AI coach that chats with you about your fitness. This page covers what it can do, what stays in your hands, and how to get the most out of it. It applies to iPhone and Android on every plan, except where a feature says Pro.

## What Coach Mo can do

**Chat in your style.** Ask about your activity, motivation, or a rough week. Mo answers in the coaching style you picked: Classic Mo (the default), Drill Sergeant, Hype Man, Big Sibling, or Sports Analyst. Styles other than Classic Mo need Pro.

**Use your activity.** When **Personalize Coach with my fitness data** is on (**Settings** > **Privacy Settings** > **AI Coach**), Mo can use your activity and history, workouts, sleep, goals, streaks, badges, competitions, and leagues.

**Remember you (Pro).** With Pro, Mo keeps notes about your goals, habits, preferences, and wins in **Files**, its long-term memory, and uses them in later conversations. You can view and edit them. On other plans and during a free trial, Mo doesn't keep long-term notes. See [Managing Coach Mo's memory](/social-coach/managing-coach-mo-memory).

**Roast you on request.** Turn on roast mode for a sarcastic take on your week. If you tell Mo it's too harsh (for example "that's harsh" or "stop swearing"), Mo switches your roast intensity to **Clean** and says so. See [Roast mode](/social-coach/roast-mode).

**Suggest a temporary plan.** Traveling, sick, or busy? Mo can suggest a plan that changes its reminders for a while. Nothing changes until you tap **Confirm**. See [Manage Coach plans and rest days](/social-coach/coach-plans-rest-days).

**Answer app questions for free.** Questions about how MoveTogether works are answered from this Help Center and don't use one of your 5 lifetime messages. See [Product help, coaching, and support](/social-coach/coach-mo#product-help-coaching-and-support).

**Pass on your ideas.** Suggest a feature and Mo shows an **Idea** card. Tap **Send to the team** to send it without your name.

**Write your weekly recap (Pro).** Mo writes a Weekly Mo Report about your week. Open it from the **Weekly recap** tile on **Home**. See [Weekly Mo Report](/engagement/weekly-report).

## What Coach Mo can't do

| Mo can't | What to do instead |
| --- | --- |
| Create a competition | The guided builder can prepare one from your answers, but you review and create it. See [Build a competition with Mo](/getting-started/build-with-mo). |
| Send messages or invite friends | Do it yourself from **Social**. |
| Change your goals | Change them yourself. See [Setting goals](/getting-started/setting-goals). |
| Change most settings | The one exception: Mo switches roast intensity to **Clean** if you complain about its tone. Switch it back in Coach Mo settings. |
| Fix account, billing, or sync problems | Mo hands you to the support chat. See [Getting help from support through Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo#getting-help-from-support-through-mo). |
| Give medical advice | Talk to a doctor. Mo doesn't diagnose or prescribe. |
| See or change other people's accounts | Each person's Mo and Files are separate. |

Mo can suggest a workout for today, but it isn't a training program builder. It doesn't plan weeks of sets, reps, and progressions.

## Get better answers

- **Be specific.** "How should I pace my Move ring today? I'm behind" works better than "What should I do for fitness?"
- **Tell Mo what matters to you.** On Pro, goals, barriers, and what motivates you end up in Files, and Mo uses them later.
- **Ask Mo to check its notes (Pro).** "What did I tell you about my knee?" makes Mo search Files.
- **Check the trace.** Each reply shows what Mo looked up. See [Reading Coach Mo's thinking](/social-coach/reading-activity-trace).

## If Mo gets something wrong

1. Tell Mo in the same conversation, for example "That's not right, I walked yesterday."
2. Open the trace on the reply to see what Mo read.
3. If a saved note is wrong, fix or delete it in [Files](/social-coach/managing-coach-mo-memory) (Pro).

For a bug or a problem with your account, billing, or syncing, use the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support). For a product idea, go to **Settings** > **Got an idea? Tell us**. For privacy or data requests, you can also email [privacy@movetogetherfitness.com](mailto:privacy@movetogetherfitness.com).

## Privacy

For Coach Mo text, Anthropic receives the fitness context and messages needed to answer. Names are replaced with placeholders, and your MoveTogether user ID is left out. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data) for every provider.

<Note>
  Coach Mo is an AI assistant, not a certified trainer or a medical professional. It gives general fitness information only. Talk to a doctor before starting a new fitness program, especially if you have health concerns.
</Note>
