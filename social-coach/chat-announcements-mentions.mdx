---

All your chats live in one place: the **Chats** page of the **Social** tab. It's laid out the same way on iPhone and Android.

## Open Chats

1. Tap **Social** in the tab bar.
2. Tap **Chats** at the top, or swipe left from **Feed**.

From top to bottom you'll see:

1. The **MoveTogether Updates** card, for official announcements.
2. **Your Chats**: your direct messages, competition chats, and joined communities in one list, newest first. Above the list, pick **All** for every chat or **Mentions** for messages where someone @mentioned you. The pencil next to **Your Chats** starts a new direct message.
3. **Explore Communities**, for communities you haven't joined yet.

## Find the right conversation

| Looking for | Where |
| --- | --- |
| A direct message, competition chat, or joined community | **Your Chats** |
| A community you haven't joined | **Explore Communities**. Tap a card to preview it. The preview is read-only until you join. See [Communities](/social-coach/communities). |
| Official news | The **MoveTogether Updates** card at the top |
| Messages that @mention you | **Mentions**, above **Your Chats** |

Tapping a mention opens the conversation it came from. Mentions come from your direct messages, communities, and competition chats.

## MoveTogether Updates

MoveTogether Updates is the official announcements channel. Everyone can read it and react to posts. You can't reply. When there are posts you haven't read, the card shows how many.

## Unread counts

- Each chat in **Your Chats** shows its own unread badge.
- The **Mentions** filter shows how many unread mentions you have.
- On iPhone, the **Social** tab icon shows your number of pending friend requests, or a dot when you have unread chats and no requests.

Whether your phone also alerts you depends on your phone's notification permission and your choices in [Notification settings](/account/notifications).

## Start a direct message

Tap the pencil next to **Your Chats**, or tap the red **\+** button in the tab bar and choose **New message**. Starting a direct message needs Membership or Pro. See [Send a direct message](/social-coach/direct-messaging).

For who can use each kind of chat, see [Chat access by channel](/social-coach/direct-messaging#chat-access-by-channel).

## Something not right?

- **A chat is missing from Your Chats.** Make sure **All** is selected, not **Mentions**. A competition chat appears only while you're a participant and the competition is active or upcoming.
- **No notification for a new message.** See [Notification problems](/troubleshooting/notification-problems).
- **Your Chats won't load.** Check your connection and try again. If it keeps failing, use the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
