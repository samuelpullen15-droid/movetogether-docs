## The Social tab

The **Social** tab holds your friends list, suggestions, contacts, and activity. The **Moving now** rail at the top shows friends' ring progress. The **Friend activity** feed shows highlights such as personal records, streaks, and competition wins; tap **See all activity** for more. Direct messages live in **Chat**.

## Your friends

Your friends appear as profile circles. A green dot means they're active in the app now. Tap a friend to view their profile. See [finding friends](/getting-started/finding-friends) to add more.

## Today's leaderboard

**Today's Leaderboard** ranks you and your friends on today's activity, with each score, rings closed, and your row highlighted.

## Activity feed

The activity feed highlights notable progress from your friends, such as competition wins, personal records, and streak milestones. Everyday ring progress appears in **Moving now**. Refresh after a friend syncs if an expected update has not appeared.

## Reacting to activities

Cheer a visible activity with its reaction buttons: **Like**, **Fire**, **Strong**, or **Clap**. Each kind toggles independently, so you can add more than one kind to the same activity; tap a selected reaction again to remove that kind.

Comments and comment reactions need Membership or Pro. On the Free plan you can react to activities in the preview.

## Who sees your activities?

Only your friends can see your activities in their feed. In **Settings → Privacy Settings**, find the **Activity** section and turn off **Show my activity in friends' feeds** to hide your activities.
