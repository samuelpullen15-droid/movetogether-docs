The **Social** tab is where you keep up with friends. It has two pages, **Feed** and **Chats**, on both iPhone and Android. This page covers **Feed**. For **Chats**, see [Find your chats, Updates, and mentions](/social-coach/chat-announcements-mentions).

Adding friends is on every plan. The full activity feed and comments need Membership or Pro. On the Free plan you see a 2-item preview of the feed and can still react to those items.

## Open the Feed

1. Tap **Social** in the tab bar.
2. Tap **Feed** at the top, or swipe right from **Chats**.

The app remembers which page you used last. From top to bottom, **Feed** shows:

1. A search box (**Search by username…**) to find people.
2. **Friend requests** and **Requests sent**, when you have any.
3. **Moving now**: today's rings for your friends.
4. **Friend activity**: your friends' highlights.
5. **Find from contacts** and **People you may know**.

**Today's Leaderboard** is on the **Compete** tab, not on Social.

## Answer friend requests

Requests sent to you appear under **Friend requests**. Tap **Accept** to add the person, or decline. Requests you've sent appear under **Requests sent** until the other person answers, and you can cancel them there. To add more people, see [Find and add friends](/getting-started/finding-friends).

## Moving now

The **Moving now** rail shows each friend's three rings for today, at their real progress. If you're in a league, it also shows people in your league group, with their rank. A friend's circle shows when they were last active. Tap **All friends** for your full list, or tap anyone to open their profile.

## Friend activity

**Friend activity** shows your friends' bigger moments, such as:

| Highlight | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Personal record | New step record |
| Streak milestone | 10 week streak |
| Competition win | Won a competition |
| Joined a competition | Joined a friend's competition |
| Event finished | Completed a seasonal event |
| Badge earned | Earned a badge |
| New friend | Now friends with someone |

Everyday ring closes and workouts aren't listed here. They show in **Moving now** instead. On a quiet day this section says **Nothing big yet today**. When there are more than five items, tap **See all activity**.

## React and comment

Tap a reaction under an activity: **Like**, **Fire**, **Strong**, or **Clap**. Each one is its own toggle, so you can add more than one to the same activity. Tap a reaction again to remove it.

To comment, tap the comment button under the activity. Comments need Membership or Pro. On the Free plan, opening comments shows an upgrade screen.

## Who sees your activity

Only your friends see your activity in their feed. To stop sharing it, go to **Settings** > **Privacy Settings** and, under **Activity**, turn off **Show my activity in friends' feeds**. See [Privacy settings](/account/privacy-settings).

## Something not right?

- **The feed didn't load.** Pull down to try again.
- **A friend's highlight is missing.** Their activity has to sync first. Pull down to refresh after a few minutes. If they turned off **Show my activity in friends' feeds**, you won't see it.
- **You only see two items.** That's the Free plan preview. Membership or Pro shows the full feed.
- **Still stuck?** See [Sync issues](/troubleshooting/sync-issues) or use the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
