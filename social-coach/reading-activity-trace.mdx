Each Coach Mo reply starts with a short list of the steps Mo took before answering, such as reading your recent activity or searching its notes. This list is called the activity trace. It lets you check where an answer came from. It's on iPhone and Android, on every plan.

## Where to find it

At the top of each reply, above the text, there's a header like:

> Prepared in 4 seconds · 3 steps

Tap the header to open or close the list of steps. Tap a step to see more detail, such as which notes Mo read or which days of activity it looked at.

While Mo is still working, the header reads **Preparing your response...** until the first step arrives. Then it counts the steps as they come in.

## What the steps say

| Step line | What Mo did |
| --- | --- |
| A short description, such as "Preparing response" | Thought through how to answer |
| Read 7-day activity | Looked at your real activity for that many days |
| Searched for "running" | Searched its notes in Files for that word (Pro) |
| Read Goals.md | Opened one of your Files documents (Pro) |
| Logged to Wins.md | Added a new note to Files (Pro) |
| Updated Habits.md | Changed a note in Files (Pro) |
| Archived from Goals.md | Removed an outdated note from Files (Pro) |

The words in quotes and the document names change with each reply.

## Use it to check an answer

If Mo says "You hit your Move ring on weekdays but drop off on Saturdays," open the trace. If you see a **Read 7-day activity** step, Mo looked at your real numbers. If there's no activity step, Mo answered without checking.

When Mo gets something wrong, the trace often shows why, such as reading an old note. Fix or delete that note in [Files](/social-coach/managing-coach-mo-memory), then tell Mo in the chat.

## Keep the steps open or closed

**Keep thinking expanded** is on by default, so the steps stay open. To have them fold away after each reply, open [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings) and turn **Keep thinking expanded** off. You can still tap any header to open it.

## What the trace doesn't show

The trace lists the steps Mo took. It doesn't show all of Mo's internal reasoning. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data) for how your information is used.
