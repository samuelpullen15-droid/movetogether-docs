Coach Mo is an AI coach built into MoveTogether. This page explains, in plain terms, what Mo uses when it answers you. For the full list of what Mo can and can't do, see [What Coach Mo can and can't do](/social-coach/coach-mo-capabilities).

## Your activity

When **Personalize Coach with my fitness data** is on (**Settings** > **Privacy Settings** > **AI Coach**), Mo reads your activity, goals, streaks, and competitions before answering. Ask "How did I do last week?" and Mo looks at your real numbers. With it off, Mo answers as a general fitness coach.

## Notes in Files (Pro)

With Pro, Mo keeps short notes about your goals, habits, wins, and what has and hasn't worked, so you don't have to start over each time. You can view, edit, or delete them. See [Manage what Coach Mo remembers](/social-coach/managing-coach-mo-memory).

## Five coaching styles

Classic Mo (the default), Drill Sergeant, Hype Man, Big Sibling, and Sports Analyst give the same advice in different voices. Styles other than Classic Mo need Pro. See [Choose a coaching style](/social-coach/coach-mo#choose-a-coaching-style).

## A visible list of what Mo looked up

Each reply starts with the steps Mo took, such as reading your recent activity. See [See what Coach Mo looked up](/social-coach/reading-activity-trace).

## Privacy

Before Coach Mo text reaches Anthropic, names are replaced with placeholders and your MoveTogether user ID is left out. On iPhone, Pro members can run normal replies on Apple Intelligence instead. See [Use Apple Intelligence](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings#use-apple-intelligence) and [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data).

## Mo can be wrong

Mo is an AI assistant, not a doctor or a certified trainer. It can get things wrong, especially on topics far from your own data. Tell Mo when it does, and it can correct itself in the same conversation.
