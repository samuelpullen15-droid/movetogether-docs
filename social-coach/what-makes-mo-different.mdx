Coach Mo is built for fitness coaching with your own data, not a general chatbot told to act like a coach.

## Built around your data

When personalization is on and data is available, Mo can use your activity, competition history, and goals. Ask "how did I do last week?" and Mo looks at your actual numbers. Ask about pacing a weekend competition and Mo can factor in your competitors' averages.

A generic chatbot says "try to walk more." Mo can say "you hit your Move ring on weekdays but drop off on Saturdays. Want a Saturday challenge?"

## Memory that lasts

Mo's long-term memory, **Files**, keeps your goals, habits, wins, and what has and hasn't worked, so you don't have to reintroduce yourself. View, edit, or delete Files any time. See [managing Coach Mo's memory](/social-coach/managing-coach-mo-memory).

## Privacy

Before Coach text prompts reach Anthropic, direct names are replaced with placeholders, and your MoveTogether user ID is left out. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data) and the [Privacy Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/privacy).

## Mo shows its work

The **activity trace** under a reply shows lookups such as competition stats, activity history, and achievements. It doesn't expose all internal reasoning. See [reading Coach Mo's thinking](/social-coach/reading-activity-trace).

## Five voices, one memory

Pick Default, Drill Sergeant, Hype Man, Big Sibling, or Sports Analyst. Same memory of you, different style. See [Coach Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo).

## More than chat

- A **Weekly Mo Report** every Monday recapping your week
- **Roast cards** to share your week with friends
- **Contextual insights** as cards on screens you're already viewing
- **Follow-up suggestions** for your next question

## The honest take

Mo is an AI assistant, not a doctor or certified trainer, and it sometimes gets things wrong, especially on fitness science far from your data. When it does, tell Mo and it can correct itself in the same conversation.
