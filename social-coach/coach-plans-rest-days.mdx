---
title: "Manage Coach plans and rest days"
description: "Add a date-range plan or a weekly rest day so Coach Mo's activity reminders fit travel, illness, a busy week, or a day off."
---

A plan tells Coach Mo that your routine is changing for a while, so its activity reminders can ease off. Plans and rest days are on iPhone and Android. They change Mo's reminders only. Your activity data, ring goals, streaks, and competition scores stay the same.

There are two kinds:

- **Date range**: a plan with a start and end date, for travel, illness, recovery, or a busy stretch.
- **Weekly rest day**: one weekday, every week, until you remove it.

## Add a plan

1. Open Coach Mo (tap the red **\+** button, then **Ask Coach Mo**).
2. Open the conversations drawer and tap **Plans & rest days**. (It's also in Coach Mo settings.)
3. Tap the **\+** button at the top right.
4. Choose **Date range** or **Weekly rest day**.
5. Set the dates (**Starts** and **Ends**) or pick the **Rest day**.
6. Under **Plan** (on Android, **Plan type**), pick one: **Travel**, **Rest & recovery**, **Recovery time**, **Busy schedule**, or **Personal plan**. A personal plan needs a short label.
7. Choose what happens to reminders (on iPhone, under **During this plan**): **Pause activity reminders**, **Make reminders gentler**, or **Keep reminders normal**.
8. Tap **Add**.

The plan shows in your list. Mo uses it the next time it decides whether to send you an activity reminder.

## Let Mo suggest a plan

You can also tell Mo in a chat, for example "I'm traveling Tuesday through Friday, go easy on reminders."

1. Mo replies with a **Temporary plan** card showing the dates and what happens to reminders. The card says **Ready to confirm**.
2. Tap **Confirm** to start it, or **Change** to go back to the message field and adjust your request.

Nothing changes until you tap **Confirm**. A suggestion you don't confirm expires, and the card shows the deadline.

## Edit or end a plan

1. Open **Plans & rest days**.
2. Tap the plan to open **Edit plan**.
3. Change what you need and tap **Save**.

To stop a plan early, tap **End plan early** (for a date range) or **Remove weekly rest day** (for a rest day; on Android, **Remove rest day**), then confirm. A date-range plan also ends on its own after its end date.

## Rules and limits

| Rule | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Longest date-range plan | 90 days |
| Personal plan label | 80 characters or fewer |
| Plans active at once | Up to 20 |
| What a plan changes | Coach Mo's activity reminders only |

## Example

You're sick from Monday to Friday. Add a **Date range** plan from Monday to Friday, pick **Recovery time**, and choose **Pause activity reminders**. Mo stops nudging you to move that week. On Saturday the plan ends and normal reminders return. Your rings and streak count exactly as they would have.

## Something not right?

- **A reminder still arrived.** Check that the plan shows as active and covers today. Some notifications, such as competition updates, aren't activity reminders and aren't changed by a plan. Review **Settings** > **Notifications** and your phone's notification permission. See [Notification problems](/troubleshooting/notification-problems).
- **You don't get Coach Mo reminders at all.** Mo's automatic notifications are a Pro feature. See [Notification settings](/account/notifications).
- **The plan won't save.** Check your connection and try again. If it still fails, use the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
