---
title: "Manage Coach plans and rest days"
description: "Create a date-range plan or recurring rest day so Coach Mo's reminders fit your schedule."
---

Plans and rest days tell Coach Mo about a temporary change in your routine. They adjust Coach reminders; they do not change activity data, ring goals, competition scores, or the underlying fitness data.

## Add a plan

1. Open Coach Mo (on iPhone, tap the red **+** button, then **Ask Coach Mo**). Open the conversations drawer and choose **Plans & rest days**, or find it in Coach Mo settings.
2. Tap the **+** button.
3. Choose **Date range** for travel, illness, recovery, or a busy period, or **Weekly rest day** for a recurring weekday.
4. Choose the dates or weekday and the reminder behavior, then save.

For example, a date-range recovery plan can quiet nonessential Coach reminders from Monday through Friday. A weekly Sunday rest day continues until you edit or end it.

## Confirming a plan from Coach Mo

You can also describe the change in a conversation, such as “I’m travelling Tuesday through Friday; pause coaching reminders.” Mo shows a plan card with the schedule, the expected reminder behavior, and **Ready to confirm**. Choose **Confirm** to apply it or **Change** to return to the message field and revise the request. A suggestion does not take effect until you confirm it.

## Editing or ending a plan

Open an existing plan to edit its dates, weekday, or reminder behavior. To remove it, choose **End plan** for a date range or **Remove rest day** for a weekly plan, then confirm. Date-range plans also stop at their scheduled end date.

If a reminder still arrives, check that the plan is active and review **Settings → Notifications** and your phone’s notification permission; those controls can prevent or permit delivery independently of a plan.
