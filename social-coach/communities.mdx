Communities are group chats built around a shared interest or training style, like runners or early risers. They're a way to meet people who train like you without sending a friend request first.

Communities are on every plan. Membership and Pro have no practical limit on joining or posting. On the Free plan you can join up to 3 communities and send up to 10 community messages a week.

## Join a community

1. Tap **Social**, then **Chats**.
2. Scroll to **Explore Communities** and swipe through the cards.
3. Tap the join button on a card to join right away. Or tap the card to preview the community, then tap **Join Now**.

Previewing doesn't join. Once you join, the community moves into **Your Chats**.

## Post in a community

Open the community from **Your Chats** and type in the message bar. Communities support text messages and reactions. Keep it on topic, hype people up, and be the person you'd want in the group.

## Free plan limits

| Limit | Free plan | Membership and Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Communities you can join | 3 | No practical limit |
| Community messages you can send | 10 a week | No practical limit |

- The weekly count starts over each Monday at midnight UTC.
- When you have 3 or fewer messages left, the chat shows how many are left this week.
- When you've used all 10, the chat says so. Messages in direct messages, competition chats, and league chat don't count.
- To join a fourth community on the Free plan, leave one first.

## Leave a community

1. Open the community from **Your Chats**.
2. Open **Community Info**: on iPhone, tap the picture at the top right. On Android, tap the community's name at the top.
3. Tap **Leave Community**, then confirm.

You'll stop seeing its messages. You can rejoin any time from **Explore Communities**.

## Moderation and reporting

Communities are public spaces. The same automatic moderation that covers direct messages and competition chat runs here too. You can report a message or a member, and everything posted follows the [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation).

## Something not right?

- **You can't join.** On the Free plan you may already be in 3 communities. Leave one, or become a Member.
- **Your messages stopped sending.** On the Free plan you may have used this week's 10 messages. They come back on Monday (UTC).
- **Still stuck?** Use the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
