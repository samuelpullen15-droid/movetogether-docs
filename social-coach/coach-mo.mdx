Coach Mo is MoveTogether's AI coach. Mo can talk through your activity, goals, streaks, and competitions, and answer questions about how the app works. Mo is not a doctor and does not give medical advice.

Coach Mo is on every plan. Membership includes weekly Mo chats, Mo alerts, and the Morning Brief with Mo's notes. Pro has no chat limit. Older Free accounts get 5 coaching messages for the life of the account. See [Coach Mo access](#coach-mo-access) for the details.

## Start a conversation

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Tap the red **\+** button at the end of the tab bar.
    2. Choose **Ask Coach Mo**.
    3. Type your question and tap **Send**.

    You can also tap the Ask Mo box near the top of **Home**. It types out sample questions, such as "How did I do today?" Tapping it opens Coach Mo with that question typed in. Nothing is sent until you tap **Send**, so you never spend a message by accident.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Tap the red **\+** button at the end of the tab bar.
    2. Choose **Ask Coach Mo**.
    3. Type your question and tap **Send**.

    If your tab bar has a **Mo** tab instead of the **\+** button, tap **Mo**. You can also tap the Ask Mo box near the top of **Home**. It opens Coach Mo with the question typed in, and nothing is sent until you tap **Send**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The first time you open Coach Mo, it introduces itself (**Meet Mo**) and asks **How should Mo talk to you?** Pick **Supportive** or **Roast me**. You can change this later. See [Roast mode](/social-coach/roast-mode).

Your conversations are saved. Open the conversations drawer to go back to one or start a new one. See [Managing your Coach Mo conversations](/social-coach/managing-conversations).

## Choose a coaching style

Coach Mo has five coaching styles. Each one gives the same advice in a different voice.

| Style | Tagline in the app |
| --- | --- |
| Classic Mo (the default) | Gen Z energy, zero filter |
| Drill Sergeant | No excuses, soldier |
| Hype Man | Your biggest fan |
| Big Sibling | Caring, honest, always real |
| Sports Analyst | The numbers don't lie |

To change your style, open [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings) and tap **Personality**. You pick a style the first time you open Coach Mo, on any plan. Changing it after that needs Pro.

## Coach Mo writes in your language

Mo replies in the language your app is set to. That includes chat replies, Mo's notifications, the Morning Brief, and the Weekly Mo Report. To change the app language, go to **Settings** > **Language**.

## Product help, coaching, and support

Mo sorts each message before it answers. Three kinds of message never count toward your plan's Coach Mo limit:

| What you send | What Mo does |
| --- | --- |
| A question about how the app works ("Where do I enter an invite code?") | Answers from this Help Center. |
| An idea for the app ("You should add...") | Shows an **Idea** card. Tap **Send to the team** to pass it on. It's sent without your name. |
| A report that something is broken (syncing, billing, your account, or a bug) | Shows a **Get help with this** card that hands you to the support chat. |

A question that needs your own data, such as "How did I do this week?", counts as coaching.

### Getting help from support through Mo

When Mo shows **Get help with this**:

1. Tap **Continue to support**. (Tap **Not now** to keep chatting with Mo.)
2. The **Send to support** screen shows your message to Mo. Edit it if you like.
3. Tap **Send**.

Nothing goes to support until you tap **Send**. Along with your message, support sees your app version, phone model, iOS or Android version, plan, and fitness tracker. These details don't appear in your chat. The [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) then opens with your message in it.

You can also reach the support chat any time: tap the red **\+** button and choose **Help**, or go to **Settings** > **Contact Support**.

### Sharing an idea without Mo

Go to **Settings** > **Got an idea? Tell us**. The **Got an idea?** form is also on **Home**.

## Coach Mo access

| Plan | Coaching messages |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 5 messages for the life of the account. On iPhone, after they're used, you may see **Watch an ad for 1 message**. |
| Membership | Mo chats with a weekly limit. The Usage card shows how much you've used as a percentage. Chats come back on your weekly reset day. No ad option. |
| Pro free trial | A limited preview. Mo shows how many trial messages you have left. They don't refill. |
| Pro | No chat limit. Very heavy use can pause Mo until the weekly reset. |

Rules and limits:

- On Free, the 5 lifetime messages never reset or refill.
- On Membership, when you reach the weekly limit, Mo says "You've reached this week's Mo limit. Chats are back" and names the day. Your reset day is your own, so it may differ from a friend's.
- Telling Mo "stop the alerts" or "pause alerts this week" turns Mo alerts off or pauses them, and never uses a chat.
- Ad messages (Free plan, iPhone) are capped at two a day. The day resets at midnight UTC. They are extra messages and never refill the lifetime five.
- On Membership and Pro, the **Usage** card in [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings#usage) shows how much you've used this week and when it resets.
- If your last Pro payment didn't go through, Coach Mo pauses until you update your payment method. The rest of Pro keeps working.
- Coach Mo voice on Apple Watch needs paid Pro. See [Talk to Coach Mo by voice](/social-coach/voice).

## What Coach Mo can help with

- Motivation, goal setting, and ideas for your routine.
- How you're doing today, this week, and over time.
- Your streaks and your competitions. When people are tied, Mo says so (for example, "tied 2nd").
- Coaching plans for travel, illness, busy weeks, or rest days. With Pro, Mo suggests them in chat, and you confirm a plan before it starts. On any plan, you can add one yourself. See [Manage Coach plans and rest days](/social-coach/coach-plans-rest-days).
- Remembering details about you between conversations, with Pro. See [Files](/social-coach/managing-coach-mo-memory).

On Membership and Pro, Mo sends Mo alerts and adds notes to your [Morning Brief](/engagement/morning-brief). With Pro, Mo also shows chart cards in chat, writes the full [Weekly Mo Report](/engagement/weekly-report), and can show short insight cards (Mo Moments) on screens you're already viewing, such as a competition.

Mo can't create competitions, send messages, invite friends, or change your goals for you. The guided builder can prepare a competition from your answers, but you review it and create it yourself. See [Build a competition with Mo](/getting-started/build-with-mo).

## Your data and Mo

Mo uses your activity, workouts, sleep, goals, streaks, badges, and competitions when **Personalize Coach with my fitness data** is on. Find it in **Settings** > **Privacy Settings** under **AI Coach**. With it off, Mo answers as a general fitness coach.

Each reply shows what Mo looked up before answering. See [Reading Coach Mo's thinking](/social-coach/reading-activity-trace).

Don't share passwords or payment details with Mo. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data) for how Coach Mo handles your information.

## Something not right?

- **Mo stopped answering.** Check your plan in the table above. On Free, your 5 lifetime messages may be used. On Membership, you may have reached this week's limit; Mo tells you the day your chats come back. On Pro, open [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings#usage) to see whether you've hit the weekly limit, or check for a payment problem.
- **Mo says Coach Mo is paused because of the community guidelines.** Mo shows when you can chat again. See [Community guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation).
- **Conversations look out of date on a new phone.** Open Coach Mo settings and tap **Re-sync chat history**.
- **Something else is broken.** Use the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).

<Note>
  Coach Mo gives general fitness information only. It can't diagnose, prescribe treatment, or replace a doctor. Talk to a doctor before starting a new fitness program, especially if you have health concerns.
</Note>
