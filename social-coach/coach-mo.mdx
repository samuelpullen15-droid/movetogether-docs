Coach Mo is MoveTogether's AI coach. It can discuss your activity, goals, routines, and competitions, but it is not medical advice.

<Note>
  Without Pro, you get 5 Coach Mo messages total, for the lifetime of the account. Pro includes unlimited Coach Mo.
</Note>

## Start a conversation

On iPhone, tap the red **+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo**, then type a question. Pro members can also use [Coach Mo voice](/social-coach/voice) on Apple Watch. Your conversations are saved so you can return to them later, and suggested follow-up questions can help you continue without starting over.

## Choose a coaching style

Pro members can choose among five personas in Coach Mo settings:

| Persona | Coaching style |
| --- | --- |
| Default | Balanced, friendly, and supportive |
| Drill Sergeant | Direct, tough-love accountability |
| Hype Man | High-energy encouragement and celebration |
| Big Sibling | Warm, caring, and gently accountable |
| Sports Analyst | Data-focused and strategic |

Your persona changes how Mo communicates, not the fitness data or app controls available to it.

## Product help, coaching, and support

Coach Mo has different paths for different jobs:

- **Product help** answers questions about using MoveTogether from the Help Center. This is separate from personalized coaching and can be available without spending a coaching message; a mixed question may be treated as coaching instead.
- **Personal coaching** uses your fitness context. Without Pro you get five messages for the lifetime of the account; Pro is unlimited.
- **Account-specific support** handles account or billing concerns. Use [contact support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) when you need help with your account rather than a general product answer.

## What Coach Mo can help with

- General fitness motivation, goal setting, and routine ideas.
- Context about your recent activity, streaks, and competitions.
- Weekly reports and in-app insights when available.
- Remembering details you choose to keep in Files.
- Proposing temporary plans for travel, illness, busy periods, rest days, or another schedule you describe. You review and confirm a plan before it is applied.

When personalization is enabled, Mo can use context such as activity history, workouts, sleep and recovery signals, goals, streaks, achievements, competitions, weekly progress, and rivalries. The activity trace beneath a response can show what Mo checked before answering; see [Reading Coach Mo's thinking](/social-coach/reading-activity-trace).

Coach Mo cannot autonomously create competitions, send messages or invitations, edit raw fitness data, or change your goals. Those actions stay with you. The separate guided competition builder can collect your choices and prepare a draft, but you review and explicitly create the competition; see [Build a competition with Mo](/getting-started/build-with-mo).

## Activity trace

Coach Mo shows its thinking through an **activity trace**, a timeline showing what data it looked up (your competition stats, activity history, achievements) before forming its response. This helps you understand how Coach Mo arrives at its recommendations.

## Conversations

Every conversation with Coach Mo is saved. Browse your history in the **conversations drawer**, pick up where you left off, or start a new topic anytime.

## Memory and Files

Coach Mo has a long-term memory system called **Files**. It remembers important details about your fitness journey, goals, preferences, and past conversations. View, edit, and organize these memories in the **Files** section of Coach Mo. Memories are organized into folders and documents you can search, add to, or remove.

## Coach Mo access

Without Pro, you get **5 personalized Coach Mo messages total, for the lifetime of the account**. They don't reset or refill. Pro includes **unlimited Coach Mo**. After the 5 are used, you may see **Watch an ad for 1 message**. These credits depend on an ad being offered, are limited to two per UTC day, and never reset the lifetime allowance.

Apple Watch voice requires Pro. See [Talk to Coach Mo by voice](/social-coach/voice) for its separate access and service-budget behavior.

## Contextual insights

Coach Mo can surface **contextual insights** as inline cards on relevant screens. When you're viewing a competition, your activity, or your achievements, Coach Mo may offer a quick insight without you opening a conversation.

## Important disclaimer

Coach Mo cannot diagnose, prescribe treatment, or replace a qualified health professional. It gives general fitness information and guidance only. Always consult a doctor before starting a new fitness program, especially if you have health concerns. Don't share sensitive personal information such as passwords or payment details in Coach Mo chat. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data) for how this feature processes information.
