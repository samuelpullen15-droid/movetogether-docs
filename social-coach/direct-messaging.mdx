A direct message (DM) is a private chat between you and one friend. Starting and sending DMs needs Membership or Pro, for you and for the friend you're messaging. It works the same way on iPhone and Android.

## Start a direct message

Any of these opens a conversation:

- Tap the red **\+** button in the tab bar, choose **New message**, and pick a friend.
- Open **Social**, switch to **Chats**, and tap the pencil (**New message**) next to **Your Chats**.
- On iPhone, open a friend's profile and tap the message button at the top.

In the friend picker, a friend without Membership or Pro shows **Can't receive messages yet**. You can message them once they have a plan that includes DMs.

Your DMs appear in **Social** > **Chats** under **Your Chats**, mixed in with your group chats and sorted by the newest message.

## Send messages, photos, and reactions

- **Text**: type in the message field and tap send.
- **Photos**: tap the attachment button next to the message field. You'll see a preview before you send.
- **Reactions**: touch and hold a message, then pick an emoji. Tap the same emoji again to remove it. When several people use the same emoji, a count shows next to it.

Photos in DMs are checked for inappropriate content. All messages follow the [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation). To control DM notifications, see [Notification settings](/account/notifications).

## If your Membership or Pro ends

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Version 2.2.0">
    Your direct messages stay in **Your Chats**. You can open them and read the whole history, but you can't reply. Where the message field was, you see **You can read this chat. Become a Member to reply.** Tap **Become a Member** to send again.

    Starting a new direct message still needs Membership or Pro. Your friend keeps full access to the chat while they still have Membership or Pro.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Version 2.1.3">
    Your direct messages are not deleted, but you can't send new ones, and they don't show in **Your Chats**. Become a Member again to start sending in your existing DMs. Your friend keeps full access to the chat while they still have Membership or Pro.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Chat access by channel

| Chat | Who can open and post |
| --- | --- |
| Direct messages | To send, you and the friend both need Membership or Pro |
| Competition chat | Every participant, on any plan, while the competition is active or upcoming. Seasonal and evergreen events don't count. |
| League chat | Everyone in your current league group, on any plan |
| Communities | Members who joined. On the Free plan, up to 3 communities and 10 messages a week. See [Communities](/social-coach/communities). |
| MoveTogether Updates | Everyone can read and react. Nobody can reply. |

Competition chat supports text and reactions. If a competition chat won't open, check that you're still a participant and that the competition hasn't ended.

## Something not right?

- **A friend shows Can't receive messages yet.** They need Membership or Pro before you can message them.
- **You can't start a DM.** Starting DMs needs Membership or Pro. See [Plans](/subscriptions/plans).
- **Messages aren't arriving.** Check your connection, then see [Notification problems](/troubleshooting/notification-problems).
- **Still stuck?** Use the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
