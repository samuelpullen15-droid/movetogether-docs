## Sending a direct message

Go to a friend's profile and tap the message icon, or access your DMs from the **Chat** tab. Type your message and tap send. You can send text messages, images, and links.

## Reactions

Tap and hold any message to open the reaction picker. Pick an emoji to react. Your reaction shows up under the bubble with a highlighted background; tap the same emoji again to remove your reaction.

If multiple people react with the same emoji, you'll see a count next to it.

## Sending images and GIFs

Tap the **+** button next to the input bar for attachment options:

- **Photo Library:** pick a photo
- **Take Photo:** open the camera
- **GIF:** search GIPHY for an animated reaction

Attached media previews inline before you send. Tap send to post.

## When new messages arrive

Conversations refresh while you are inside one. Direct-message notifications are managed in **Settings → Notifications** and appear through the Chat experience.

## Hiding conversations

To clean up your message list, **hide** a conversation. This removes it from your visible list but doesn't delete it. If the person sends you a new message, the conversation comes back.

## Content moderation

Images shared in DMs are scanned for inappropriate content to keep the platform safe. All messages are subject to our [Community Guidelines](/account/guidelines-moderation).

## Chat access by channel

DMs need Membership or Pro. Other Chat channels have their own rules:

| Channel | Who can open and participate |
| --- | --- |
| Direct messages | Membership and Pro members who can message the friend |
| Competition chat | Participants in an eligible active or upcoming ordinary competition, on every plan; seasonal and evergreen events do not grant this Chat access |
| Communities | Joined members; on the older Free plan, up to three communities and 10 messages a week |
| League chat | Members of an eligible current league cohort when the cohort meets the in-app eligibility requirements |
| MoveTogether Updates | Everyone can read official announcements; replies are unavailable |

Competition chat supports text messages and reactions. If a competition chat will not open, confirm that you are still a participant and that the competition is active or upcoming; otherwise see [Chat, Updates, and mentions](/social-coach/chat-announcements-mentions).
