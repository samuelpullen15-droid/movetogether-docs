---
title: "Manage what Coach Mo remembers (Files)"
description: "Open Files on Pro to see, add, edit, search, and delete the notes Coach Mo keeps about your goals, habits, and preferences."
---

**Files** is Coach Mo's long-term memory. It holds short notes about your goals, habits, preferences, wins, and anything else Mo has learned, so you don't have to repeat yourself in each conversation. Mo adds notes on its own as you chat.

Files is a Pro feature on iPhone and Android, and a Pro free trial doesn't include it. On the Free plan, Membership, or a trial, Mo doesn't save or use long-term notes, and tapping **Files** shows an upgrade screen. Mo still sees your activity and the current conversation.

## Open Files

1. Open Coach Mo (tap the red **\+** button, then **Ask Coach Mo**).
2. Open the conversations drawer (tap the menu icon at the top left; on iPhone you can also swipe right from the left edge).
3. Tap **Files** near the top.

You'll see **Coach Mo Files** and two folders. Folders start collapsed. Tap a folder to open it, then tap a document to see its notes.

## What's in Files

| Folder | Document | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core | **User** | Basic facts about you |
| Coaching Memory | **Goals** | What you're working toward |
| Coaching Memory | **Habits** | Things you do, or want to do, regularly |
| Coaching Memory | **Barriers** | What gets in the way |
| Coaching Memory | **Preferences** | When you like to work out and what you don't want suggested. Split into Exercise, Nutrition, Recovery, and Other. |
| Coaching Memory | **Wins** | Records, streaks, and milestones Mo noticed |
| Coaching Memory | **Patterns Mo Noticed** | Patterns Mo finds in your recent activity |
| Coaching Memory | **Roast Material** | Details Mo saves for roasts |

## Add a note

1. Open a document.
2. Tap the **\+** button at the top right.
3. Type the note, up to 150 characters. In **Preferences**, also pick a section.
4. Tap **Save**.

You can't add notes to **Patterns Mo Noticed**. Mo fills it in, and you can still edit or delete what's there.

## Edit or delete a note

1. Tap the note to open **Edit bullet**.
2. Change the text and tap **Save**, or tap **Delete bullet** and confirm **Delete**.

On iPhone you can also swipe left on a note and tap **Delete**.

A deleted note disappears from Files right away, and Mo stops using it. The conversation where it came up is not deleted. To remove that too, see [Managing your Coach Mo conversations](/social-coach/managing-conversations).

## Search and sort

- **Search files** at the top matches document names, such as "Goals" or "Wins". It doesn't search the text of each note.
- **Sort** orders the documents in each folder by **Modified**, **Date created**, or **Name**, newest or oldest first. Most recently modified comes first by default.

## Delete everything in Files

Open [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings) and tap **Delete all files**, then confirm. This wipes every note in Files and can't be undone. Your conversations stay.

## Something not right?

- **Mo keeps bringing up something wrong.** Find the note in Files and fix or delete it. Then tell Mo in the chat.
- **Files shows an upgrade screen.** Files needs Pro. A Pro free trial doesn't include it.
- **A document says Empty.** Mo hasn't saved anything there yet. Add a note, or keep chatting.
