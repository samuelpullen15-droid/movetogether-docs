---
title: "Manage your Coach Mo conversations"
description: "Find, start, rename, search, and delete Coach Mo conversations on iPhone and Android, and how conversations differ from Mo's memory in Files."
---

Every chat with Coach Mo is saved as a conversation. You can go back to one, start a new one, rename it, search, or delete it. This works on iPhone and Android, on every plan.

## Open the conversations drawer

1. Open Coach Mo (tap the red **\+** button, then **Ask Coach Mo**).
2. Tap the menu icon at the top left. On iPhone you can also swipe right from the left edge.

The drawer lists your conversations with their titles and how long ago you last chatted. **Files**, **Plans & rest days**, and **Settings** are in the drawer too.

## Start a new conversation

Tap the compose icon (**New conversation**) next to the search field at the top of the drawer. Your current conversation is saved first.

## Rename a conversation

Mo titles each conversation from what you talked about. To change the title:

1. Open the drawer.
2. Touch and hold the conversation.
3. Tap **Rename**.
4. Type a new title and tap **Save**.

## Delete a conversation

1. Open the drawer.
2. Touch and hold the conversation.
3. Tap **Delete**, then confirm **Delete**.

Deleting can't be undone. To delete every conversation at once, use **Delete all chats** in [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings#delete-all-chats-or-files).

## Search your conversations

Tap **Search** at the top of the drawer and type. Search matches each conversation's title and its most recent message. It doesn't search every message, so if nothing comes up, try words from the title.

## Conversations and Files are separate

| | Conversations | Files (Pro) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | The back-and-forth transcript of each chat | Short notes Mo keeps about you between chats |
| Delete one | Touch and hold it in the drawer | Open the note in [Files](/social-coach/managing-coach-mo-memory) |
| Delete all | **Delete all chats** | **Delete all files** |

Deleting a conversation doesn't delete notes Mo saved in Files from it. Deleting a note doesn't delete the conversation.

## Something not right?

- **A conversation is missing on a new phone.** Open Coach Mo settings and tap **Re-sync chat history**.
- **The drawer can't load your conversations.** They're still saved. Check your connection, then close and reopen the drawer.
- **Still stuck?** Use the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
