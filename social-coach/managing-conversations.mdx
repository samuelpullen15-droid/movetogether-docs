---
title: "Managing your Coach Mo conversations"
description: "How to find, rename, delete, and search past Coach Mo conversations, and how chat transcripts differ from Mo's long-term memory stored in Files."
---

Every conversation with Coach Mo is saved automatically. You can browse them, rename them, delete them, or search across all of them.

## Opening the conversations drawer

On iPhone, tap the red **+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo**, then swipe from the left edge or tap the menu icon to open the conversations drawer. You'll see your past conversations listed with their title and how long ago they happened.

## Starting a new conversation

Tap **New chat** at the top of the drawer. Your current conversation is saved automatically before the new one starts.

## Renaming a conversation

Coach Mo gives every chat a default title based on what you talked about. To change it:

1. Open the conversations drawer.
2. Tap and hold a conversation, or use the **⋯** menu next to it.
3. Tap **Rename**.
4. Enter a new title and confirm.

A good rename helps when you want to find a specific past conversation later (e.g., "Marathon training plan" instead of the auto-title).

## Deleting a conversation

1. Open the conversations drawer.
2. Tap the **⋯** menu next to the conversation.
3. Tap **Delete** and confirm.

<Note>
  Deleting a conversation removes it permanently. Your Coach Mo memory (Files) isn't affected. Anything Mo learned about you from that conversation stays in Files unless you remove it from there too.
</Note>

## Searching

Tap the search field at the top of the drawer to find any past message. Search looks across all your conversation history, not just titles.

## What about Coach Mo's memory?

Conversations and **Files** (Coach Mo's long-term memory) are separate. Conversations are the transcripts of what you said back and forth. Files is what Mo remembers about you between conversations.

Deleting a conversation does **not** delete the memories Mo formed during it. Manage those in **Files** from the conversations drawer or in **Coach Mo settings**. Conversely, deleting a File does not delete its conversation transcript.
