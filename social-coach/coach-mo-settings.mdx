Coach Mo settings control how Mo talks to you and show how much of Mo you've used. They're on every plan. A few rows, such as styles other than Classic Mo, need Pro.

## Open Coach Mo settings

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Tap the red **\+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo**.
    2. Tap the menu icon at the top left, or swipe right from the left edge, to open the conversations drawer.
    3. Tap **Settings** at the bottom of the drawer.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Tap the red **\+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo** (or tap the **Mo** tab if your tab bar has one).
    2. Tap the menu icon at the top left (**Open conversations**).
    3. Tap **Settings** at the bottom of the drawer.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Usage

The **Usage** card shows a bar and a percentage, for example "47% used." Its label depends on your plan:

| Label | Plan | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lifetime usage** | Free and Membership | Your 5 lifetime messages. They never reset or refill. |
| **Trial preview** | Pro free trial | A limited preview. Messages don't refill. |
| **Weekly limits** | Pro | No message count, but very heavy use can pause Mo until the reset. The card shows when that is, for example "Limit resets every Sunday at 8 PM ET." |

App questions, ideas, and problem reports don't use a message. See [Product help, coaching, and support](/social-coach/coach-mo#product-help-coaching-and-support). For the full plan comparison, see [Coach Mo access](/social-coach/coach-mo#coach-mo-access).

## Personality

Tap **Personality** to pick a style:

- **Classic Mo** (the default): Gen Z energy, zero filter
- **Drill Sergeant**: No excuses, soldier
- **Hype Man**: Your biggest fan
- **Big Sibling**: Caring, honest, always real
- **Sports Analyst**: The numbers don't lie

Any style other than Classic Mo needs Pro. Without Pro, picking one opens an upgrade screen. Your style shapes Mo's replies, notifications, and Weekly Mo Report.

## Morning Brief

Tap **Morning Brief** to turn the daily brief on or off and pick its time. The row shows your saved time, or **Off**. Morning Brief is a Pro feature. See [Use Morning Brief](/engagement/morning-brief).

## Plans & rest days

Tap **Plans & rest days** to add, edit, or end a temporary plan. See [Manage Coach plans and rest days](/social-coach/coach-plans-rest-days).

## Roast mode

A switch. On, Mo gets meaner and uses your real numbers as ammunition. Off, Mo goes back to normal coaching. You can also turn roast mode on and off from the flame button in the message bar.

The first time you turn on **Roast mode** here without having picked an intensity, Mo asks **How hard should Mo roast?** so you can choose one. This doesn't appear for accounts known to be under 18. See [Roast mode](/social-coach/roast-mode).

## Roast intensity

Pick **Clean**, **Classic**, or **Unhinged**. This sets how much Mo swears in chat, notifications, and Apple Watch voice. If you never pick one, Mo uses **Classic**. The roast switch still decides whether Mo roasts you in chat.

The same choice appears in **Settings** > **Notifications** as **How much Mo swears**, on every plan. Changing it in one place changes both.

Accounts known to be under 18 can only use **Clean**, and the other levels show **Available at 18+**.

## Keep thinking expanded

A switch for the step list (the activity trace) at the top of Mo's replies. It's on by default.

- **On**: the steps stay open.
- **Off**: the steps fold away once a reply finishes. Tap the header to open them.

See [Reading Coach Mo's thinking](/social-coach/reading-activity-trace).

## Use Apple Intelligence

This iPhone switch shows only for Pro members on iOS 27 or later, on an iPhone that can use Apple's Private Cloud Compute. It's off by default.

When it's on, Mo's normal replies run on Apple Intelligence. Your message and your coaching details, like your activity and goals, go to Apple's Private Cloud Compute servers. Roast mode and rich cards still use the standard engine. If you report a problem, Mo still shows the support card. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data).

## Re-sync chat history

Reloads your conversations from the server. Use it if conversations look out of date or didn't carry over to a new phone.

## Delete all chats or files

Two red buttons sit at the bottom of settings. Both ask you to confirm and can't be undone.

| Button | What it deletes | What it keeps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Delete all chats** | Every conversation with Mo | Files (Mo's memory of you) |
| **Delete all files** | Everything in Files: facts, goals, habits, preferences, and wins | Your conversations |

<Note>
  These buttons only affect Coach Mo. Your activity, competitions, friends, badges, and account stay as they are. To delete your whole account, see [Your data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy).
</Note>

## Something not right?

- **The Usage card doesn't load.** Close settings and open them again.
- **You don't see Use Apple Intelligence.** It needs Pro, iOS 27 or later, and an iPhone that supports Private Cloud Compute. It isn't on Android.
- **Mo still swears after you picked Clean.** Check that **How much Mo swears** in **Settings** > **Notifications** also shows **Clean**. If it does, contact the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
