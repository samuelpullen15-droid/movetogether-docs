title: "Talk to Coach Mo by voice"
description: "Have a spoken conversation with Coach Mo on Apple Watch with Pro, or dictate a typed message on iPhone, and fix voice problems."

There are two ways to use your voice with Coach Mo:

| | Apple Watch voice | iPhone dictation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it does | A live spoken conversation. Mo talks back. | Turns your speech into text you can edit, then send as a normal message |
| Plan | Pro (not the free trial) | Every plan |
| Where | MoveTogether on Apple Watch | The Coach Mo message bar on iPhone |

Android doesn't have Coach Mo voice or a dictation button in Coach Mo.

## Talk to Mo on Apple Watch

1. Open MoveTogether on your Apple Watch.
2. Tap the microphone button (**Talk to Coach Mo**) at the top left of the screen.
3. Tap **Chat with Coach Mo**. Allow microphone access if watchOS asks.
4. After **Connecting…**, start talking. Mo's latest reply shows on screen.
5. Tap the red phone button to end the call. Then tap **Talk again** or **Close**.

## Dictate a message on iPhone

1. Tap the red **\+** button and choose **Ask Coach Mo**.
2. Tap the **Dictate** microphone in the message bar.
3. Allow microphone and speech recognition if iOS asks.
4. Speak, then check and edit the text.
5. Tap **Send**. Mo replies in text.

Dictation doesn't start a voice call, and Mo doesn't read replies aloud on iPhone.

## How Mo sounds

- **Style.** Watch voice uses your coaching style from **Personality** in [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings). Each style has its own voice. The Watch has no separate voice picker.
- **Swearing.** Voice follows your **Roast intensity** (Clean, Classic, or Unhinged).
- **Roasts.** Voice ignores the roast mode switch. Mo roasts you only when you ask, for example "roast me," and goes back to normal on your next turn. See [Roast mode](/social-coach/roast-mode).

## Rules and limits

- Watch voice needs Pro. A Pro free trial can't use it. On other plans the Watch shows **Pro feature**.
- Voice and typed chat share Pro's weekly usage limit. If you've reached it, the Watch shows **Coach Mo is resting** until the weekly reset. The **Usage** card in Coach Mo settings shows when that is.
- Voice is powered by a voice provider, ElevenLabs. See [AI and your data](/account/ai-and-your-data) for what it receives.

## Something not right?

- **Pro feature.** Make sure the Watch is signed in to the same account as your iPhone, and that the account has Pro (not a free trial).
- **Nothing happens when you talk.** Allow microphone access for MoveTogether on the Watch, then start a new call.
- **Something went wrong.** Check that the Watch has a connection, then tap **Try again**.
- **Coach Mo is resting.** Wait for the weekly reset instead of reconnecting. You can check the reset time on iPhone in [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings#usage).
- **Still not working?** Use typed Coach Mo on your phone, or the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).

Coach Mo voice gives general fitness guidance. It isn't medical advice or an emergency service.
