Member and Pro are charged by the store you bought them from. That store holds your payment method, sends your receipts, and handles refunds. MoveTogether never sees your card details for a store purchase.

## Who charges you

| Where you subscribed | Who charges you | Plans sold there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iPhone app | Apple (App Store) | Member and Pro |
| Android app | Google (Google Play) | Member and Pro |
| [Web portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/) | Stripe, our payment processor | Pro only |

Not sure which one bills you? Open **Manage Subscription** and tap **Edit subscription**. On iPhone, the app opens the right place for you. See [cancel, change, or restore your subscription](/subscriptions/managing).

Your plan belongs to your MoveTogether account. Sign in to the same account on another phone or on the web portal, and your plan works there too. You still manage and cancel it where you bought it.

## Receipts and purchase history

- **App Store:** Apple emails a receipt, and your purchases are listed at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Google Play:** Google emails a receipt, and your purchases are listed in your Google Play account under **Payments & subscriptions**.
- **Web:** Stripe emails a receipt. Sign in to the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/) to see your web subscription.

## Refunds

All sales are final. Member and Pro are non-refundable, except where the law or the store that charged you requires a refund.

- **Cancelling stops the next renewal.** It doesn't refund the period you already paid for. You keep your plan until that period ends.
- **App Store:** only Apple can give a refund. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Google Play:** only Google can give a refund. Request one through your Google Play account.
- **Web:** your receipt names the seller. If Stripe is the seller, Stripe handles the refund request. If DesignSpark Studio LLC is the seller, we do. Ask in the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).

MoveTogether can't grant, promise, or override a refund decision from Apple or Google. The full rules are in the [Refund Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/refund-policy).

## Free trials and first-time offers

- A free trial costs nothing if you cancel before it ends. If you don't cancel, you're charged the renewal price on the date shown at checkout.
- Cancelling a free trial ends it right away. See [cancel a free trial](/subscriptions/managing#cancel-a-free-trial).
- A paid first-time offer, like Pro's \$1.99 first month on Google Play, is charged when you check out. Then the normal price starts on the renewal date unless you cancel first.
- Charges already made, including a first-time offer, aren't refunded unless the law or the store requires it.

See [free trials](/subscriptions/subscribe#free-trials) for which plans have a trial.

## If a payment fails

Your store tries to collect the payment and tells you what to update. Fix the payment method in the store that bills you:

- **App Store:** in the iPhone **Settings** app, tap your name, then **Payment & Shipping**.
- **Google Play:** in the Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then **Payments & subscriptions**.
- **Web:** on the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/).

While a Pro payment problem is open, you can't cash out Movecoins. The Rewards tab says "There's a problem with your subscription payment. Update it to cash out." with an **Update payment** button. See [cash out for gift cards](/movecoins/cash-out).

## Something not right?

- Charged twice, or charged but no plan? See [subscription issues](/troubleshooting/subscription-issues).
- Questions about a charge? Message us in the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support). Keep receipts and payment details in that private chat, never in a community or a direct message.
