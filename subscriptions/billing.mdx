## How billing works

Your purchase channel processes payment and shows the price, billing period, renewal terms, and cancellation options before you confirm.

### Apple App Store

Apple processes in-app purchases made on iOS using the payment method associated with your Apple account. MoveTogether does not see or store card details for those purchases.

### Google Play

Google processes in-app purchases on Android using the payment method associated with your Google Play account. MoveTogether does not see or store card details for those purchases.

### Web

Pro can be bought through the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/). Membership is sold in the app only.

## Receipts and purchase history

- **Apple App Store:** Apple provides purchase records through your Apple account.
- **Google Play:** Google Play provides purchase records through your Google account.
- **Web:** Sign in to the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/) to review web billing details.

## Refund policy

Canceling prevents a future renewal; it does not automatically refund a completed charge. Refund eligibility is subject to the [Refund Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/refund-policy) and any applicable legal exceptions.

- **App Store purchases:** request a refund from Apple through your Apple purchase history.
- **Google Play purchases:** request a refund through Google Play.
- **Web purchases:** use the support contact or payment route identified in the [Refund Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/refund-policy).

If you do not want to be charged again, cancel through the purchase channel that handles your subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Common questions

### Will I be charged when I subscribe?

The App Store, Google Play, or web checkout shows the terms before you confirm. Annual Membership starts with a 7-day free trial, and the first charge comes when the trial ends unless you cancel first. Monthly Membership has no trial and is charged when you subscribe.

### What happens if my payment fails?

Check the purchase channel that handles your subscription for the current payment status or next steps.

### Can I share my subscription with family?

MoveTogether does not currently support Family Sharing. Each person needs their own subscription.

### Do I lose my data if I downgrade?

No. Your competition history, badges, friends, and other account data are kept. You lose the paid features until you resubscribe.
