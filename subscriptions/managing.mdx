---
title: "Managing your subscription"
description: "Check, manage, cancel, restore, or redeem a MoveTogether Membership or Pro subscription through the store that handles it."
---

Your subscription lives on the **Manage Subscription** screen. To get there, open **Settings** (tap your photo at the top right of Home, then the gear) and tap your plan card at the top.

## Check your plan

Manage Subscription shows your current plan and the actions available to you.

## Manage or cancel

Cancel or change billing through the store that handles your subscription:

- **App Store:** your Apple Account subscription settings.
- **Google Play:** Google Play's subscription settings.
- **Web (Pro only):** the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/).

After you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the current paid period (or trial). Your data stays in your account.

## Restore purchases

If you changed phones or reinstalled and don't see your plan:

1. Sign in to the same MoveTogether account that bought it.
2. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple or Google account used for the purchase.
3. On Manage Subscription, tap **Restore Purchases**, then check your plan again.

Still missing? See [subscription issues](/troubleshooting/subscription-issues).

## Redeem an offer code

On Manage Subscription, tap **Redeem Offer Code**. On iPhone, iOS opens its redemption sheet. On Android, Google Play opens its redemption page.
