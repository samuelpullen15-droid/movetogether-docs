You manage Member and Pro on the **Manage Subscription** screen, on iPhone and Android. Billing changes and cancelling happen in the store that charges you: the App Store, Google Play, or the web portal.

## Open Manage Subscription

1. Open **Settings** (tap your photo at the top of **Home**, then the gear).
2. Tap your plan card at the top.

The screen shows your plan, when it renews, and what it includes.

## Cancel or change your plan

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open **Manage Subscription**.
    2. Tap **Edit subscription**.
    3. The app finds where you bought your plan. For an App Store plan, Apple's subscription screen opens. For a web portal plan, you get your web management link.
    4. Cancel or change your plan there.

    You can also cancel an App Store plan in the iPhone **Settings** app: tap your name, then **Subscriptions**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open **Manage Subscription**.
    2. Tap **Edit subscription**. Google Play's subscription screen opens.
    3. Cancel or change your plan there.

    You can also cancel in the Google Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web">
    Pro bought on the web is managed on the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/). Sign in and open your subscription to cancel or change it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the store, or you'll keep being charged.

## What happens when you cancel

### Cancel a paid plan

Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for. Then it ends. Cancelling doesn't refund the current period. See [billing and refunds](/subscriptions/billing#refunds).

### Cancel a free trial

Cancelling a free trial, Member or Pro, ends it right away. You lose trial access at once, not at the end of the trial, and you aren't charged. To get the features back, subscribe to the paid plan.

### When your plan ends

- **Accounts created on version 2.1.1 or later** need Member or Pro to keep using the app. The app asks you to choose a plan again.
- **Accounts created before 2.1.1** go back to Free, unless the account gave up the Free plan by starting a plan of its own. See [older accounts](/subscriptions/subscribe#older-accounts-and-the-free-plan).

Your account data isn't deleted. Your competition history, friends, and earned badges stay. You lose the plan's features, like hosting more competitions or sending direct messages, until you subscribe again. Your Movecoins stay too, but you can't cash them out without a plan. See [compare plans](/subscriptions/plans) for what each plan includes.

## Restore purchases

Use this if you got a new phone or reinstalled the app and don't see your plan.

1. Sign in to the same MoveTogether account you used when you subscribed.
2. Make sure your phone is signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account that paid for the plan.
3. Open **Manage Subscription** and tap **Restore Purchases**.

On iPhone, you'll see "Purchases restored. Your subscription should appear shortly." If you see "No active purchases found to restore on this Apple ID," the plan isn't on that Apple Account.

A plan bought on the [web portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/) isn't restored this way. It's tied to your MoveTogether account, so sign in with that account and it shows up.

## Redeem an offer code (iPhone)

1. Open **Manage Subscription**.
2. Tap **Redeem Offer Code**.
3. Enter the code in Apple's redemption screen.

The Android app doesn't have this button.
