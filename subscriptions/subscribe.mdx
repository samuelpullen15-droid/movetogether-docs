## Subscribe or upgrade

1. Open **Settings** (tap your photo at the top right of Home, then the gear) and tap your plan card at the top. Or tap an upgrade prompt on any locked feature.
2. Review the plan, your price, and the billing options shown.
3. Choose one and confirm through your store.

- **iPhone:** confirm through the App Store with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple Account password.
- **Android (open beta):** confirm through Google Play. The app shows your Google Play price first.
- **Web:** Pro is also sold through the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/). Your plan syncs to the app after checkout.

## The Membership trial

Accounts created on version 2.1.1 or later start a 7-day free trial of Membership during onboarding. After the trial it renews at the Membership price unless you cancel before it ends, through the store you used. See [managing your subscription](/subscriptions/managing).

## Other offers

If an introductory, promotional, or trial price is available, the App Store, Google Play, or web checkout shows it, with renewal terms, before you confirm.
