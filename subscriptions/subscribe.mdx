You can buy Member or Pro in the app on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Pro is also sold on the [web portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/), for less. Member is sold in the app only. To see what each plan includes, see [compare plans](/subscriptions/plans).

## Subscribe in the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plans">
    Open **Settings** (tap your photo at the top of **Home**, then the gear) and tap your plan card at the top. Or tap the upgrade prompt on any locked feature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    On **Manage Subscription**, tap **Become a Member** for Member, or **Upgrade to Pro** for Pro. To see both plans side by side first, tap **See full plan comparison**. It has the same two buttons at the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose monthly or yearly">
    The screen shows the price from your store, in your currency, and any free trial you qualify for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with your store">
    On iPhone, confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple Account password. On Android, confirm in Google Play.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your new plan shows up in the app right away. If it doesn't, see [restore purchases](/subscriptions/managing#restore-purchases).

## Subscribe to Pro on the web

Pro costs less on the web: \$9.99 a month or \$79.99 a year, against \$12.99 or \$99.99 in the app (US prices).

1. Go to the [MoveTogether portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/) and sign in with your MoveTogether account.
2. Choose monthly or yearly Pro and check out.
3. Open the app. Pro syncs to your account after checkout.

On iPhone in the US, a Free account also sees **Subscribe on the web & save** on **Manage Subscription**. It opens the portal already signed in.

## New accounts

Accounts created on version 2.1.1 or later choose Member or Pro when they sign up. The plan screen during sign-up has no close button. If you don't want a plan yet, you can sign out.

- If a friend invited you, you get a free month of Member, and the plan screen goes away once it arrives. See [Invite & Earn](/account/referrals).
- If a Member or Pro friend invited you to their private competition, you may be able to play there for free. See [Host pays](/subscriptions/plans#host-pays-friends-play-free).

## Free trials

| Plan | Trial |
| --- | --- |
| Member, yearly | 3 days free, then \$14.99 a year |
| Member, monthly | No trial |
| Pro, yearly | 1 week free for first-time subscribers on the App Store and the web portal |
| Pro, monthly | No free trial. On Google Play, your first month is \$1.99 if you have never subscribed before |

What to know before you start a trial:

- The store or checkout shows the trial length, renewal price, and renewal date before you confirm.
- If you don't cancel, the plan renews at the full price when the trial ends.
- **Cancelling a free trial ends it right away.** You lose trial access at once, not at the end of the trial. To keep the features, subscribe to the paid plan.
- During a Pro trial, Coach Mo is a preview of up to 10 messages, and Movecoins can't be cashed out until your first payment goes through.

## Older accounts and the Free plan

If your account was created before version 2.1.1, you're on the Free plan and you don't have to pay.

<Warning>
  If you start a Member or Pro subscription or free trial yourself, your account gives up the Free plan for good. When that plan or trial ends, you need Member or Pro to keep using MoveTogether. A plan given to you as a reward or promotion, like free Pro time from Invite & Earn, doesn't count.
</Warning>

This applies to plans and trials started after you accepted the Terms dated October 18, 2026. The [Terms and Conditions](https://movetogetherfitness.com/terms-and-conditions) and the [Refund Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/refund-policy) have the full wording.

## Switch from Member to Pro

1. Open **Manage Subscription** and tap **See full plan comparison**. Or tap any locked Pro feature, like Coach Mo after your 5 messages.
2. Tap **Upgrade to Pro**.
3. Choose monthly or yearly and confirm with your store. The store shows how the change is billed before you confirm.

## Something not right?

- Charged, but no plan in the app? See [restore purchases](/subscriptions/managing#restore-purchases).
- Web plan not showing in the app? Make sure you signed in to the portal with the same MoveTogether account you use in the app.
- More fixes are in [subscription issues](/troubleshooting/subscription-issues), or message us in the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
