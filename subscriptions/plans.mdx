MoveTogether has two paid plans:

- **Membership** unlocks the whole social side: unlimited hosting, direct messages, group chat, communities, the full friends feed, and custom taunts. It's $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year (US prices; the store shows your local price).
- **Pro** includes everything in Membership, plus unlimited Coach Mo, deeper analytics, premium badges, and no ads. The app shows the current Pro price before you buy.

**New accounts:** accounts created on version 2.1.1 or later start with a 7-day free trial of Membership during onboarding. It renews as a paid plan unless you cancel before the trial ends.

**Existing accounts:** accounts created before 2.1.1 stay on the **Free** plan at no cost, and can upgrade at any time.

## Hosting and chat

The Free hosting limit counts competitions you host, not ones you join. Draft and upcoming competitions use a slot; ending or deleting one frees it. Anyone can join Personal Goal and Stick To It competitions.

Each chat channel has its own membership rules. Competition chat needs you to be in the competition; community and league chat depend on membership. See [chat access by channel](/social-coach/direct-messaging#chat-access-by-channel).

## Which plan?

Want to compete and chat with friends without limits? Membership. Want Coach Mo, deeper analytics, and premium badges too? Pro.
