MoveTogether has three plans: **Free**, **Member**, and **Pro**. This page is the full list of what each one includes. Other help pages link here when a feature needs a plan.

- **Member** (also called Membership) is the social app: unlimited hosting, direct messages, the full friends feed, communities, and custom taunts.
- **Pro** is everything in Member, plus Coach Mo without a message count, activity analytics, milestone badges, Group Challenges, and double Movecoins.
- **Free** is for accounts created before version 2.1.1, and for friends playing free in a paying host's competition. New accounts can't choose Free.

## Prices

US prices. The App Store or Google Play shows the price in your own currency before you confirm.

| Plan | In the app (App Store and Google Play) | On the web portal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member | \$1.99 a month, or \$14.99 a year | Not sold on the web |
| Pro | \$12.99 a month, or \$99.99 a year | \$9.99 a month, or \$79.99 a year |

Free trials and first-time offers:

- **Member, yearly:** starts with a 3-day free trial. The monthly plan has no trial.
- **Pro, yearly:** starts with a 1-week free trial for first-time subscribers on the App Store and on the [web portal](https://portal.movetogetherfitness.com/).
- **Pro, monthly on Google Play:** your first month is \$1.99 if you have never subscribed to MoveTogether before, then \$12.99 a month.

The store or web checkout shows any trial or offer you qualify for, with the renewal price and date, before you pay. See [how to subscribe or upgrade](/subscriptions/subscribe).

<Note>
  Cancelling a free trial ends it right away, not at the end of the trial. See [managing your subscription](/subscriptions/managing#cancel-a-free-trial).
</Note>

## Who is on the Free plan

- **Accounts created before version 2.1.1** keep the Free plan at no cost and can upgrade any time. If one of these accounts starts a Member or Pro subscription or free trial of its own, it gives up the Free plan for good. See [older accounts](/subscriptions/subscribe#older-accounts-and-the-free-plan).
- **Friends of a paying host** can play free in that host's private competitions. See [Host pays](#host-pays-friends-play-free).

Accounts created on version 2.1.1 or later pick Member or Pro when they sign up. If that plan ends, the app asks you to choose a plan again before you can keep using it.

## Hosting competitions

**Joining** means taking part in a competition someone else made. **Hosting** means you created it.

- On Free, you can host up to 2 competitions at a time. Drafts, upcoming, and active competitions all count. Seasonal events don't. When one ends, or you delete it, the slot opens again.
- On iPhone, a Free account at the limit may see **Watch an ad for 1 competition**. Watching the ad gives you one more slot until that competition ends.
- Member and Pro can host as many competitions as they like.
- Personal Goal, Stick To It, Collective (one shared group goal), Best Days, and Sleep competitions take Member or Pro to host. Anyone who can join a competition can join these.
- A Group Challenge (a big event split into divisions) takes Pro to host.
- The **Crew health** card shows a Pro host which crew members have gone quiet, with a **Check in** button. Only the host sees it.

See [how competitions work](/competitions/how-it-works), [personal and shared goals](/competitions/personal-and-shared-goals), and [Group Challenges](/competitions/group-challenges).

## Host pays: friends play free

When a Member or Pro hosts a **private** competition, friends without a plan can join it for free.

| Host's plan | Friends who can play free |
| --- | --- |
| Pro | Unlimited |
| Member | 3, counted across all of the host's private competitions |
| Free | None |

Rules:

- It covers private competitions only. Public competitions and seasonal events don't use free spots. Seasonal events are free for everyone anyway.
- A Member host's 3 spots are shared across all their private competitions. The same 3 friends can play every round.
- A friend who is already playing keeps their spot. The limit only stops new friends from joining once all 3 spots are taken.
- Friends who already have Member or Pro don't use a spot. Neither do older accounts on the Free plan.
- When the spots are full, a new friend sees "This host's free guest spots are all taken, so joining needs a Membership."

**Example:** Sam is a Member and hosts two private competitions. Alex, Jo, and Kim join without a plan. That uses all 3 of Sam's spots. Next week Sam starts a third competition, and Alex, Jo, and Kim can join it too. When Lee tries to join, Lee needs a plan, because Sam has no spots left. If Sam upgrades to Pro, Lee can join free.

## Chat

- **Competition chat** is open to everyone in the competition, on any plan.
- **Direct messages** and **group chats** you start with friends take Member or Pro.
- **Communities** on Free: you can join up to 3 and send 10 messages a week. The week resets on Monday (UTC). Member and Pro have no limit.

See [direct messages](/social-coach/direct-messaging) and [communities](/social-coach/communities).

## Coach Mo

- **Free and Member:** 5 Coach Mo messages for the life of the account. They don't reset. On iPhone, a Free account that has used them may see **Watch an ad for 1 message**, up to a daily limit. Member accounts don't get this option.
- **Pro:** no message count. Very heavy use can pause Coach Mo until your weekly limit resets.
- **Pro free trial:** Coach Mo is a preview of up to 10 messages. They don't refill during the trial.

Coach Mo's personalities, Automations, Mo Moments, Morning Brief, and the Weekly Mo Report are Pro. See [Coach Mo](/social-coach/coach-mo) and [Coach Mo settings](/social-coach/coach-mo-settings).

## Badges

"Badges" covers every medal in the app.

- **Seasonal event badges** are free for everyone. They're only available while the event runs.
- **Streak, achievement, and competition badges** are Pro only to earn. Every plan can open the Badges screen.

See [badges](/competitions/achievements).

## Ads

On iPhone, the Free plan shows ads. Member and Pro show no ads unless you choose to watch one. See [ads and measurement](/account/ads-and-measurement).

## Movecoins

Movecoins are reward points for active days. Anyone in the US can earn them. Pro earns double coins from activity. Cashing out for gift cards takes Member or Pro, and you must be 18 or older. See [What are Movecoins?](/movecoins/overview).

## Ways to get a plan without paying

- **Invited by a friend:** a new account that joins with a friend's invite gets a free month of Member. See [Invite & Earn](/account/referrals).
- **Inviting friends:** when friends you invite join and stay active, you can earn free Pro time. See [Invite & Earn](/account/referrals).
- **Playing in a paying host's competition:** see [Host pays](#host-pays-friends-play-free).

## Check which plan you're on

- Your plan shows as a **PRO** or **MEMBER** pill under your photo on your profile. Free shows no pill. Friends see the same pill on your profile.
- Or open **Settings** (tap your photo at the top of **Home**, then the gear). Your plan card is at the top. Tap it to open **Manage Subscription**.
