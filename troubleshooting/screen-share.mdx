When a problem is hard to explain, a person from our team can watch your MoveTogether screen live while you show them. Nothing is shared until you say yes, and you can stop at any time. Marco, our AI support agent, never sees the video. A person does.

Screen sharing works on iPhone and Android. If you'd rather send one picture instead, see [send support a screenshot](/troubleshooting/screenshots).

## How a share starts

There are two ways.

**In the support chat.** Marco can offer a share as a card in your chat that says **Share your screen?** Tap **Share screen** to go ahead, or **Not now**. You can also ask for one yourself. Tap the **\+** next to the message box and choose **Share screen**. That sends "I'd like to share my screen." to support.

**As a request from support.** Support can also send a request that pops up in the app: **MoveTogether Support wants to see your screen**. Tap **Allow** to go ahead, or **Not now**. If you don't see it, open the app.

## Allow the share

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    After you tap **Share screen** or **Allow**, iPhone asks to record your screen. Tap **Allow** there too.

    Only MoveTogether is shared. Other apps on your iPhone are not.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    After you tap **Share screen** or **Allow**, Android asks what to share. Choose to share one app, then pick MoveTogether. Then only MoveTogether is shared.

    <Warning>
      If you choose to share your entire screen, everything on it is shared. On Android 10 through 13, Android always shares your entire screen, including other apps you open and notifications that arrive. Close anything private first, and don't open other apps until you stop.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## While you share

- A glow runs around the edge of your screen, and a banner says **Support is viewing your screen**. On Android, a notification also shows while you share.
- Support sees everything in MoveTogether except your chats: your rings, health and fitness numbers, competitions, and settings.
- Your chats are hidden. If you open a chat screen, you see **Chats are hidden while you share your screen**. They come back when you stop.
- Support can point and draw on your screen to show you where to tap. Their marks fade after a few seconds.
- Support can message you during the share. Tap the chat button on the banner to read and reply.

## Stop sharing

Tap **Stop** on the banner at any time. On Android you can also tap **Stop sharing** in the notification.

A share also ends on its own after 30 minutes. If support doesn't connect after you allow it, sharing stops and the app says **Support didn't connect, so sharing stopped.** When a share that started in the chat ends, the chat shows a line such as **Screen share ended · 4 min**.

## What is kept

| | Share offered in the chat | Request from support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video of your screen | Not recorded | Not recorded |
| Pointers and drawings | Not saved | Not saved |
| Messages sent during the share | Saved in your support chat | Not saved |

MoveTogether also keeps a record that a share happened: who asked, when it started and ended, and whether you allowed or declined it. See the [Privacy Policy](https://movetogetherfitness.com/privacy) for details.

## Something not right?

- **"Couldn't start screen sharing. The request may have expired. Ask support to send a new one."** The request timed out. Ask in the chat for a new one.
- **The card says "This request has ended."** The offer timed out. Tap **\+** and choose **Share screen** to ask again.
- **You tapped Don't Allow on the iPhone or Android prompt by mistake.** Ask support for a new request and allow it this time.
