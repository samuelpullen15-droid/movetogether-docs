---
title: "I'm not getting notifications"
description: "Fix missing or unwanted MoveTogether notifications: check the in-app switches, your phone's notification settings, Focus modes, and Coach Mo's Morning Brief."
---

MoveTogether notifications need two things turned on: the switch for that kind of notification inside MoveTogether, and notifications for MoveTogether in your phone's settings. Check both. This works the same on every plan, though some Coach Mo notifications need Pro.

## 1. Check your phone's settings

The app tells you first. Open your **Profile**, tap the gear to open **Settings**, then tap **Notifications**. The top of the page shows whether your phone lets MoveTogether notify you.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    | What the top says | What to do |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Notifications: On** | Your phone is fine. Go to step 2. |
    | **Notifications: Quiet delivery** | Notifications arrive silently in Notification Center only. Tap **Enable notifications** and allow them to get banners and sound. |
    | **Notifications: Off** | iPhone is blocking every notification. Tap **Open iOS Settings** and turn on **Allow Notifications**. |

    Also check:

    1. A Focus (Do Not Disturb, Sleep, Work) isn't hiding MoveTogether. In iPhone **Settings** > **Focus**, allow MoveTogether or turn the Focus off.
    2. Low Power Mode can delay notifications.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    If notifications are off for MoveTogether, the page says **Notifications are off for MoveTogether in your Android settings, so the switches below have no effect until you turn them back on.**

    1. Tap **Open notification settings**.
    2. Allow notifications for MoveTogether.
    3. Android can also list MoveTogether's notifications by category. Turn on the categories you want there too.

    Battery saver and Do Not Disturb can also hold notifications back.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 2. Check the in-app switches

In **Settings** > **Notifications**, make sure the kind of notification you're missing is on. The switches are the same on iPhone and Android:

| Section | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Daily Reminders** | **Good morning**, **Good night**, and **Stay-active nudges** |
| **Mo Automations** (Pro) | Coach Mo's **Plan updates**, **Competition edge**, **Streak rescue**, and **Recovery adjustments**. Up to four a week, within your quiet hours. |
| **Competition Updates** | Standings, results, and invites |
| **Competition Lifecycle** | **Lifecycle alerts** (start, countdown, last call to sync, final results) and **Position changes** |
| **Leagues** | Promotion, demotion, and when someone passes you |
| **Cheers** | When someone cheers you on |
| **Friend Activity** | Friends joining competitions, milestones, and friend requests |
| **Direct Messages** | New direct messages |
| **Group Chat** | Messages in your competition group chats |
| **Achievements & Milestones** | New badges and personal records |
| **Weekly recap** | Your week's summary, by push and email |
| **Coach Mo** | Tips and insights from Coach Mo |
| **Promotions & Events** | Seasonal events and announcements. Turning this off doesn't stop competition updates. |
| **Account & Security** | Your account, subscription, and security |

Support replies aren't on this list. They come through as long as your phone allows MoveTogether notifications.

## Missing a Morning Brief (iPhone)

1. Open Coach Mo's settings and tap **Morning Brief**.
2. Turn on **Daily Morning Brief**.
3. Set your **Greeting time**, then tap **Save**.

A Morning Brief notification arrives only on days a brief is ready.

## Missing a Coach Mo reminder

Open **Plans & rest days** from the Coach Mo conversations list or Coach Mo's settings. A temporary plan (for travel or recovery) or a recurring rest day changes which reminders Mo sends while it's active.

## Getting too many notifications

Go to **Settings** > **Notifications** and turn off the switches you don't want. For Coach Mo, you can turn off **Mo Automations** or set a rest day in **Plans & rest days**.

## Still not getting them?

Restart your phone, update MoveTogether, and check again. If a notification you have turned on still never arrives, tell the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) which one and since when.
