---
title: "Why your numbers differ from Apple Health or Health Connect"
description: "On iPhone, MoveTogether counts one tracker while Apple Health combines every device. On Android, it counts the tracker you pick, or Health Connect's combined total if you haven't picked one. Here's how to check your numbers."
---

MoveTogether and your phone's health app can show different numbers for the same day. That's usually expected. How it works depends on your phone.

## The short version

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    MoveTogether counts **one tracker**, the one you chose, for Steps and Exercise. Apple Health shows a **combined total** from every app and device, so it usually reads higher. Move works a little differently: see [why your numbers don't match your tracker's app](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker).

    To check MoveTogether, compare it with **your tracker itself** (your watch, ring, or its app), not with Apple Health's total.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    If you picked a tracker, MoveTogether counts **that app's** steps and calories. Health Connect shows a **combined total** from every app, so it can read higher. On a day your tracker didn't record a metric, MoveTogether uses Health Connect's combined total for it.

    If you haven't picked a tracker, MoveTogether uses **Health Connect's own combined total**, minus anything typed in by hand, so it should match Health Connect.

    To pick or change your tracker, see [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source#on-android).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Steps and workouts typed in by hand never count in MoveTogether, on either phone. The health app does include them, so on those days it reads higher.

## iPhone: why one tracker

Scores must never count the same movement twice. If you wear two trackers on one walk, both record it. Adding every source together would count that walk two or three times and reward owning more devices. So MoveTogether uses one tracker's record for your Steps and Exercise rings. Move is the exception, and so is a day your tracker hasn't synced yet: see [why your numbers don't match your tracker's app](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker).

You can change which tracker counts. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source).

| App | What its number means |
| --- | --- |
| MoveTogether on iPhone | Your chosen tracker's Steps and Exercise for the day, without hand-typed entries. Move follows your Apple Watch, or without one, the higher of your tracker and Apple Health's Move |
| Apple Health | A merged total from your iPhone, watch, and any other connected apps |

### Check your steps

1. Open your tracker or its app (for Apple Watch, the Activity app or the watch itself).
2. Compare its number with the same day in MoveTogether.
3. They should match.

If MoveTogether matches your tracker but not Apple Health, everything is working.

### Why Apple Health shows more

Apple Health fills gaps with your **iPhone's** steps, for example while your watch is charging, and adds any other apps or trackers. If you wear your tracker all day, the numbers are usually the same.

Steps you take with only your iPhone, while your tracker is off, count in Apple Health but not in MoveTogether. On those days, expect MoveTogether to read lower.

Exercise minutes work the same way. Apple's own Exercise ring can include workouts from other devices and workouts typed in by hand. MoveTogether counts only your chosen tracker's minutes, so it can read lower.

## Android: when the numbers differ

- **You picked a tracker.** MoveTogether counts its steps and calories, not Health Connect's combined total. Compare with your tracker's own app instead.
- **You changed your tracker recently.** A pick counts from the day you make it. Earlier days keep the numbers they had.
- **Hand-typed entries.** Steps or workouts typed into Health Connect by hand are left out.
- **App priority.** When MoveTogether uses Health Connect's combined total (no tracker picked, or a day your tracker didn't record that metric), Health Connect's app priority decides which app counts. Change it in Health Connect.

## Still looks wrong?

- Let your tracker finish syncing to Apple Health or Health Connect, then pull to refresh.
- Compare the same date and metric. Active calories are not total calories, and Exercise minutes are not total workout time.
- See [fitness data not syncing](/troubleshooting/sync-issues) and [manage and troubleshoot data sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).
- If it still doesn't match, contact [the support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) with the date, metric, device, and app version.
