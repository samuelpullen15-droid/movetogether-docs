title: "My numbers don't match Apple Health or Health Connect"
description: "Why MoveTogether can read lower than the Health app on iPhone or Health Connect on Android: one tracker counts, hand-typed entries don't, and the health app adds every device together."
---

This page is for when MoveTogether doesn't match your phone's health app: **Apple Health** on iPhone or **Health Connect** on Android. If it doesn't match your watch's or ring's own app (Garmin Connect, Oura, WHOOP, and so on), see [my numbers don't match my tracker's app](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker) instead.

A difference is usually expected. The health app adds up every device and app on your phone. MoveTogether counts one tracker and leaves out anything typed in by hand.

## The short version

| | Health app shows | MoveTogether counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steps and calories | A merged total from your phone, watch, and any connected apps | Only your chosen tracker. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source). |
| Exercise minutes | Can include workouts from other devices and typed-in workouts | Only your chosen tracker's workouts, never typed-in ones |
| Hand-typed steps or workouts | Included | Never counted |

So on most days the health app reads the same or higher, never because MoveTogether lost data.

## Why one tracker

Scores must never count the same movement twice. If you wear two trackers on one walk, both record it. Adding every device together would count that walk twice and reward owning more devices. So MoveTogether takes one tracker's numbers for your day.

## iPhone: compare the right number

1. Open your tracker or its app. For an Apple Watch, use the Fitness app or the watch itself.
2. Compare its number with the same day in MoveTogether.
3. They should match. If MoveTogether matches your tracker but not Apple Health, everything is working.

Apple Health fills gaps with your **iPhone's** steps, for example while your watch is charging, and adds any other apps. Steps you take with only your iPhone, while your tracker is off, count in Apple Health but not in MoveTogether.

Move works a little differently from Steps and Exercise. See [my numbers don't match my tracker's app](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker#with-an-apple-watch-move-matches-your-watch).

## Android: what MoveTogether counts

- **You picked a tracker in Data Sources.** MoveTogether counts only that tracker's steps and calories, starting with the day you picked it. If it recorded nothing for a day, MoveTogether uses Health Connect's combined total for that day.
- **You haven't picked one.** MoveTogether uses Health Connect's combined total for the day. Health Connect's app priority decides which app counts when two record the same activity.

In both cases, anything typed into Health Connect by hand is left out.

## Still looks wrong?

- Let your tracker finish syncing to Apple Health or Health Connect, then pull to refresh.
- Compare the same date and metric. Active calories are not total calories, and Exercise minutes are not total workout time.
- On iPhone, a recent day's steps can go up but not down. See [my steps went up and won't come down](/troubleshooting/sync-issues#my-steps-went-up-and-wont-come-down-iphone).
- See [my activity isn't syncing](/troubleshooting/sync-issues) and [my tracker isn't showing up or counting](/troubleshooting/data-sources).
- If it still doesn't match, tell the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) the date, the metric, your tracker, and both numbers.
