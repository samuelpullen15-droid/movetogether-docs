---
title: "Why your numbers differ from Apple Health or Health Connect"
description: "MoveTogether counts one tracker, while Apple Health and Health Connect combine every device, so the health app usually reads higher. Here's how to check your numbers."
---

MoveTogether and your phone's health app can show different numbers for the same day. That's expected. They answer different questions.

## The short version

MoveTogether shows the numbers from **one tracker**, the one you chose. Apple Health (iPhone) and Health Connect (Android) show a **combined total** from every app and device, so they usually read higher.

To check MoveTogether, compare it with **your tracker itself** (your watch, ring, or its app), not with the health app's total.

## Why one tracker

Scores must never count the same movement twice. If you wear two trackers on one walk, both record it. Adding every source together would count that walk two or three times and reward owning more devices. So MoveTogether uses one tracker's record as a whole. It never mixes one device's steps with another device's calories.

You can change which tracker counts. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source).

| App | What its number means |
| --- | --- |
| MoveTogether | Your chosen tracker's record for the day |
| Apple Health or Health Connect | A merged total from your phone, watch, and any other connected apps |

## Check your steps

1. Open your tracker or its app (for Apple Watch, the Activity app or the watch itself).
2. Compare its number with the same day in MoveTogether.
3. They should match.

If MoveTogether matches your tracker but not the health app, everything is working.

## Why the health app shows more

The health app fills gaps with your **phone's** steps, for example while your watch is charging, and adds any other apps or trackers. If you wear your tracker all day, the numbers are usually the same.

Steps you take with only your phone, while your tracker is off, count in the health app but not in MoveTogether. On those days, expect MoveTogether to read lower.

## Still looks wrong?

If MoveTogether doesn't match **your tracker** for a day:

- Let the tracker finish syncing to Apple Health or Health Connect, then pull to refresh.
- Compare the same date and metric. Active calories are not total calories, and Exercise minutes are not total workout time.
- See [fitness data not syncing](/troubleshooting/sync-issues) and [manage and troubleshoot data sources](/troubleshooting/data-sources).
- If it still doesn't match, contact [Marco support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) with the date, metric, device, and app version.
