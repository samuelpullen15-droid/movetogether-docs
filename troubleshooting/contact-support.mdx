title: "Contact support"
description: "When to use MoveTogether support for account, sync, billing, privacy, deletion, security, and other sensitive issues, and where to send product feedback."
---

For account access, sync, billing, privacy, deletion, security, and other sensitive issues, open **Settings → Resources → Contact Support**. On iPhone you can also tap the red **+** button and choose **Help**. In-app support includes your account context. If the app uses the web support route instead, include the details needed to identify the issue without sending passwords, payment-card details, or other secrets.

## What to include

To help support understand a technical problem, include:

- A description of what happened
- Your tracker and phone, if relevant
- When the issue started
- Any error messages you saw
- Your phone model and OS version
- Screenshots, if they do not contain sensitive information you do not want to share

## Feedback and feature requests

Use [Share product feedback](https://movetogetherfitness.com/roadmap) for ideas, bugs, rough edges, and feature requests.

<Note>
  The feedback board is public. Do not include account, health, billing, security, or other sensitive information there. Use the in-app support flow or [MoveTogether support](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact) instead.
</Note>
