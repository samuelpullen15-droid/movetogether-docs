The support chat is how you reach us. It is free on every plan. Use it for sync problems, billing, your account, deletion, security, moderation appeals, or anything else. When you open it from the app, the chat is linked to your account, so you don't need to type your email or user ID.

## Open the support chat

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    Any of these opens the same chat:

    - Tap the red **\+** button, then **Help**.
    - Tap **Help** at the top of Home, if you see it there.
    - Open your **Profile**, tap the gear to open **Settings**, then under **Resources** tap **Contact Support**.

    If the chat can't load, you see **Support chat didn't load** with a **Try again** button. If it still can't open, the app shows the **Help** screen. Tap **Message** there to try the chat again, or tap **Help Center** to read these articles.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Any of these opens the same chat:

    - Tap the **\+** button, then **Help**.
    - Open your **Profile**, tap the gear to open **Settings**, then under **Resources** tap **Contact Support**.

    If the chat can't open on your phone, the app opens an older version of the chat instead. If that can't open either, the app starts an email to [support@movetogetherfitness.com](mailto:support@movetogetherfitness.com).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Website">
    Go to [movetogetherfitness.com/contact](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact) and tap the chat button in the bottom-right corner. It is on every page of the website.

    You can chat without signing in. To link the chat to your account, tap **Sign in** in the chat and use the email you signed up with in the app (we email you a code), or sign in with Apple or Google. Use the website chat if you can't open the app.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What you see in the chat

The chat opens on the support home. It says **How can we help?** and gives you a few ways in:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Start a chat** topics: **My activity isn't syncing**, **Switch my tracker**, **Competition scoring**, **Billing and plans** | Starts a chat and sends that topic as your first message. |
| **Something else** | Opens an empty message box so you can describe the problem in your own words. |
| **Ask anything about MoveTogether** (the bar at the bottom) | Opens the chat with the keyboard up. |
| **Help Center** | Opens these help articles. |
| Your conversation | If you already have an open chat, it appears at the top. Tap it to pick up where you left off. |

## Who answers

**Marco** answers first. Marco is our AI support agent, and the chat labels him **AI support** next to his name. He can look into your account, explain how things work, and file a ticket for the team.

When a person from the team takes over, the chat shows a line such as **Marco handed this over to Sam**, and their replies show their own name. Marco's answers are AI-generated and can be wrong. If something doesn't look right, ask for a person.

Under your newest message you see **Sent**, then **Read** once support has it. While someone is writing back, you see a line such as **Marco is typing**.

You don't have to keep the chat open. When support replies, you get a notification. In the app you also see a **Support replied** banner (tap it to read the reply), and the **Help** button shows a count of new replies.

## What to include

The more of this you give, the faster we can fix it:

- What you expected and what happened instead
- The date and the metric (for example, "Steps on October 12")
- Your tracker (Apple Watch, Garmin, Oura, WHOOP, and so on)
- Any error message, word for word
- A screenshot of the screen with the problem. See [send support a screenshot](/troubleshooting/screenshots).

When the chat starts from the app, support can already see your app version, phone model, plan, and tracker, so you don't need to look those up.

Don't send passwords, payment card numbers, or other secrets in the chat. Only share health details that help explain the problem.

## Coach Mo can send you here

If you tell Coach Mo that something is broken, like a sync failure, a crash, or a billing problem, Mo offers a **Get help with this** card. Tap **Continue to support** to see your message before anything is sent. You can edit it, then tap **Send**. Support also gets your app version, phone model, phone software version, plan, and tracker. Those details don't appear in your chat.

## Tickets

If your problem needs a fix from the team, support opens a ticket and you can follow it under **Settings** > **Resources** > **Your tickets**. See [follow a support ticket](/troubleshooting/support-tickets).

## Screen sharing

For some problems, support may ask to see your screen. Nothing is shared until you allow it. See [share your screen with support](/troubleshooting/screen-share).

## Ideas and feature requests

To suggest a feature, use **Got an idea? Tell us** at the bottom of Home or in **Settings** > **Resources**. It goes straight to the team. See [suggest a feature](/troubleshooting/share-an-idea). The old public feedback board is closed.

## Privacy and data requests

To ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted, email [privacy@movetogetherfitness.com](mailto:privacy@movetogetherfitness.com) or ask in the support chat. We may need to confirm it's you before we act.
