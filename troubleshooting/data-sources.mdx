---
title: "Manage and troubleshoot data sources"
description: "Check Apple Health or Health Connect access, your chosen tracker, and missing metrics before changing anything."
---

Start in **Settings → Data Sources**. MoveTogether reads Apple Health on iPhone and Health Connect on Android, so most problems are one of three things: your wearable isn't sharing into the health app, MoveTogether can't read that metric, or a different tracker is counting.

## What you see and what to do

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Your device isn't in the tracker list | Turn on sharing in the wearable's own app. See [Apple Health](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker) or [Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker). |
| Your tracker **hasn't synced recently** | Open the wearable's app and let it sync, then tap **Get it syncing** or refresh. |
| Apple Health needs access | Tap **Connect** or **Review Access** and allow the categories you want. |
| One metric is empty | Check that the device records it and shares it into the health app, and that MoveTogether can read it. |
| Totals differ from another device | Check which tracker is counting. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source). |
| No recent activity in the wearable's app | Sync the device there first. MoveTogether can't read what hasn't arrived. |

Pull to refresh after each check.

## iPhone: Apple Health

**Health Data** in Data Sources shows today's value for each metric. To allow or block a specific app for a metric, open the Health app, tap **Browse**, choose the metric, then **Data Sources & Access**.

If Data Sources offers to load your history, let it finish before checking older dates.

## Android: Health Connect

1. Confirm the reading is in Health Connect.
2. Check that your wearable's app has permission to write it.
3. Check MoveTogether's read permissions in Health Connect. Background and history access are separate permissions.
4. Reopen MoveTogether and refresh.

See [Android public beta](/getting-started/android-public-beta) for what MoveTogether reads.

## Revoking access

Revoking a data type stops future reads of that type. It doesn't remove copies already stored in your account. See [data controls](/account/data-and-privacy).

## "Moving to Apple Health" in Data Sources

This section appears on iPhone if you connected a wearable directly before version 2.1.1. That connection keeps working for now. Turn on Apple Health sharing in the wearable's app ([guide](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker)), then choose it as your tracker, so nothing is lost when you switch. On Android, switch to Health Connect the same way ([guide](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker)).

Still stuck? Use [fitness data not syncing](/troubleshooting/sync-issues).
