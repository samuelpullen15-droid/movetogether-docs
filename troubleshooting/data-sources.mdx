---
title: "Manage and troubleshoot data sources"
description: "Check Apple Health or Health Connect access, your chosen tracker, missing metrics, and older connections before changing anything."
---

Start in **Settings → Data Sources**. MoveTogether reads Apple Health on iPhone and Health Connect on Android, so most problems are one of three things: your wearable isn't sharing into the health app, MoveTogether can't read that metric, or a different device is counting (your chosen tracker, or on Android, Health Connect's app priority when no tracker is picked).

## What you see and what to do

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Your device isn't in the tracker list | Turn on sharing in the wearable's own app. See [Apple Health](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker) or [Health Connect](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker). |
| Your tracker **hasn't synced recently** | Open the wearable's app and let it sync, then refresh. On iPhone, you can also tap **Get it syncing**. |
| Apple Health needs access | Tap **Connect** or **Review Access** and allow the categories you want. |
| One metric is empty | Check that the device records it and shares it into the health app, and that MoveTogether can read it. |
| Totals differ from another device | Check which tracker is counting. See [choose which tracker counts](/getting-started/primary-activity-source). On Android with no tracker picked, check Health Connect's app priority. |
| Steps or a workout you typed in by hand is missing | That's expected. Steps and workouts typed in by hand don't count. |
| No recent activity in the wearable's app | Sync the device there first. MoveTogether can't read what hasn't arrived. |

Pull to refresh after each check.

## iPhone: Apple Health

**Health Data** in Data Sources shows today's value for each metric. To allow or block a specific app for a metric, open the Health app, tap **Browse**, choose the metric, then **Data Sources & Access**.

If a recent day is missing, tap **Backfill from Apple Health**. It pulls in recent days that didn't reach MoveTogether. **Review Health Permissions** lets you allow newly supported metrics, and **Manage in Apple Health** opens the Health app.

## Android: Health Connect

1. Confirm the reading is in Health Connect.
2. Check that your wearable's app has permission to write it.
3. Check MoveTogether's read permissions in Health Connect. Reading in the background is a separate permission. MoveTogether reads up to the last 30 days.
4. Reopen MoveTogether and refresh.

See [MoveTogether on Android](/getting-started/android) for what MoveTogether reads.

## Revoking access

Revoking a data type stops future reads of that type. It doesn't remove copies already stored in your account. See [data controls](/account/data-and-privacy).

## Older connections in Data Sources

If you connected a wearable to MoveTogether directly before version 2.1.1, Data Sources may still list it.

- **iPhone:** it appears under **Connected services**. These older connections can only be removed: tap **Disconnect**. Your wearable now reaches MoveTogether through Apple Health, so turn on Apple Health sharing in the wearable's app ([guide](/apple-health/connect-your-tracker)) to keep its activity counting.
- **Android:** it appears under **Moving to Health Connect**. These connections are being retired. Turn on Health Connect sharing in the wearable's app ([guide](/health-connect/connect-your-tracker)) so your activity keeps counting.

Still stuck? Use [fitness data not syncing](/troubleshooting/sync-issues).
