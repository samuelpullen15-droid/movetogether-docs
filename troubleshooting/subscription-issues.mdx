---
title: "My plan or purchase isn't showing"
description: "Fix a Member or Pro plan that isn't showing, restore a purchase, understand why a cancelled free trial ends at once, and fix a payment problem."
---

Use this page if you paid for Member or Pro but features are still locked, your plan looks wrong, or your payment didn't go through. It covers iPhone, Android, and plans bought on the website.

## Features are locked or a purchase is missing

1. Check you're signed in to the same MoveTogether account you bought the plan on.
2. Check the plan is active in the same Apple ID, Google account, or website account you paid with.
3. Open your **Profile**, tap the gear to open **Settings**, then tap your plan card at the top. This opens **Manage Subscription**.
4. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
5. Check your plan again.

On Android you see **Purchases restored. Your subscription should appear shortly.** If you see **No active purchases found to restore on this Google account.**, you're signed in to Play with a different Google account than the one you paid with.

## I was charged but don't see my plan

Follow the steps above first. If your plan still doesn't show, message the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) and tap **Billing and plans**. Keep receipts and order numbers inside that chat, which is private to your account.

## I cancelled my free trial and lost access right away

That's expected. Cancelling a free trial of Member or Pro ends it at once, not at the end of the trial. To get access back, subscribe to a paid plan.

A paid plan works differently. When you cancel a paid plan, you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

## There's a problem with my payment

If your last payment didn't go through, update your payment method in the store you subscribed with:

- **iPhone:** in the iPhone **Settings** app, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**.
- **Android:** in Google Play, open **Payments & subscriptions**.
- **Website:** in your account on the MoveTogether website.

While a payment is outstanding, some features pause:

- Coach Mo pauses. On iPhone it says **We couldn't take your last payment, so Coach Mo is paused. Update your payment method to start chatting again.**
- Movecoins shows **There's a problem with your subscription payment. Update it to cash out.** Tap **Update payment** to go to your store's subscription page.

Once the store takes the payment, reopen MoveTogether and they come back.

## Cancel or change your plan

In **Manage Subscription**, tap **Edit subscription**. You can also cancel in the store you paid with, using the same places listed above. See [manage your subscription](/subscriptions/managing).

## Something else?

Message the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support). Don't post receipts or account details anywhere public.
