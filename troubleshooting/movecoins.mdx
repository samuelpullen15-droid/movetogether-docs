Find the message you see, then follow the fix. Movecoins is for members in the US. Anyone can earn coins, but cashing out for gift cards needs a paid Member or Pro plan and you must be 18 or older. For anything not listed here, use the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).

## I don't see Movecoins at all

Movecoins appears as a **Rewards** tab only when all of these are true:

- Your App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) account country is the **United States**.
- You have the latest version of MoveTogether.
- Movecoins has reached your account. It's rolling out, and it can be paused for everyone for a while.

## Turning Movecoins on

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Movecoins are only available in the US.** | Movecoins needs a US App Store or Google Play account. |
| **Movecoins needs the latest Apple Health sync. Try again.** (iPhone) | The sync update didn't finish. Tap **Turn on Movecoins** again and let it complete. |
| **Connect Health Connect first, then turn on Movecoins.** (Android) | Connect Health Connect in **Settings** > **Data Sources**, then try again. |
| **We're still waiting for your first Health Connect sync. Try again in a minute.** (Android) | Wait a minute, then tap **Turn on Movecoins** again. |
| **Our terms were just updated. Take a look, then turn on Movecoins again.** | Read the updated terms, then tap **Turn on Movecoins** again. |
| **Update the app to turn on Movecoins.** | Update MoveTogether from the App Store or Google Play. |
| **We didn't hear back. Check your connection and try again.** | Check your internet connection and try again. |
| **Too many tries. Wait a bit and try again.** | Wait a while before trying again. |
| **Movecoins aren't available right now.** | Movecoins is paused. Try again later. |

## The "Get my card" button stays gray

Check both **I'm 18 or older** and **I live in the US**, and enter a valid email under **Send it to**.

If you're under 18, you can keep earning coins, but you can't cash out until you turn 18. Coins still expire on the normal schedule while you wait.

## My gift card hasn't arrived

Every cash-out waits **2 days** before it's sent, so refunds and cancelled plans can show up first. Your first cash-out, and any that need a closer look, are also checked by a person on our team. Until it's sent, your history on the **Rewards** tab shows **Processing**, and your coins are already set aside for it.

1. Check the history row. **Processing** means it hasn't been sent yet.
2. Once it's sent, check your spam folder for the email.
3. Still missing after it shows as sent? Contact the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) with the card amount and the date you cashed out.

## My history says "Couldn't send. Our team is on it."

The card couldn't be sent. If it failed before a card was ordered, your coins come back to your balance on their own, and the row says **Returned. Coins back in your balance**. If it failed after a card was ordered, the team reviews it and either delivers the card or sends one of equal value.

## My balance looks too low

- Only days after you turned Movecoins on count.
- Steps and workouts typed in by hand don't count.
- Activity can arrive late, so today's coins may show up later.
- Coins expire 12 months after the month you earned them.
- On days you hold Pro, activity coins are doubled. On other days they aren't.

See [how you earn Movecoins](/movecoins/earning).
