## My score is not updating

1. In **Settings → Data Sources**, check that your tracker is counting and has synced recently.
2. Let your tracker's own app finish syncing to Apple Health or Health Connect.
3. Open the competition leaderboard and pull down to refresh.
4. Confirm that the competition is active and that the metric is eligible for its scoring method.

Scores are calculated on the server from synced activity. A refresh can't add data that hasn't reached Apple Health or Health Connect yet. See [the scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference) for method-specific caps and qualification rules.

## I cannot join

Check that the competition has not ended, the invite or code is current, and any invitation restriction includes you. Joining is unlimited on every plan. If it is an active competition, its displayed dates and rules determine whether activity can be counted.

## I cannot host

Membership and Pro can host unlimited competitions. On the older Free plan you can host up to two active, upcoming, or draft competitions; complete or delete one to free a slot, or use a bonus slot if you have one. Seasonal and evergreen competitions don't count. Also check the Fair Play notice and your tracker in **Settings → Data Sources**.

## A score or position changed unexpectedly

First, wait for everyone's trackers to sync, then refresh. If the host proposed a scoring change, every participant must accept before it applies; then prior days are rescored and positions can move. One decline leaves the existing scoring in place. Read [changing competition scoring](/competitions/changing-scoring) for the full process.

If the result still appears incorrect after the source has synced, use the in-app support flow and include the competition name, date, scoring method, and the activity value you expected.
