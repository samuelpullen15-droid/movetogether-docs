title: "I can't join a competition, or my score looks wrong"
description: "Fix competition problems: join and hosting messages, plan limits, scores that won't update, ties and co-winners, results that changed, and retired WHOOP Strain competitions."
---

Find the problem you have below. Scores come from activity that has synced to MoveTogether, so many score problems are really sync problems. See [my activity isn't syncing](/troubleshooting/sync-issues) if your rings are wrong too.

## My score isn't updating

1. Open **Settings** > **Data Sources** and check that your tracker is counting and has synced recently.
2. Let your tracker's own app finish syncing to Apple Health or Health Connect.
3. Open the competition and pull down to refresh.
4. Check that the competition is active and that your activity fits its scoring method.

MoveTogether calculates scores on its servers from synced activity. A refresh can't add data that hasn't reached Apple Health or Health Connect yet. Steps and workouts typed in by hand never count. See [the scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference) for each method's caps and rules.

In a [Distance competition](/competitions/distance), only the distance in recorded runs (or runs and walks, if the host chose that) counts. Everyday steps don't add distance.

## I can't join

| What you see | What it means and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| **This competition is full** | The host's participant limit is reached. Ask the host to raise it, or join another. |
| **Joining this competition needs a Membership. Private competitions hosted by a Member or Pro user are free to join while the host has room.** | Your account needs a Member plan to join this one. A private competition hosted by a Member or Pro is free to join while the host has free spots. |
| **This host's free guest spots are all taken, so joining needs a Membership.** | A host on the Member plan can bring 3 friends for free across all their private competitions, and those spots are taken. Ask the host, or get a Member plan. |

On Android, the two Membership messages show as a **Become a Member** screen instead of text.

How free joining works:

- **Pro host:** any number of friends join their private competitions free.
- **Member host:** up to 3 friends join their private competitions free. Friends already playing keep their spot.
- **Public competitions and Free-plan hosts:** no free guest spots.
- **Seasonal events:** free for everyone to join.

Some older accounts and members under 18 can join without a Member plan. If you see none of these messages, check that the competition hasn't ended and that your invite or code is current.

## I can't host

On the Free plan you can host up to 2 competitions that are active, upcoming, or still drafts. Joined competitions, seasonal events, and evergreen competitions don't count. To free a spot, finish or delete one, or use a bonus slot if you have one. Member and Pro can host as many as they like. If you hit the limit, you see **You've reached your hosting limit. Get a Membership or Pro to host as many as you like.**

Some formats need a plan to host. On iPhone their cards show a plan label. On Android a locked format's name ends in **(Member)**. In version 2.2.0, where creating a competition is one screen, these cards are on the **Scoring** page, and on both iPhone and Android the **Best days** row under **Fine-tune** shows a **MEMBER** label. Without the plan, you see an upgrade screen or one of these messages:

| Message | What it needs |
| --- | --- |
| **Hosting a Personal Goal competition takes a Membership or Pro.** | Member or Pro |
| **Hosting a Stick To It competition takes a Membership or Pro.** | Member or Pro |
| **Hosting a Shared Goal competition takes a Membership or Pro.** | Member or Pro (a Collective competition, where the group shares one goal) |
| **Hosting a Sleep competition takes a Membership or Pro.** | Member or Pro. See [Sleep](/competitions/sleep). |
| **Sleep competitions are for friends only. Make it private to use Sleep.** | Sleep competitions must be private. |
| **Hosting a Best Days competition takes a Membership or Pro.** | Member or Pro |
| **Hosting a Group Challenge is a Pro feature. Anyone you invite to a private one joins free.** | Pro. Member isn't enough. See [Group Challenges](/competitions/group-challenges). |

The first time you create a competition, you also agree to the one-time **Fair play** rules. Tick the box and tap **Accept and continue**. If you tapped **Cancel**, nothing was created. Try again and accept to continue. See [your first competition](/getting-started/first-competition#fair-play-agreement).

## We tied

If two or more people tie for first when a round ends, they all win it. Each co-winner gets the win and the "you won" notification. Teams work the same way: every member of every team tied at the top wins. If nobody scored at all, the round has no winner.

In version 2.1.3, the iPhone leaderboard lists tied people one above the other, with whoever joined first on top. On Android tied people share the same rank number. In version 2.2.0, tied people share the same rank number on both, and each co-winner sees "You tied for first!" when it ends.

## The results changed, or someone moved unexpectedly

- **Late syncs.** Everyone's activity keeps arriving after the fact, and recent days update for about two days. Wait for trackers to sync, then refresh.
- **After the results.** Once the results notification goes out, the round is final. A late sync still saves that person's day, but it no longer changes the order or the winner.
- **Scoring changes.** If the host proposes a scoring change, every participant must accept before it applies. Then earlier days are rescored and positions can move. One decline keeps the current scoring. See [changing competition scoring](/competitions/changing-scoring).

## Someone's score stopped moving

If a member's account is suspended, their competition scores stop updating for as long as the suspension lasts. They also can't comment, react, or nudge in competitions. If it's your account, see the message in the app, or ask the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support).

## WHOOP Strain competitions

WHOOP Strain is retired as a scoring method. You can't create a new Strain competition or switch a competition to it. If you try, you see **WHOOP Strain competitions are no longer available. Pick a different scoring method and your whole group can compete.**

A Strain competition that already existed keeps scoring until it ends. If it was set to repeat, the current round is its last.

## Still looks wrong?

Message the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) and tap **Competition scoring**. Include the competition name, the date, and the activity value you expected. A screenshot of the leaderboard helps. See [send support a screenshot](/troubleshooting/screenshots).
