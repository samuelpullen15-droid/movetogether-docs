---
title: "The app is slow, freezing, or crashing"
description: "Fix a slow MoveTogether app, a screen that won't load, a freeze, or a crash on launch, starting with updating to the latest version."
---

Most slowdowns and crashes are fixed by updating the app and restarting your phone. Start there, then work down this page. This applies to every plan, on iPhone and Android.

## First: update MoveTogether

Many fixes ship in app updates, so make sure you have the latest version.

- **iPhone:** update from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/movetogether-fitness-compete/id6757620063).
- **Android:** update from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=studio.designspark.movetogether).

To see which version you have, scroll to the bottom of **Settings**.

## Tapping Social freezes the app (iPhone)

On iOS 26, tapping **Social** could freeze MoveTogether until you closed it. Version 2.1.3 fixes this. Update the app, then open **Social** again.

## The app is slow

1. Force-close MoveTogether and reopen it.
2. Check your internet connection. A slow or patchy connection slows leaderboards and syncing.
3. Update to the latest version.
4. Restart your phone.

## A screen won't load

1. Go back, then open the screen again.
2. Pull down to refresh.
3. Force-close and reopen the app.
4. Check your internet connection.

It may be a short outage on our side. Try again in a few minutes.

## The app crashes when it opens

1. Update the app to the latest version.
2. Restart your phone.
3. If it keeps crashing, delete MoveTogether, reinstall it, and sign in again. Your activity, competitions, and badges are saved to your account, not the phone.

## Still happening?

Tell the [support chat](/troubleshooting/contact-support) what you were doing when it happened, the screen it happened on, and your app version. A screenshot or a [screen share](/troubleshooting/screen-share) helps. If the app won't open at all, use the chat on [movetogetherfitness.com/contact](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact).
