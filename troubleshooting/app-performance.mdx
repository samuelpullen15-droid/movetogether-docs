---
title: "App performance issues"
description: "How to fix MoveTogether crashes, slow performance, and screens that won't load, with quick checks for connectivity, app version, and device restarts."
---

## App is running slowly

- Force-close and reopen the app.
- Check your internet connection. A slow or unstable connection can affect leaderboard loading and data sync.
- Update to the current version for your platform.
- Restart your phone.

### Update MoveTogether

- **iPhone:** update MoveTogether from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/movetogether-fitness-compete/id6757620063).
- **Android (open beta):** update MoveTogether through Google Play. Open the [MoveTogether Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=studio.designspark.movetogether) to check for an update.

## App crashes on launch

1. Update the app to the current version.
2. Restart your phone.
3. If the issue continues, reinstall MoveTogether and sign in again.

## A specific screen isn't loading

- Try navigating away and back to the screen.
- Pull down to refresh.
- Force-close and reopen the app.
- Check your internet connection.

If the issue continues, it may be temporary. Try again in a few minutes. If it keeps happening, use the in-app support flow or [MoveTogether support](https://movetogetherfitness.com/contact). Include your phone model, OS version, app version, and the screen that is not loading.
