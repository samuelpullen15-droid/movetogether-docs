When a competition reaches its halfway point and when it ends, MoveTogether builds a short recap story of how it went. From the final recap, anyone can start a rematch. Hosts on Member or Pro also get an organizer report. All of this works on iPhone and Android.

## Halfway recap

Competitions that run 7 days or longer get a halfway recap. The morning after the halfway point, you get a notification such as "Halftime in 'Step Showdown'. Your recap is ready." Tap it to open the story.

The halfway recap shows the standings at the halfway mark, how the race has gone so far, and the group goal if there is one. The notification is the only way to open it.

## Final recap

When the final results are in, you get a results notification. Tap it to open the final recap. You can also open it any time from the competition: tap **More options** (**⋯**), then **View recap**.

The final recap can show, depending on the competition:

- **The podium** with a headline about how it was decided.
- **The race:** how often the lead changed hands.
- **Awards**, one per person so more people get a moment: Champion, Most Consistent, Most Improved, Comeback, Weekend Warrior, Weekday Hustler, Single-Day Champ, and Iron Streak.
- **Team awards** in a team competition.
- **The group's biggest day** and weekend versus weekday averages.
- **The group goal**, whether you hit it, and a suggested next goal.
- **You:** your place, points, best day, and how many days you were active.
- **Rematch**, on the last slide.

**Members and Pro also see** their rank day by day and a line from Coach Mo about their competition. On the Free plan, this part shows as locked.

### Share your recap

On the last slide, tap **Share** to share a recap card with your points, place, and activity. Public competitions, seasonal events, and past rounds of a repeating competition don't have a rematch slide, so they don't have this button.

## Rematch

The last slide of a final recap asks **Run it back?** Tap **Rematch, same crew** to set up the same competition again.

| | What the rematch gets |
| --- | --- |
| Rules | Same name, scoring, and teams |
| Dates | Starts tomorrow and runs the same number of days |
| Group goal | A little higher if you hit it, or a little past what you did if you missed |
| People | Everyone from the original gets an invitation. They aren't added until they accept. |
| Not copied | The cover photo. The rematch is private and doesn't repeat. |

Anyone who played can tap it, and they become the rematch's host. The normal hosting rules apply. For example, on the Free plan the rematch uses one of your 2 hosting spots. Only finished private competitions can be rematched.

On iPhone, a finished one-on-one challenge also has **Rematch** in the **More options** (**⋯**) menu.

## Past rounds of a repeating competition

A repeating competition shows a row of round tabs, such as **Cycle 1**, **Cycle 2**, with each round's dates. Tap a past round to see its final podium, leaderboard, and daily breakdown. To see that round's recap, tap **More options** (**⋯**), then **View recap**.

Your [competition history](/competitions/competition-history) also lists repeating competitions.

## Organizer report (hosts on Member or Pro)

The host of a finished competition can see who took part and how they did, for everyone who agreed to share.

### Share your results with the organizer

If you're playing in someone else's competition, a **Share my results with the organizer** switch on the competition screen controls what they can see. It's off by default. When it's on, the host can see your name as it shows on the leaderboard, your points and steps, and any awards you win, in their report and spreadsheet. Turn it off any time, and the next report leaves you out. Seasonal events don't have this switch.

### Open the report

1. Open a finished competition, or a finished round.
2. Tap **More options** (**⋯**), then **Organizer report**.

The report shows how many people shared with you, then one row per person who shared: place, name, points, steps, and awards. With 5 or more people, it also shows group totals. Hosts on the Free plan see a locked preview.

### Organizer email

After each finished round, a host on Member or Pro also gets an email: "Your report: [competition name]". It's sent only if at least one other person shared their results, and only if the host has competition notifications on.

## Export results as a spreadsheet

The host can download a CSV spreadsheet of results. Tap **Export CSV** inside the organizer report. On iPhone, any host can also tap **Export results** on the competition screen, whatever their plan.

- It lists one row per person per round: name, username, round, round dates, status, and points.
- It includes you and everyone who turned on **Share my results with the organizer**. Nobody else is in it.
- Names follow the leaderboard's privacy rules, so a hidden name stays blank.
- You can export up to 3 times an hour.

Distance competitions add distance columns and pass or fail. See [Distance competitions](/competitions/distance#share-your-results-with-the-organizer).

## Something not right?

- No recap: the final recap is ready once results are final, usually the morning after the last day. See [how competitions work](/competitions/how-it-works#from-start-to-final-results).
- The organizer report is empty: nobody has turned on sharing yet.
- More help: [competition issues](/troubleshooting/competition-issues).
