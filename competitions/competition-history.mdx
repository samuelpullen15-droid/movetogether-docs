---
title: "See your past competitions"
description: "Open your competition history, read your Competed, Wins, and Podium totals, search past competitions, and revisit final results."
---

Every competition you finish is saved to your history, so you can look back at results and open any final leaderboard. History works on iPhone and Android, on every plan.

## Open your history

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    Open **Compete** and tap **Competition history** (the archive box button at the top).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Open **Compete** and tap the history button at the top.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The screen is called **History**.

## What you'll see

At the top, three totals:

| Total | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Competed** | Competitions you finished. Each finished round of a repeating competition counts once. |
| **Wins** | Times you finished first on the final leaderboard. |
| **Podium** | Times you finished in the top 3. |

Below, one card per finished competition, newest first. A repeating competition has one card for the whole series, with its first and last dates.

With more than 8 competitions, the list is grouped by year.

## Open a past result

Tap a card to open that competition's final leaderboard. For a repeating competition, use the round tabs at the top to switch between rounds. See [past rounds](/competitions/recaps#past-rounds-of-a-repeating-competition).

## Search

With more than 5 competitions, a **Search history** box appears. Type part of a competition's name to narrow the list.

## Something not right?

- A competition is missing: it shows here only after its last day has ended for everyone in it. A repeating competition shows once its first round has finished.
- Your Wins total differs from the wins on your profile: in version 2.1.3, History counts your place on the final leaderboard as listed, and your profile counts shared first places. When two people tie for first, both are winners on their profiles, but only the one listed first counts a win in History. In version 2.2.0, every co-winner counts a win in History too, including every member of teams tied for first.
