---
title: "Captain draft"
description: "Let captains pick the teams one at a time in a live draft before a team competition starts."
---

With **Captain draft**, each team has a captain, and the captains pick their teammates one at a time in a live draft before the competition starts. Everyone in the competition can watch the picks. It works on iPhone and Android, on every plan, in team competitions and [Group Challenges](/competitions/group-challenges).

## Set it up

1. Set up a [team competition](/competitions/teams#set-up-teams) as usual.
2. Under **TEAM ASSIGNMENT**, pick **Captain draft** ("Captains pick the teams one at a time in a live draft before the start.").
3. Invite people with **Who**, and tap **Create competition**. Captain draft doesn't ask for **YOUR TEAM**, because the draft places you too.

A Captain draft competition always starts as upcoming, even if its start date is today, so the draft can run first.

## Open the draft room

Open the competition and tap **Open draft room** on the **Captain draft** card. The card says **Captain draft is live** while picks are happening, **Your pick!** when it's your turn, and **Teams are set** once the draft is over.

The room shows each team with its captain and the people picked so far. While the draft is live, it also shows whose turn it is (**On the clock:** and a name), the time left, **Up next**, and **Still available**, with a **Rookie** label on members without enough recent activity yet.

## Before the draft (host)

Under **Captains**, the host can tap **Choose captains** and pick one captain per team, then tap **Save captains**. If no captains are chosen, the fastest members lead the teams, based on their recent activity.

When everyone you want is in, tap **Start draft**. Every member gets a notification that the draft has started. Each team needs a captain, so the draft can't start with fewer members than teams.

## How picks work

- Picks go in snake order: 1, 2, 3, then 3, 2, 1, and so on. The slowest captain picks first, because the fastest captain is already the strongest player.
- Each pick has a 60-second clock. The captain on the clock gets a notification: "Your pick! Choose a teammate before the clock runs out."
- To pick, tap **Pick** next to a name under **Still available**.
- If the clock runs out, the app picks for that captain: the fastest member left, with Rookies after everyone else.
- A captain can turn on **Auto-draft for me**, before or during the draft. The app then picks the fastest member left each time it's their turn, and they don't get a notification for each pick.
- If someone else's pick lands first, you see **That pick already went. Here's the draft now.** Pick again.

The host can tap **Finish draft now**, then **Finish draft**, to end it early. Every pick left is made automatically, fastest member first.

## When the draft ends

When the last pick is made, the teams are final, and every member gets a notification with their team, for example "The captain draft is done. You're on Team Red." Nobody can change teams after that.

If the draft hasn't started or finished by the competition's start, MoveTogether finishes it then, making every pick left automatically.

People who join after the draft has started go on the team that needs them most, the same way as with [Fair split](/competitions/teams#fair-split-teams-picked-for-you). They don't get picked.

## Something not right?

- **You don't see Captain draft under TEAM ASSIGNMENT:** update the app.
- **The competition didn't start:** a Captain draft needs at least one person per team. The host gets a notification, and the competition starts as soon as there are enough people. If its end date passes first, it closes with no results.
