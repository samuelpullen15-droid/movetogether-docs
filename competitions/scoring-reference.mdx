The host picks a scoring mode when creating a **Compete** competition. A **Collective** competition doesn't use one: see [Collective competitions](#collective-competitions) below. iPhone has eight: Ring Close Count, Head to Head, Workout Count, Percentage of Goals, Total Activity, Personal Goal, Stick To It, and Distance. Android has the same list without Distance. Head to Head scores one metric (Step Count, Calorie Burn, Exercise Minutes, or Sleep), so those four formulas appear in the table below. Scores are calculated on the server from synced activity, and each day's score is added across the competition window.

## Activity-based methods

| Method | Daily score | Limit or qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ring Close Count | One point for each Move, Exercise, and Steps goal reached | Maximum 3 points per day |
| Percentage of Goals | Move-goal percentage + Exercise-goal percentage + Steps-goal percentage | Each ring is capped at 100%, for a maximum of 300 points per day |
| Total Activity | active calories + exercise minutes + rounded(steps ÷ 100) | No method cap |
| Step Count | steps | No method cap |
| Calorie Burn | active calories | No method cap |
| Exercise Minutes | exercise minutes | No method cap |
| Sleep (iPhone) | minutes asleep, including naps | Maximum 540 points (9 hours) per day; see [Sleep competitions](/competitions/sleep) |
| Workout Count | 1 for a workout day; otherwise 0 | Maximum 1 point per day. A day counts with one workout longer than 15 minutes, or with more than 15 Exercise minutes |
| Distance (iPhone) | meters covered in recorded runs (or runs and walks) | No method cap; see [Distance competitions](/competitions/distance) |

**Example: Percentage of Goals.** With 50% Move, 100% Exercise, and 75% Steps progress, the day scores `50 + 100 + 75 = 225` points. Going to 150% of a ring goal still contributes 100, not 150.

**Example: Workout Count.** A day with a 12-minute workout and 20 Exercise minutes scores 1 point, because Exercise passed 15 minutes. A day with a 12-minute workout and 10 Exercise minutes scores 0. Two long workouts on one day still score 1.

**Example: Total Activity.** A day with 420 active calories, 35 exercise minutes, and 8,250 steps scores `420 + 35 + round(8,250 ÷ 100) = 538` points.

## Goal-based methods

### Personal Goal

The host selects Steps, Calories, or Exercise Minutes and sets an allowed range. Each participant must choose a personal goal in that range. The daily score is that participant’s progress toward the goal as a percentage, capped at 100 and rounded to two decimal places. A participant who has not selected a goal scores zero until they do, and the personal goal is locked once set.

**Example:** With a 10,000-step Personal Goal, 7,500 steps scores 75 points; 10,000 or 12,000 steps scores 100 points.

Hosting Personal Goal and Stick To It needs Membership or Pro. Anyone can join them.

### Stick To It

The host sets one daily Steps, Calories, or Exercise Minutes target for everyone. Reaching the target scores 1 point for that day; missing it scores 0. Meeting the number exactly counts.

**Example:** In a 7-day Stick To It competition with an 8,000-step daily target, reaching at least 8,000 steps on five days produces 5 points.

The host also answers **On how many days?** That number is the group's headline target. It doesn't change points: every day you hit the goal still scores 1.

## Head to Head

In the create flow, **Head to Head** is the shortcut for choosing one metric: steps, active calories, exercise minutes, or sleep. It uses the corresponding Step Count, Calorie Burn, Exercise Minutes, or Sleep formula above. Sleep needs Membership or Pro to host and is for friends only; see [Sleep competitions](/competitions/sleep). Highest total wins.

## Workout goal

A Workout Count host can turn on **Set a workout goal** and choose **How many workouts?** Everyone who reaches it finishes, and the leaderboard keeps running underneath. Points don't change. This option is free to host.

## Distance (iPhone)

Distance scores 1 point per meter covered in recorded runs, or runs and walks, with an optional target to qualify. It's free to host and can't be picked as a scoring change later. See [Distance competitions](/competitions/distance).

## Collective competitions

A Collective competition has no scoring method and no ranking. Your steps, calories, or exercise minutes (whichever the host chose) during the competition dates add to one group total, which counts toward the host's **Together, reach** number. See [Collective competitions](/competitions/personal-and-shared-goals#collective-competitions).

## Goals, caps, and fairness

Every device's data reaches MoveTogether the same way, through Apple Health or Health Connect. Most methods score the same Move, Exercise, and Steps numbers. Two read more than that:

- **Workout Count** also checks recorded workouts, from any device.
- **Distance** (iPhone) counts only the distance in recorded runs, or runs and walks. Everyday steps don't add distance. A workout saved by two apps counts once.

On iPhone, each person's chosen tracker supplies Steps and Exercise, so wearing more devices never adds to them. Move follows the Apple Watch on days it's worn, and otherwise uses the higher of the tracker's active calories and Apple Health's Move total. See [why your numbers don't match your tracker's app](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker). Steps and workouts typed in by hand never count.

Your everyday Move, Exercise, and Steps goals affect Ring Close Count and Percentage of Goals. They don't change raw-metric methods. Personal Goal uses the goal you lock for that competition, and Stick To It uses the host's shared daily target.

WHOOP Strain competitions are retired. You can't create a new one, but one that already exists keeps scoring until it ends. If it was set to repeat, the current round is its last.
