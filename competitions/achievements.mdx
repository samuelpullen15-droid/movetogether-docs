Badges are the medals you collect in MoveTogether. Every badge lives on one **Badges** screen, grouped by how you earn it. Seasonal event, referral, and monthly challenge badges are free to earn on every plan. Streak, achievement, and competition badges need Pro to earn. Anyone can view the screen.

## Open your badges

1. Open your profile. On iPhone, tap your photo at the top of **Home**.
2. Next to **Badges**, tap **See all**.

## Badge groups

The top of the screen shows seasonal event badges and how many you've earned. Below it, **Milestones** has one tile per group. Tap a tile to see every badge in it.

| Group | How you earn them | Plan to earn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seasonal events | Take part in a time-limited event. They're gone when the event ends. See [seasonal events](/engagement/seasonal-events). | Every plan |
| Streaks | Stay active 3 days a week for weeks in a row. See [workout streaks](/engagement/streaks-shields). | Pro |
| Achievements | Reach activity and consistency milestones. | Pro |
| Competition | Win competitions in particular ways (below). | Pro |
| Referrals | **Ringleader**: invite a friend who joins MoveTogether and logs 3 active days. See [referrals](/account/referrals). | Every plan |
| Challenges | Finish a month of daily challenges. See [monthly challenges](/engagement/monthly-challenges). | Every plan |

On the Free plan, the screen shows a note that Streaks, Achievements, and Competition are Pro.

### Competition badges

| Badge | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| First Blood | Win your very first competition |
| Underdog | Come from last place to win a competition |
| Photo Finish | Win a competition by less than 1% |

## Badge details

Tap any badge to see what it takes to earn it. Achievement badges show their Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum levels in the detail sheet. When you earn a badge, you can share it as an image.

## Feature a badge on your profile

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    Go to **Settings** > **Personal Details** > **Featured badge**. Pick **Automatic** or one of your earned badges.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    Open **Personal Details** from your profile, then **Featured badge**, and pick one of your earned badges.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The featured badge shows near the top of your public profile.

## Something not right?

- A streak, achievement, or competition badge didn't unlock: these need Pro. Activity before you started Pro doesn't count toward achievements.
- A seasonal badge is missing: check the event's requirement in [seasonal events](/engagement/seasonal-events).
- More help: [competition issues](/troubleshooting/competition-issues).
