MoveTogether uses **badges** for named accomplishments. They are not Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum medal tiers.

## Badge groups

- **Streak milestones:** weekly workout-streak milestones. See [workout streaks and shields](/engagement/streaks-shields).
- **Achievements:** cumulative activity and fitness milestones.
- **Competition:** wins, comebacks, close finishes, rivalries, and other competitive accomplishments.
- **Seasonal events:** time-limited event requirements.
- **Monthly challenges:** a monthly collection fed by completed daily and weekly challenges. See [monthly challenges](/engagement/monthly-challenges).
- **Referrals:** the Ringleader badge through the referral program.

Seasonal-event, referral, and monthly-challenge badges can be earned on any plan. Streak, achievement, and competition badges need Pro to earn.

## View and feature a badge

Tap your photo at the top right of **Home** to open **Profile**, then tap **See all** next to **Badges**. Each badge detail explains its requirement. To feature an earned badge, go to **Settings → Personal Details** and choose it there.

Some badges are granted as soon as the requirement is met. Referral Pro-time rewards are claimed separately from **Invite & Earn**.
