Sleep is one of the numbers you can race in **Head to Head**, next to Steps, Calories, and Exercise. Most time asleep wins. Hosting a Sleep competition needs a Membership or Pro. Joining is free. It works on iPhone and Android, and only sleep recorded by a wearable counts.

## How a day scores

Each day scores **1 point per minute asleep**, up to **9 hours (540 points)**. The leaderboard adds up every day in the competition and shows scores in hours and minutes, like "7h 32m".

- **Asleep only:** time awake during the night doesn't count.
- **Naps:** they count too, inside the same 9-hour daily cap.
- **Which day:** a night counts on the day you wake up. A night from 11 p.m. Monday to 7 a.m. Tuesday counts entirely for Tuesday. So the night before a competition's last day counts on that last day.
- **Two devices:** if more than one device records the same night, each minute counts once.

**Example:** you sleep 7h 32m overnight and nap for 45 minutes in the afternoon. That day scores 8h 17m (497 points). A 10-hour night scores 9h (540 points), the cap.

## Which devices count

Sleep must come from a wearable. Sleep your phone estimates on its own, sleep from a bedtime schedule, and sleep typed in by hand never count.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    MoveTogether reads sleep from Apple Health. It counts sleep from an Apple Watch, or from the Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Polar, Fitbit, or Ultrahuman app writing to Apple Health.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    MoveTogether reads sleep from Health Connect. It counts sleep recorded by a watch, ring, band, or chest strap, whichever app saved it, and sleep from the Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Polar, Fitbit, or Ultrahuman app.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Create a Sleep competition

1. Open **Compete**, tap **+** (**Add competition**), then **Create competition**, and pick **Compete**.
2. On the scoring step, choose **Head to Head**.
3. On **Pick the metric**, choose **Sleep**. On iPhone the question is **What are you racing on?** On Android it's **Competing on**.
4. Finish the steps and tap **Create competition**. Sleep competitions are always private.

On the Free plan, Sleep shows with a lock, and tapping it opens the upgrade screen. Coach Mo can also draft a Sleep competition for you if your plan can host one.

## Join a Sleep competition

Joining doesn't need a paid plan while the host has room for free guests. See [when joining needs a Membership](/competitions/how-it-works#when-joining-needs-a-membership). Before you join, the app tells you:

- "Players in this competition will see your sleep time." That's how the leaderboard works.
- If MoveTogether hasn't seen any sleep from you in the last 14 days, it warns that you'll score 0 unless your wearable starts sending sleep. You can still join, and start wearing your watch or ring to bed.

## Rules

- **Friends only:** a Sleep competition can't be public, always-on, or part of a seasonal event. Trying to make one public shows "Sleep competitions are for friends only. Make it private to use Sleep."
- **No vote to switch:** the host can't ask the group to change an existing competition to Sleep. See [changing scoring](/competitions/changing-scoring).
- **No Best days:** [Best days](/competitions/best-days) doesn't work with Sleep.

## Something not right?

- You score 0: check that your wearable records sleep and sends it to Apple Health or Health Connect. Sleep from your phone alone doesn't count.
- A night landed on the wrong day: a night counts on the day you woke up.
