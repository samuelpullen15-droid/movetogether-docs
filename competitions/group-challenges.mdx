A Group Challenge is a bigger competition for a company, gym, club, or event. It holds up to 100 people and can split them into as many as 20 teams (divisions). It can use any scoring method.

<Note>
  Hosting a Group Challenge needs Pro, on iPhone or Android. Membership isn't enough. Anyone you invite to a private one joins free.
</Note>

## Group Challenge, team competition, or Collective?

| | Group Challenge | [Team competition](/competitions/teams) | [Collective](/competitions/personal-and-shared-goals#collective-competitions) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plan to host | Pro | Free | Member or Pro |
| People | 10 to 100, you set the limit | No limit | No limit |
| Teams | Optional, up to 20 | 2 to 4 | None |
| Leaderboard | Yes | Yes | No, one shared total |

## Host a Group Challenge

1. Open **Compete**, tap **+** (**Add competition**), then **Host a group challenge**. If you're not on Pro, this opens the upgrade screen.
2. Name it, choose scoring, and pick dates as usual. A Group Challenge is always a race, so the app skips **What kind of competition?**
3. Under **Challenge size**, **Group challenge** is on. Set the most people who can join with the **people max** control: 10 to 100, one at a time. Hold **\+** or **−** to move faster. It starts at 50.
4. To split people into divisions, turn on **Team competition** (**Compete in groups instead of individually**). Then either:
   - set **people per team**, and the app works out how many teams you need. For example, 50 people in teams of 4 makes 13 teams. Or
   - leave **people per team** on **Any** and choose up to 20 teams yourself.
5. Under **TEAM ASSIGNMENT**, pick **Auto-assign** (each new player goes on the team with the fewest people) or **Manual** (you put each invited player on a team on the next step).
6. Review the summary and tap **Create competition**.

You can also turn on **Group challenge** in the **Challenge size** section while making any competition. Without Pro, it shows a **PRO** label.

### How team size works

Teams can be as small as the people max allows within 20 teams. When you tap **\+** from **Any**, the app starts at the smallest size that fits.

| People max | Smallest team size |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 40 | 2 |
| 41 to 60 | 3 |
| 61 to 80 | 4 |
| 81 to 100 | 5 |

Nobody can join a team that's already full.

## Invite people

Open the competition and tap **More options** (**⋯**). The invite row shows how full it is, such as **Invite people (12/50 joined)**. Tap **Share invite link** to send one link to everyone.

When the limit is reached, new people can't join, and the app tells them the competition is full.

## Join a Group Challenge

Open the invite link, or enter the code from **Compete** > **+** (**Add competition**) > **Enter invite code**. Joining a private Group Challenge is free on every plan.

## Standings

The competition screen starts with a **Your team** card: your team's rank (such as **#4 of 13**) and your teammates with their points.

Below it, **Team Standings** ranks every team by average points per person, so a team that's one person short isn't penalized. The list shows the top 5 and your team. Tap **Show all** for the rest. Tap any team to see who's on it. On the leaderboard, a dot in each person's team color shows their team.

Each person also has their own score on the leaderboard. See [team competitions](/competitions/teams), [the leaderboard](/competitions/leaderboard), and the [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference).

## Something not right?

- People can't join: check the invite row to see whether it's full. A free player may also need a Membership if the challenge is public. See [when joining needs a Membership](/competitions/how-it-works#when-joining-needs-a-membership).
- Someone is on the wrong team: the apps can't move people between teams after the competition is created.
- More help: [competition issues](/troubleshooting/competition-issues).
