A competition turns everyone's synced activity into a race or a shared goal. Anyone can host one or join one on iPhone or Android. Some formats need a Membership or Pro to host, and the table below says which.

## Pick the right kind in a minute

| You want to... | Pick | Plan to host |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Race friends on one leaderboard | **Compete**, any scoring method | Free for most methods |
| Race on steps, calories, exercise, or sleep only | **Compete** > **Head to Head** | Free (Sleep needs Member or Pro) |
| Reward showing up, not big numbers | **Compete** > **Stick To It** or **Workout Count** | Stick To It: Member or Pro. Workout Count: Free |
| Make it fair for very different fitness levels | **Compete** > **Personal Goal** or **Percentage of Goals** | Personal Goal: Member or Pro. Percentage: Free |
| Race on runs or walks | **Compete** > **Distance** | Free |
| Stop one sick day from deciding it | Turn on **Best days** | Member or Pro |
| Race as teams of friends (2 to 4 teams) | **Compete** with **Team competition** on | Free |
| Run something for a company, gym, or club (up to 100 people, 20 teams) | **Host a group challenge** | Pro |
| Chase one big number together, no ranking | **Collective** | Member or Pro |

Not sure which scoring method? **Ring Close Count** is the default and suits most groups. The [scoring reference](/competitions/scoring-reference) has the math and an example for every method.

## Host a competition

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Open **Compete**, tap **+** (**Add competition**), then **Create competition**. You can also tap **New competition** in the quick actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Compete or Collective">
    On **What kind of competition?**, pick **Compete** ("Race each other. Everyone ranks on a leaderboard.") or **Collective** ("Move as one group. Everyone's activity adds up toward a shared goal.").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how points are scored">
    On **How are points scored?**, tap a method. A lock or **MEMBER** label means your plan can't host it yet. Tapping it shows the upgrade screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick dates">
    Choose a preset or **Custom**. See [Pick dates](#pick-dates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set privacy, teams, and extras">
    Choose public or private. Turn on **Team competition**, **Best days**, or a finish line if you want them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite and create">
    Invite friends, review the summary, and tap **Create competition**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Who can host what

| Plan | What you can host |
| --- | --- |
| Free (accounts from before 2.1.1, and friends playing free) | Up to 2 competitions at a time. Ring Close Count, Head to Head on Steps, Calories, or Exercise, Workout Count, Percentage of Goals, Total Activity, Distance, and team competitions. |
| Member | As many as you like. Adds Collective, Personal Goal, Stick To It, Sleep, and Best days. |
| Pro | Everything in Member, plus Group Challenges. |

On the Free plan, every competition you host that is a draft, upcoming, or active uses one of your 2 spots. Competitions you join never count. Seasonal events don't count. When a competition finishes or you delete it, the spot frees up. If you're at the limit, the app tells you you're at your hosting limit and offers a Membership. See [plans](/subscriptions/plans) for prices.

## Join a competition

- **From an invite code:** open **Compete**, tap **+** (**Add competition**), then **Enter invite code**. You can also tap **Join with code** in the quick actions menu.
- **From a link a friend sent:** open it on your phone. The app opens the competition and adds you.
- **From an in-app invitation:** open the invite and tap **Join Competition** (iPhone) or **Accept** (Android).
- **A public competition:** tap **+** (**Add competition**), then **Discover public competitions**.

### When joining needs a Membership

Members, Pro, and Free accounts created before version 2.1.1 can join any competition.

New accounts choose Member or Pro when they sign up. The exception is a friend you invite: when a Member or Pro host invites someone new to a private competition, that friend can play on the Free plan at no cost (the host pays). That friend:

- **Can join** private competitions hosted by a Member or Pro friend, while the host has room. A Pro host can bring as many free guests as they like. A Member host can bring 3 free guests in total, across all their private competitions.
- **Needs a Membership** to join a public competition, or one hosted by someone on the Free plan.
- **Can always join** seasonal events.

When joining needs a Membership, the app says so. On iPhone it explains why, for example when the host has no free guest spots left. On Android, a **Become a Member** message appears.

### Why a join can fail

- **The code is wrong.** Check it with the host, or ask them to send the invite link instead.
- **The competition has finished.** Finished competitions don't take new people.
- **It's full.** Only Group Challenges have a people limit.
- **It needs a Membership.** See [When joining needs a Membership](#when-joining-needs-a-membership).
- **It's private.** A private competition can only be joined with its code, its link, or an invitation.

## Pick dates

The dates step offers **This weekend**, **This week** (Monday to Sunday), **Next 7 days**, **Next 28 days**, and **Custom**. A competition can be as short as one day. There's no maximum length.

## Repeat a competition

When you create a Compete competition, set **Repeat** to **None**, **Weekly**, **2 Weeks**, or **Monthly**. Each round keeps the same length and has its own dates and final results. Collective competitions don't repeat.

A round can't be longer than the gap between rounds. A 10-day competition can't repeat weekly, and the app says "Each round is 10 days, so a new one can't start sooner than that."

If the host changes **Repeat** while a round is running, the change starts with the next round. Setting it to **None** lets this round finish, then the competition ends. See each finished round on the competition screen, in [past rounds](/competitions/recaps#past-rounds-of-a-repeating-competition).

## Edit a competition

Only the host can edit, and only before the competition is completed.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    1. Open the competition.
    2. Tap **More options** (the **⋯** button).
    3. Tap **Edit competition**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    1. Open the competition.
    2. Tap **Competition info** (the **ⓘ** button).
    3. Tap **Edit competition**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

| Setting | Before it starts | While it's active |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Editable | Editable |
| End date | Editable | Editable |
| Public or private | Editable | Editable |
| Cover photo (iPhone only) | Editable | Editable |
| Repeat | Editable | Editable, from the next round |
| Start date | Editable | Locked |
| Scoring method (iPhone only) | Editable | Locked. The host can [ask the group to change it](/competitions/changing-scoring). |
| Collective **Together, reach** number | Editable | Locked |
| What a Collective adds up | Locked | Locked |
| Teams and Best days | Set when created | Set when created |

A Sleep competition always stays private. The Android app can't change the cover photo or the scoring method. Use an iPhone for those, or ask the group to change scoring once it's active.

## Leave or delete a competition

On iPhone, tap **More options** (**⋯**), then **Leave competition**. Leaving removes your points and place in that competition, and it can't be undone. The host can't leave. The host deletes the competition instead: **Delete competition** is in the same menu on iPhone and at the bottom of **Edit competition** on Android.

## From start to final results

| Stage | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Upcoming | People can join. Nothing is scored yet. |
| Active | Synced activity is scored, and the leaderboard updates as data arrives. |
| Finalizing | The last day is over. The competition says it's tallying results and shows how many people have synced. Late workouts still count. |
| Completed | Final standings are set. Winners are announced and the [recap](/competitions/recaps) is ready. |

A competition starts when the first person in it reaches the start date in their time zone. It ends after the last person in it reaches midnight after the end date. Results are then held for up to about 12 hours so late syncs still count, and they land sooner once everyone has synced. Final standings usually land by the next morning.

Scores are calculated on MoveTogether's servers from what your phone syncs. A refresh can't add activity that hasn't reached MoveTogether yet. Steps and workouts typed in by hand never count.

### Joining after the start

When you join a competition that has already started, MoveTogether scores the days you missed from your synced history, back to the start date. It never counts days before you created your account. This can take a few minutes after you join.

Seasonal events work differently. Unless you have Pro, days before you joined a seasonal event don't count.

## Something not right?

- A score looks low or late: open the app so it syncs, then check [sync issues](/troubleshooting/sync-issues).
- You can't find a competition or can't join: see [competition issues](/troubleshooting/competition-issues).
- Your numbers differ from your tracker's app: see [why numbers differ from your tracker](/troubleshooting/why-numbers-differ-from-your-tracker).
