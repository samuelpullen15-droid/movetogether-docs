Every Compete competition has a leaderboard that ranks everyone by points. This page explains what you see on it, on iPhone and Android. For the weekly league ladder and the Friends and Global boards, see [Move Leagues](/competitions/leagues).

## Reading the leaderboard

Each row shows a person's place, photo, name, and points. The board updates as each person's activity syncs.

- **Rank arrows.** An up or down arrow with a number shows how many places someone has moved since the last daily update. Arrows show while a competition is active.
- **Today's rings (iPhone).** Each row shows small Move, Exercise, and Steps rings for today. Use the **All time** / **Today** menu above the list to rank by today's points instead.
- **Distance competitions** can show miles or kilometers instead of points. See [Distance competitions](/competitions/distance).
- **Sleep competitions** show hours and minutes, such as "7h 32m".
- **Refresh.** On iPhone, pull down on the competition to refresh it.

## Ties and winners

People with the same points share a rank. A tie at the top means everyone tied for first is a winner. Each of them gets the win notification, and the win counts on each of their profiles. If nobody has scored yet, nobody is in the lead.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Version 2.2.0">
    On the leaderboard, tied people show the same place number, though they're still listed one above the other. Before anyone scores, places show a dash. The rival card says when you're tied, for example **Tied for #1**. When a competition ends in a tie for first, each winner sees "You tied for first!" and the results list every winner.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Version 2.1.3">
    On the leaderboard itself, tied people are still listed one above the other, and the row numbers can differ (for example, 2 and 3) even though the scores are equal. The rival card says when you're tied, for example **Tied for #1**.

## Daily Breakdown (iPhone)

<Note>New in version 2.2.0. Update the app if you don't see this yet.</Note>

Each day card in **Daily Breakdown** shows the points saved for that day, the same number the leaderboard uses. If Apple Health has more of today's activity than MoveTogether has scored, today's card shows **Syncing…** under its points while the app sends it, then updates. Sleep, distance, and strain competitions don't show **Syncing…**. Tap a day to see how its points add up. In a paced seasonal Competition, that sheet reads **Scores are % of your own daily target. Each day counts up to 150%.**

## You versus the people around you

Above the leaderboard, a rival card shows the person just ahead of you, you, and the person just behind, with the gap between each. For example: **You're #3 of 8**, **120 pts to catch Sam**, and **Alex is 45 pts behind you.** When you're level with someone, it says **Level with Sam**.

The card is hidden when you're not on the leaderboard or you're the only one on it.

## Top Performers podium

With 3 or more people, **Top Performers** shows the top 3 on a podium.

## Choose which cards you see

You can hide the rival card or the podium on your own screen. It only changes what you see, and it applies to every competition.

1. On a competition with at least 2 people, tap **Customize view** (the sliders button above the cards).
2. Turn **You vs. the people around you** or **Top Performers podium** on or off.

You can also change these in **Settings** > **Customization** > **Competition screen**.

## Your streak

Below the leaderboard, a streak card shows your current day streak, such as **5 day streak**, and your best if it's higher. It's the same streak you see on Home and your profile, not a streak for this competition. See [workout streaks](/engagement/streaks-shields).

## Past You (Pro)

In a repeating competition, **Past You** adds a row that races your best past round. Pro shows how far ahead or behind your best you are. Without Pro, you see "Race your best week. Unlock with Pro." It shows only on the current round.

## Activity feed

The competition's **Activity** section posts moments as they happen: people joining, workouts, all rings closed, ring streaks, goals reached, lead changes, and close races. You can react to posts and send taunts.

## Head-to-head records

A friend's profile shows a **Head to Head** card with your wins, ties, and losses against them. Only private competitions count toward it. Public competitions, seasonal events, and Collective competitions don't. The card is hidden if you've never finished a competition together.

## Ask Siri your rank (iPhone)

Say "What's my rank in Step Showdown in MoveTogether" (use your competition's name). Siri answers with your place, such as "You're 2nd of 5 in Step Showdown." This also works from the Shortcuts app.

## Privacy on the leaderboard

Your privacy settings control whether leaderboards show your real name, whether you appear on public leaderboards, and whether competitors can see detailed stats. See [privacy settings](/account/privacy-settings).

## Something not right?

- Someone's score looks stuck: their phone may not have synced. Scores update when it does.
- Tied scores with different numbers: that's expected in version 2.1.3. Version 2.2.0 gives tied people the same number. See [Ties and winners](#ties-and-winners).
