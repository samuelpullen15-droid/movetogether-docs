The **Leaderboard** screen at the top of **Compete** has three views: **League** (your weekly cohort of up to 30 people at your tier), **Friends** (a daily ranking of your friends), and **Global** (the top 100 worldwide for the week). This page covers the leaderboard inside a competition. For the weekly ladder, see [Move Leagues](/competitions/leagues).

## Reading the leaderboard

Each competition ranks its participants by current score, showing rank, photo, name, score, and how far ahead or behind you are.

## Past You ghost

The **Past You** ghost is a Pro feature. It compares your current performance with your best past week for the same scoring type and cadence. Without Pro you may see a locked preview.

## Real-time updates

Scores update as each person's activity syncs from Apple Health or Health Connect. Pull down to refresh.

## Competition events

The leaderboard shows live events such as ring closures, overtakes, taunts, and daily challenge completions.

## Privacy on the leaderboard

Your privacy settings control whether leaderboards show your real name, whether you appear on public leaderboards, and whether competitors can see detailed stats. Adjust these in **Settings → Privacy Settings**.
