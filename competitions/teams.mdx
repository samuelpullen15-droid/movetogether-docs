A team competition splits everyone into 2 to 4 teams that race each other. Each person still scores on their own, and teams rank by their average. Hosting a team competition is free on every plan, on iPhone and Android. For more than 4 teams or up to 100 people, use a [Group Challenge](/competitions/group-challenges) (Pro).

## Set up teams

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Version 2.2.0">
    1. Start a new competition: **Compete** > **+** (**Add competition**) > **Create competition**. Set **Type** to **Compete**.
    2. Set **Scoring** and **When** as usual.
    3. Under **Fine-tune**, tap **Teams**, then turn on **Team competition** (**Compete in groups instead of individually**).
    4. Pick how many teams: 2, 3, or 4.
    5. Name each team and pick its color.
    6. Under **TEAM ASSIGNMENT**, pick one:
       - **Fair split:** MoveTogether makes even teams at kickoff from everyone's recent activity. See [Fair split](#fair-split-teams-picked-for-you).
       - **Auto-assign:** each person who joins goes on the team with the fewest people. If teams are tied, they go on the lower-numbered team.
       - **Manual:** you put each invited player on a team on the **Who** page.
    7. Under **YOUR TEAM**, pick your own side. Fair split doesn't ask, because it places you too.
    8. Tap **Done**, invite people with **Who**, and tap **Create competition**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Version 2.1.3">
    1. Start a new competition: **Compete** > **+** (**Add competition**) > **Create competition**, then pick **Compete**.
    2. Choose scoring and dates as usual.
    3. On **Public or private?**, under **Teams**, turn on **Team competition** (**Compete in groups instead of individually**).
    4. Pick how many teams: 2, 3, or 4.
    5. On **Set up teams**, name each team and pick its color.
    6. Under **TEAM ASSIGNMENT**, pick one:
       - **Auto-assign:** each person who joins goes on the team with the fewest people. If teams are tied, they go on the lower-numbered team.
       - **Manual:** you put each invited player on a team on the next step.
    7. Under **YOUR TEAM**, pick your own side.
    8. Invite people and tap **Create competition**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  With **Manual**, anyone who joins without your invite, for example through a shared link, waits without a team. The apps can't change teams after the competition is created, so invite everyone you want to place before you create it, or use **Auto-assign**.
</Note>

## Fair split: teams picked for you

<Note>New in version 2.2.0. Update the app if you don't see this yet.</Note>

With **Fair split**, nobody picks teams. Right before the competition starts, MoveTogether splits everyone into even teams based on their last 2 weeks of activity, the host included.

1. When you set up teams, under **TEAM ASSIGNMENT**, pick **Fair split** ("Teams form at kickoff, balanced by everyone's recent activity, yours included.").
2. Invite people. Until the start, the competition shows **Teams form at kickoff** and how many people are in so far.
3. As host, you can lock teams early: tap **Lock teams now**, then **Lock teams**. Teams are final once they lock.

When teams lock, the competition shows **Teams are set**, each team's members, and **Team balance**: **Evenly matched**, **Close match**, or **A bit uneven**. More dots next to a name means more active lately. **Rookie** means someone without enough recent activity yet, and they count as middle of the pack.

People who join after teams lock go on the team with room that's furthest behind on paper. Nobody else moves. A Fair split competition always starts as upcoming, even if its start date is today, so teams can form first.

## Rules

- Teams are set when the competition is created. The host can't add, remove, or rename teams later.
- A team competition can't be a Collective competition, and it can't use [Best days](/competitions/best-days).
- When two teams have the same average, the app lists one above the other, but their scores are level. In version 2.2.0, tied teams also show the same place number.

## Read the team standings

**Your team card.** At the top of the competition screen, **YOUR TEAM** shows your team's name, its rank (such as **#2 of 4**), and your teammates with their points. Long lists show 5 people and a **Show all** button.

**Team Standings.** Every team, ranked by **avg per person**: the team's total points divided by its number of people. That keeps it fair when one team has fewer people.

**Team colors.** On the leaderboard, a dot in each person's team color shows which team they're on.

**See a team.** Tap any team in **Team Standings** to see its members, their points, and their place on the overall leaderboard.

**Example:** Team Red has 3 people with 30, 24, and 18 points (72 total). Team Blue has 4 people with 25, 20, 15, and 12 points (72 total). Red's average is 24 and Blue's is 18, so Red leads, even though both totals are 72.

## Something not right?

- You're not on a team: the competition probably uses **Manual** assignment and you joined through a link. Ask the host.
- A team's total looks small: rankings use the average per person, not the total.
- More help: [competition issues](/troubleshooting/competition-issues).
