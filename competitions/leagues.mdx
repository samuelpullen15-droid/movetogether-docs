Move Leagues put you in a weekly group (a cohort) of up to 30 people at the same tier. Finish near the top to move up a tier, near the bottom to move down. Leagues are free on every plan, on iPhone and Android, and you're placed automatically once you start moving.

## Open your league

Open **Compete** and tap the league card at the top, or tap **Leaderboard**. The leaderboard opens on **League**, titled with your tier, such as **Runner League**. The other views are **Friends** and **Global**.

Your league shows your rank, the time left in the week, and which zone you're in.

## The tiers

You start at Walker. Each week, the top of the cohort moves up a tier and the bottom moves down one.

| # | Tier | Promote at a full cohort | Demote at a full cohort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Walker | Top 15 | None |
| 2 | Jogger | Top 12 | Bottom 5 |
| 3 | Runner | Top 10 | Bottom 5 |
| 4 | Strider | Top 9 | Bottom 5 |
| 5 | Athlete | Top 8 | Bottom 5 |
| 6 | Sprinter | Top 7 | Bottom 5 |
| 7 | Champion | Top 6 | Bottom 5 |
| 8 | Elite | Top 5 | Bottom 5 |
| 9 | Master | Top 3 | Bottom 5 |
| 10 | Legend | None | Bottom 5 |

### Smaller cohorts

- No more than half the cohort can move up. A 10-person Jogger cohort promotes its top 5, not 12.
- At least one person always stays in the middle. For example, an 8-person Jogger cohort promotes 4 and demotes 3.
- With fewer than 8 people, it's a quiet week: nobody moves up or down. The league shows **No promotions or demotions this week**.

## The zones

| Zone | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Promotion zone** | You'd move up a tier if the week ended now. |
| **Safe zone** | You'd stay at your tier. |
| **Demotion zone** | You'd move down a tier, unless your shield saves you. The league tells you how many spots you need to climb to be safe. |

Zones are live. They move as everyone's activity syncs.

## How points are counted

```text
league points = active calories + exercise minutes + round(week's steps ÷ 100)
```

Calories and minutes are added up from Monday to Sunday. Steps are added up for the whole week, then divided by 100 and rounded once. Your daily ring goals don't affect league points, so a low Move goal never helps or hurts you.

**Example:** a week with 2,940 active calories, 245 exercise minutes, and 57,750 steps scores `2,940 + 245 + round(577.5) = 2,940 + 245 + 578 = 3,763` league points.

When two people finish with the same points, the order between them is fixed by account, not by name, so changing your display name can't change your result.

## When the week ends

Your league week runs Monday at midnight through Sunday, in the time zone saved on your profile when you joined the cohort.

After the week ends, the league shows **Results pending** while everyone in the cohort finishes their week and syncs. Results land by about 6 a.m. Eastern Time on Monday, and sooner when everyone's week has already ended. Then MoveTogether records your result and puts you in a new cohort at your new tier. Your new week's points count from Monday while that happens.

## Demotion shield

Everyone starts with one demotion shield. If you finish in the demotion zone while you have it, it's used automatically and you stay at your tier. Once it's used, it's gone. The league shows **Protected** while you still have it.

## League chat

Once your cohort has 8 or more people, a chat button appears on the League view. Everyone in your cohort is in the chat. You get a notification only when someone @mentions you.

## Friends and Global

- **Friends** ranks you and your friends on today's activity, using the same formula as league points. It resets at your local midnight.
- **Global** shows the top 100 movers worldwide for this week, Monday through today in your time zone.

## Something not right?

- You're not in a league yet: move today and you'll be placed. The league card says **Not in a league yet** until then.
- Your rank didn't change after a workout: wait for it to sync. See [sync issues](/troubleshooting/sync-issues).
- Results are taking a while on Monday: they land by about 6 a.m. Eastern Time.
- More help: [competition issues](/troubleshooting/competition-issues).

For a race with friends using a different scoring method, [create a competition](/competitions/how-it-works).
